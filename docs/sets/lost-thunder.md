# Lost Thunder (2018)

Set data for Lost Thunder. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: November 2, 2018.
- Abbreviation: LOT. English set number: 78.
- Card count: 214 main set + 22 secret rares = 236.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a Prism Star card (Elite Fourum post
  140).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); Prism Star
  slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or Prism Star) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no (rare slot
  card 7, Prism Star card 6). Pack-trick order: yes (rare slot last,
  Prism Star second to last).
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a Prism Star is a second hit in the reverse slot.** This
  set has the highest Prism Star rate of the era.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Prism
Star, Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star (reverse slot) | 1 in 8.2 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 10 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 23.3 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 71.0 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 102.6 | Empirical study |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 3.6 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.1 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.3 full art Trainers and secret rares. Each of
the 22 secret rares prints 3 times (66 of 100 slots). The observed
secret rare share was 65.2% (@burpies), 63.2% (Derium), and 56.1% (the
author's own sample).

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Entei and Raikou (theme deck exclusive holos) are short printed.
- Uncommons come from two stacks in turn. The two runs follow the pack
  art pairs (Lugia with Blacephalon, Zeraora with Celebi) inside a box.
  Confidence: empirical study (Elite Fourum post 155).
