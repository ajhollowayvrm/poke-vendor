# White Flare (2025)

Set-specific data for White Flare. This is one file per set. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach and the index.
The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md).

White Flare is one half of a split special expansion. The other half is
[Black Bolt](black-bolt.md). The two sets share release date, pack
template, and the only pull-rate study. **Read
[black-bolt.md](black-bolt.md) for the full pack order analysis and
the odds table.** This file records the White Flare differences and
repeats the data the rip screen needs.

## Pack structure

Official product text: each pack holds "10 cards and 1 Basic Energy"
(pokemon.com, Elite Trainer Box). The slot contents are **unknown** for
this set. The expected layout copies the Prismatic Evolutions slot
report:

| Slot | Count | Expected contents |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Regular reverse holo or Poké Ball pattern |
| Reverse holo 2 | 1 | Regular reverse holo, Master Ball pattern, Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, or Black White Rare |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Double Rare, or Ultra Rare |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | 1 | Pokémon TCG Live code (not confirmed for this set) |

## Pack order

Confidence: **unknown** for this set. The layout is the same as
[black-bolt.md](black-bolt.md#pack-order).

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 4 Commons |
| 5–7 | 3 Uncommons |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 (Poké Ball pattern if the pack has one) |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2 (Master Ball, Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, or Black White Rare if the pack has one) |
| 10 | Rare slot (Double Rare or Ultra Rare if the pack has one) |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

Only the rare-slot hit (position 10) is one of the last three cards.
Position 9 is the fourth-last card. Position 8 is the fifth-last card.
Sources disagree on whether the code card is first or last. TheGamer's
account gives a resolution (one source, Community estimate): see
[eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md#conflicts-in-the-template).

## Rarities and hit odds

All studies combine Black Bolt and White Flare. No source measures
White Flare alone. Use the same odds as Black Bolt.

| Rarity | Rate | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Poké Ball pattern | 30.56% (about 1 in 3) | Empirical study (TCGplayer, 700+ packs, combined sets) |
| Double Rare | 21.11% (about 1 in 5) | Empirical study (TCGplayer, 700+ packs, combined sets) |
| Illustration Rare | 16.39% (about 1 in 6) | Empirical study (TCGplayer, 700+ packs, combined sets) |
| Ultra Rare | 5.83% (about 1 in 17) | Empirical study (TCGplayer, 700+ packs, combined sets) |
| Master Ball pattern | 5.14% (about 1 in 19); PokeBeach gives 1 in 15 | Empirical study; conflict |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1.25% (about 1 in 80); PokeBeach gives 1 in 76 | Empirical study |

The full table with error margins and every source is in
[black-bolt.md](black-bolt.md#rarities-and-hit-odds).

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Poké Ball and Master Ball pattern reverse holos.**
- **Black White Rare:** 2 cards in White Flare (Cardrake): Reshiram ex
  and the Victini promo (card 172). Wargamer confirms "the Reshirams
  can be found in White Flare", against Zekrom ex in Black Bolt. A
  PokeBeach search extract places the Victini BWR promo as card 172 in
  White Flare (card 171 in Black Bolt).
- **No booster box** (PokeBeach extract).
- **God packs:** no source found. Confidence: unknown.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure above, copied from the Prismatic Evolutions slot report (see black-bolt.md). The slot contents are Expected, not confirmed. Odds come from the TCGplayer study, which combines Black Bolt and White Flare.
