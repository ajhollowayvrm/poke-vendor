# Sun & Moon (2017)

Set data for Sun & Moon, the first set of the Sun & Moon Series. The era
template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: February 3, 2017.
- Abbreviation: SUM. English set number: 71.
- Card count: 149 main set + 14 secret rares = 163.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card.

- 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic
  Energy, 1 code card.
- Sun & Moon was the first English set to add a Basic Energy to every
  pack (Bulbapedia; PokeBeach).
- Confidence: well documented. Bulbapedia, PokeBeach, and ThePriceDex
  agree.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order (move the Energy and 3 uncommons to
  the front, remove the code card): yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  "Sun & Moon through Sword & Shield" group.
- Exceptions: none known. This set has no Prism Star cards, so the
  reverse slot has only reverse holos.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra
(full art Pokémon-GX and full art Trainers), Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret
(gold items and Energy).

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 9 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 23.1 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 75 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 100 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 7.9 | Estimate ("Reverse card rates are estimated") |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 7.4 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 5.6 holos, 4.0 "regular"
Pokémon-GX, 1.0 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.4 full art Trainers and secret
rares. The model prints 10 secret rares 4 times and 4 secret rares 5
times on the shared sheet. That gives 0.84 secret rares per box. The 10
rarest secret rares are 1 in 642.9 packs each. Confidence: empirical
study; the Derium data for this set comes from two large opening videos.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- First set with Pokémon-GX (Bulbapedia).
- New reverse holo style (PokeBeach).
- The study finds no clear short print of the theme deck exclusive holos
  (Decidueye, Incineroar, Primarina) in the rare slot.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure and the ThePriceDex
odds above. The card list gives Basic Energy cards the Common rarity,
so the Common slot and the Basic Energy slot split by category, not by
a separate TCGdex rarity. TCGdex records Rare Holo GX, Ultra Rare (full
art), and Rainbow Rare as one rarity, Ultra Rare, and the true Secret
Rare shares its rarity with Rainbow Rare. The card numbers tell the four
outcomes apart: Rare Holo GX is the first appearance of each GX card,
Ultra Rare is the full art repeat, Rainbow Rare is the second repeat,
and Secret Rare is the trailing Trainer and Energy cards.

  under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare and Secret Rare under
  one rarity, Secret Rare. The slot map splits each pair by card number:
  the first appearance of a GX card is Rare Holo GX, its full art repeat
  is Ultra Rare, its second repeat is Rainbow Rare, and the trailing
  Trainer and Energy cards are Secret Rare. No source confirms this
  split by name; it follows the repeating pattern in the card numbers.
