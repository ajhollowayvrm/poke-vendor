# Primal Clash (2015)

Set-specific data for XY—Primal Clash. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: February 4, 2015 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: PRC. Bulbapedia also numbers it XY5.
- Card count: 160 cards plus 4 secret rares (164 total).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons (ThePriceDex).

**Conflict.** Pack Fresh lists "1 Rare, 3 Uncommons, 6 Commons, and 1
Reverse Holo", plus a code card. That is 11 cards, not 10. This file
uses 5 commons, as Bulbapedia and ThePriceDex do.

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo EX, Rare Ultra
(Bulbapedia), and Secret Rare (Bulbapedia expansion list, ThePriceDex).

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Per box (36) | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 24 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | 5.6 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 9 | 4 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 17.0 | 2.1 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 125 | 0.3 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | about 12 | Derived from the rows above |

All per-pack figures come from ThePriceDex. Roaring Skies has the same
rare-slot figures on ThePriceDex. This suggests template values (see
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#thepricedex-data-quality)).

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 7.3 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 5.3 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 2.6 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 3.4 packs".

Flipside Gaming gives a secret rare rate of 1:72 for the whole era
(**community estimate**).

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Ancient Traits start in this set (Bulbapedia).
- Bulbapedia: starting with this set, M Pokémon-EX were "printed as
  both Regular card and Full Art card".

## Open topics

- The common count conflicts: 5 (Bulbapedia, ThePriceDex) against 6
  (Pack Fresh).
- No source shows a real opened Primal Clash pack. The pack order is
  an era-wide community claim.
- The ThePriceDex figures match Roaring Skies exactly. They may not be
  set-specific.
- The contents of the theme decks, blisters, Collector Chest, and
  Launch Kit were not checked.
- Boxes per case: 6 (see [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sealed-products)).
  Collation is Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** Secret Rare uses 1 in 125 from ThePriceDex. Flipside Gaming gives 1 in 72 for the era.
