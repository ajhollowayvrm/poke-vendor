# Generations (2016)

Set-specific data for Generations, a special set for the 20th
anniversary. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file
covers. See [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.
This set **breaks the template**.

## Release

- English release: February 22, 2016 (Bulbapedia, Pokémon.com,
  Limitless TCG). Japanese release: February 27, 2016.
- Code: GEN.
- Card count: 83 main-set cards plus the 32-card Radiant Collection
  (RC1 to RC32). Total 115. No secret rares are listed.

## Pack structure

- **10 cards per pack.** Pokémon.com: "Pokémon TCG: Generations
  10-card booster packs inside special collections throughout the
  year". Confidence: **official**.
- **8 main-set cards and 2 Radiant Collection cards.** Source:
  Bulbapedia. Confidence: **community wiki**. The ThePriceDex figures
  agree: about 2 Radiant Collection cards and about 8 main-set cards
  per pack.
- Main-set part (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): about 3.1
  commons, 2 uncommons, Energy "1 in 1.1 packs", 1 rare slot, and 1
  reverse holo.
- Radiant Collection part (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): 1 RC
  common per pack, plus 1 RC slot with an RC uncommon, RC EX, or RC
  Ultra Rare.
- Whether each pack has a code card is Unknown.

## Pack order

**Unknown.** No source describes the card order in a Generations pack.
No source says where the two Radiant Collection cards sit. The XY-era
method applies to template sets. Do not apply it here without a
source.

Until a source is found, the rip screen has no real order for this
set. The game default (hit in the last three cards) is a placeholder
only.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare Holo, Rare Holo EX.
ThePriceDex also lists Rare and Ultra Rare in the main set, and RC
tiers.

Main-set rare slot (ThePriceDex):

| Rarity | Per pack | Per specific card | Confidence |
|--------|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 1 in 16.5 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 9.5 | 1 in 47.4 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 4.9 | 1 in 58.4 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 45 | 1 in 90 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | — | Derived from the rows above |

Radiant Collection slot (ThePriceDex):

| Rarity | Per pack | Per specific card | Confidence |
|--------|----------|-------------------|------------|
| RC Common | 1 per pack | 1 in 13 | Community estimate |
| RC Uncommon | 1 in 1.4 | 1 in 16.7 | Community estimate |
| RC Rare Holo EX | 1 in 12.5 | 1 in 25 | Community estimate |
| RC Ultra Rare | 1 in 5 | 1 in 25 | Community estimate |

ThePriceDex lists 5 RC Ultra Rare cards and 2 RC Rare Holo EX cards.
Reverse holo Rare Holo: "1 in 12.9 packs" (ThePriceDex).

A Radiant Collection card of RC Uncommon or higher counts as a hit
under the game's hit rule. So nearly every pack has two hit
candidates: the main rare slot and the RC slot.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Radiant Collection: Pokémon.com: "The second Radiant Collection, a
  32-card subset with unique art". Bulbapedia: the cards are
  "separately numbered with the prefix RC".
- Pokémon.com: "16 Pokémon-EX, including 4 Mega Evolution Pokémon".
- Bulbapedia: Generations booster packs "were only made available in
  special 20th Anniversary-related items". No booster box exists.

## Open topics

- The pack order is Unknown, including where the two Radiant
  Collection cards sit. This is the biggest gap for the rip screen.
- Bulbapedia lists no non-holo Rare or Ultra Rare, but ThePriceDex
  does. Confirm the rarity list.
- The main-set "8 cards" includes the reverse holo in the ThePriceDex
  figures. No source states this directly.
- The English release date of the Generations ETB is Unknown. The
  PokéBeach articles returned HTTP 403.
- Whether each pack has a code card is Unknown.
- Other Generations products (for example, other 20th anniversary
  boxes) may exist. Bulbapedia names only the products above.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Rare Holo EX (1 in 4.9) comes more often than Rare Holo (1 in 9.5). The rarity list therefore puts Rare Holo EX above Rare Holo.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Radiant Collection — Ultra Rare (1 in 5) comes more often than Radiant Collection — Rare Holo EX (1 in 12.5). The rarity list therefore puts Radiant Collection — Ultra Rare above Radiant Collection — Rare Holo EX.
