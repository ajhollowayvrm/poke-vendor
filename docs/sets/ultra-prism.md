# Ultra Prism (2018)

Set data for Ultra Prism, the first set with Prism Star cards. The era
template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: February 2, 2018.
- Abbreviation: UPR. English set number: 75.
- Card count: 156 main set + 17 secret rares = 173.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a Prism Star card instead of a reverse
  holo (Elite Fourum post 146).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); Prism Star
  slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or Prism Star) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no. The rare
  slot is card 7, and a Prism Star is card 6. Pack-trick order: yes.
  The rare slot is last, and a Prism Star is second to last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a Prism Star is a second hit in the reverse slot.**

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Prism
Star, Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret (includes gold Solgaleo-GX
and Lunala-GX).

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star (reverse slot) | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 22.7 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 83.3 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 93.8 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 10.5 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 5.9 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 3.0 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
0.8 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.6 full art Trainers and secret rares, 3.0
Prism Stars. Each secret rare prints 3 times on the shared sheet, and
full art Trainers fill the other 49 slots.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Prism Star cards: one copy per deck; they go to the Lost Zone when
  discarded (Bulbapedia).
- Gold Pokémon-GX (Solgaleo-GX and Lunala-GX) share the full art Trainer
  and secret rare sheet (Elite Fourum post 140).
- The set needs two 11x11 reverse sheets. This is why the study uses a
  360-pack sequence.
- Empoleon and Garchomp (theme deck exclusive holos) do not appear
  short printed.
