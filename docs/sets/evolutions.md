# Evolutions (2016)

Set-specific data for XY—Evolutions, the last set of the XY era. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: November 2, 2016 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
  Japanese release: September 16, 2016.
- Code: EVO.
- Card count: 108 cards plus 5 secret rares (109 to 113). Total 113.

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template for its size. Confidence:
**community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card (ThePriceDex).
- ThePriceDex averages: 4.6 commons, 2.9 uncommons, Energy "1 in 2.7
  packs", 1 rare slot, and 1 reverse holo.
- These averages do not match the template exactly. See the conflict
  under [Rarities and hit odds](#rarities-and-hit-odds).

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**. NinePocket
applies the method to "XY era sets (2014–2016)", which includes this
set by date.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
EX, Rare BREAK, Rare Secret. ThePriceDex also lists Ultra Rare.

| Rarity | Per pack | Per box (36) | Per specific card | Confidence |
|--------|----------|--------------|-------------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 24 | 1 in 13.5 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.9 | 5.2 | 1 in 90 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 8.2 | 4.4 | 1 in 98.2 | Community estimate |
| Rare BREAK | 1 in 18 | 2 | 1 in 72 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 15 | 2.4 | 1 in 135 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 8.1 | 4.5 | 1 in 40.3 | Community estimate |

All figures come from ThePriceDex.

**Conflicts.**

- These rates add up to about 1.18 cards per pack for one slot.
  Either a figure is wrong, or some hits sit outside the rare slot.
- The secret rare rate conflicts. ThePriceDex gives 1 in 8.1.
  Flipside Gaming gives 1:72 for the era, but also says Evolutions had
  "more favorable pull rates for Secret Rares" than later sets.
- A PokéBeach forum report of one Evolutions box lists 2 BREAK, 2
  full art (one Mega), 5 EX (2 Mega), and 5 secret rares. Confidence:
  **anecdotal, one box**. The page returned HTTP 403, so this was seen
  only as a search snippet. It supports a secret rare rate near the
  ThePriceDex figure, not 1:72.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Secret rares include alternate artwork cards such as "Flying
  Pikachu" and "Surfing Pikachu", and the Trainer card "Here Comes Team
  Rocket!" (Bulbapedia).
- The set includes basic Energy cards (ThePriceDex Energy figure).
- The reverse holo pattern for this set is Unknown. Bulbapedia gives
  the Type-symbol pattern only up to Steam Siege.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**Rare Holo EX and Ultra Rare split one TCGdex rarity.** TCGdex records
both the regular Pokémon-EX cards and the full-art secret cards under
the single rarity "Ultra Rare". The Cards column splits them: the
full-art cards sit in a number block at the end of the Ultra Rare
range; every other Ultra Rare number is a regular Rare Holo EX.

**Rare BREAK sits in the reverse holo slot, not the rare slot** (see
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sets-that-break-the-template)). TCGdex also
records BREAK cards under the "Ultra Rare" rarity; the Cards column
picks them out by number.

Confidence: this map follows the pack template in
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#the-pack-template). No source shows a real
opened pack for this set; the slot contents are the era-wide community
guide.
