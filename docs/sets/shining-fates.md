# Shining Fates (2021)

Set data for Shining Fates, a special expansion. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the approach. See
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md) for the era template and the
evidence behind the pack order.

## Release

- English release: February 19, 2021.
- Set code: SWSH4.5 (official website and checklist). Code card sequence code:
  SF. Abbreviation: SHF. TCGplayer set code: SHF.
- Cards: 195 total. 72 in the main numbering, plus 1 secret rare, plus 122
  Shiny Vault cards.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards per pack (TCGplayer). Energy card and code card: the era template
  says 1 of each. No set-specific source confirms this.
- Slots: Unknown. The era template gives 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse
  holo, 1 rare or higher.
- A Shiny Vault card "can possibly be in a booster, replacing the Reverse
  Holofoil card when it is" (Bulbapedia).
- Bulbapedia says the packs have "one guaranteed Pokémon V or Pokémon VMAX
  card". That sentence is about the Japanese Shiny Star V packs, not the
  English packs. Do not apply it to Shining Fates.

## Pack order

**Confidence: community guide, low.** NinePocket lists Shining Fates and the
Shiny Vault under the Sun & Moon to Sword & Shield method.

| Position | Reveal starts at the commons end |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (can be a Shiny Vault card) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | Uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare is **not** one of the last three cards in either direction.
- A Shiny Vault card sits next to the rare slot, near the middle.
- Which end faces the wrapper front: Unknown.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Shiny Vault: 122 cards numbered SV001/SV122 to SV122/SV122. A Shiny Vault
  card takes the reverse holo slot.
- No booster box. Packs came only in set products.
