## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Confidence: official rule, via Bulbapedia.
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons.
  Confidence: community template for the era. No source covers this set
  by name.
- **1 code card.**
- **No basic Energy card.**
- **Rare slot content:** Rare, Holo Rare, Pokémon-EX, Full Art, or
  secret rare. Confidence: community (PokeBeach, 2013).

### Box reports (PokeBeach)

| Report | Regular EX | Full Art | Secret |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alex282, box 1 (Feb 20, 2012) | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Alex282, box 2 (Feb 25, 2012) | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| **Total, 2 boxes** | **4** | **2** | **1** |

Confidence: empirical, very small sample.

Other reports, which do not split regular EX from Full Art:

- A judge's box had "just 3 EX's and 1 Shiny" (reported second-hand,
  February 1, 2012).
- At one prerelease, a member counted about 12 EX, Full Art included, in
  198 packs. That is ~1 in 16.5 packs (January 28, 2012). Confidence:
  anecdotal hand count.
- One member reported 7 Full Art EX from about 100 packs, mostly from
  blisters (December 27, 2012). That is ~1 in 14 packs. Confidence:
  anecdotal, approximate pack count.

## Special mechanics

- Next Destinies introduced Pokémon-EX. Each Pokémon-EX gives two Prize
  cards when Knocked Out.
- Pokémon-EX came in a regular print and a Full Art print.
- From this set on, secret cards show Shiny Pokémon. They reprint cards
  from earlier Black & White sets.
