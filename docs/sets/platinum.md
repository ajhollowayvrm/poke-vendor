# Platinum (2009)

Set data for Platinum. This set uses the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: February 11, 2009. Japanese release: October 10, 2008,
  as Galactic's Conquest.
- Set number: PL1. Abbreviation: PL.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 127. Secret cards: 3 (128/127 to 130/127). Shiny cards: 3
  (SH4 to SH6).
- First set of the Platinum Series.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 rare slot. Source:
  Bulbapedia (era template).
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, a Rare Holo LV.X, or a secret
  card. PokeBeach Card Ratios says reprints "(SF, PL, RR, SV)" come "as a
  Rare". Confidence: community estimate.
- **Exception:** the SH cards come in the reverse holo slot. PokeBeach Card
  Ratios lists Shiny cards for "SF, PL, SV, AR" with "Comes as a Reverse
  Holo". Confidence: community estimate.

## Pack order

The era template applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period. The
  two sources conflict.
- Secret cards and LV.X cards sit in the rare slot position.
- **Exception:** an SH card is a hit in the reverse holo slot. Its position
  is Unknown.
- The common and uncommon order: Unknown. No code card. No separate Energy
  slot. Which end the cards face: Unknown.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon SP** debut with Pokémon G (Team Galactic's Pokémon). All
  Pokémon SP that are not LV.X are Basic Pokémon.
- **Lost Zone** debut.
- **Error card:** Dialga G LV.X (122/127) had misprinted Energy symbols for
  weakness and resistance. A corrected version exists (Bulbapedia).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**The main Rare Holo tier uses the TCGdex rarity "Holo Rare" in this
set**, not "Rare Holo" as in earlier sets. The Rarity list entry stays
"Rare Holo"; only the TCGdex rarity column differs.

**The 3 secret cards (128–130) and the Shiny (SH4–SH6) cards share the
"Rare" TCGdex rarity with the non-secret Rares.** The secret cards use
the "Holo" variant; SH4–SH6 use "Reverse holo". One non-secret Rare
(38/127, Shaymin) also carries a stray "Holo" print; the Nos. filter
on the secret outcome excludes it.

Confidence: this map follows the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md), with
the set's own exception that Shiny (SH) cards come in the reverse holo
slot. The Rare Holo LV.X rate uses 1 in 18, the lower figure in the
file's "1 in 18 or 1 in 12" range (see this file's Open topics).

| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Shiny (SH) | Shiny (SH) | Rare | Reverse holo | Nos. SH4–SH6 | 1 in 36 |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | Not nos. SH4–SH6 | Rest |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo LV.X | Rare Holo LV.X | Rare Holo LV.X | Holo | Nos. 122–127 | 1 in 18 |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Secret | Rare Secret | Rare | Holo | Nos. 128–130 | 1 in 36 |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Holo Rare | Holo | All | 1 in 3 |

## Open topics

- Pack order: the two community guides conflict. No source shows real
  opened packs. The SH card position is Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: no
  source found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- LV.X per box: 2 or 3. The sources conflict. Searched 2026-09-12: a
  PokeBeach forum post mentions a box with six secret cards and four LV.X
  cards. This is a single anecdote and does not resolve the conflict.
- The names of the secret cards 128–130 and SH4–SH6: not recorded here.
- Whether the misprinted and corrected Dialga G LV.X both came in booster
  packs, and at what ratio: Unknown.
- The non-holo rare rate. Boxes per case. Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo LV.X uses 1 in 18 or 1 in 12. PokeBeach Card Ratios' first post gives 1 in 12 (3 per box); a reply gives 1 in 18 (2 per box).
- **Slot map:** the Rare Holo LV.X odds use 1 in 18, the lower end of
  the file's "1 in 18 or 1 in 12" range. The higher end (1 in 12) would
  give more LV.X pulls per box.
