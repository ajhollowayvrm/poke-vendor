# Legendary Treasures (2013)

Set data for Black & White—Legendary Treasures, the last set of the
Black & White Series. Legendary Treasures does **not** use the era pack
template. The template is in [eras/black-white.md](eras/black-white.md).
The general approach is in [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md).

## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** pokemon.com: "Packs of 10 additional cards
  selected from the expansion". Confidence: official.
- **1 code card.** Confidence: community (PokeBeach, October 27, 2013).

| Slot | Count | What it holds | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Main set commons | Community |
| Reverse holo or holo rare | 1 | A reverse holo, or a holo rare | Community |
| Rare | 1 | Rare, Holo Rare, Pokémon-EX, or gold secret rare | Community |
| Radiant Collection, uncommon or higher | 1 | RC uncommon, RC11 Meloetta-EX, or an RC Full Art | Community |
| Radiant Collection, common | 1 | RC common | Community |
| Uncommon | 2 | Main set uncommons | Community |
| Code card | 1 | Not a game card | Community |

Sources for the slots: AnimeDudde, a judge who opened a half box
(PokeBeach, October 27, 2013). A second member (DNA, PokeBeach, December
16, 2013) gives the same slots.

Slot rules:

- A pack cannot hold two holo rares. AnimeDudde: "if the reverse-slot is
  occupied by a holo rare, it won't occupy the next slot."
- DNA: "If the RH slot has a rare holo in it, the rare slot can have
  rare, EX, or gold rare in it - just not a rare holo."
- There are no ACE SPEC cards in this set.
- Reverse holos are less common than in other sets. AnimeDudde: "I think
  only about half of my packs had them." Another member got "under 10"
  reverse holos in one box. Confidence: community.

## Pack order

AnimeDudde listed the cards "in order":

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Common |
| 5 | Reverse holo or holo rare (a hit when holo rare) |
| 6 | Rare slot (a hit) |
| 7 | Radiant Collection, uncommon or higher (a hit when RC11 or RC21–RC25) |
| 8 | Radiant Collection, common |
| 9–10 | Uncommon |
| 11 | Code card |

- **Where the hits sit:** cards 5, 6, and 7 of 10 in the listed order.
  In reverse order, the hits are cards 4, 5, and 6. No hit is one of the
  last three cards in either direction.
- **Direction:** Unknown.
- **Confidence:** community. The member says the list is "in order". A
  second member repeats the same order without the code card. No photo
  or video confirms it.
- **Difference from the template:** Legendary Treasures has 4 commons
  instead of 5, and 2 uncommons instead of 3. Two Radiant Collection
  cards sit after the rare slot.

## Hit odds

| Hit | Odds | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rare slot filled | Every pack | Official rule, via Bulbapedia |
| Radiant Collection card | 2 per pack | Community |
| Reverse holo | About half of packs | Community (one half box) |
| Pokémon-EX (main set) | ~6 per box (~1 in 6 packs) | Empirical, 6 box reports |
| RC Full Art (RC21–RC25) | ~8.7 per box (~1 in 4 packs) | Empirical, 6 box reports |
| Meloetta-EX RC11 | ~1.5 per box | Empirical, 6 box reports |
| Gold secret rare | ~0.5 per box (~1 in 72 packs) | Empirical, 6 box reports |
| Gold secret rare | 3 in about 5 prerelease boxes | Community (AnimeDudde, October 27, 2013) |
| Holo rare | 17, 18, and 21 holos per box in three reports | Empirical, three boxes |

The per-pack figures assume 36 packs per box. ToyWiz and Potomac
Distribution confirm 36 packs per box for this set.

## Special mechanics

- **Radiant Collection.** A 25-card subset from the Japanese Shiny
  Collection. The cards "have a special coating and foil patterns" and
  their own numbers (RC##/RC25). Source: pokemon.com and Bulbapedia.
- **Gold Full Art cards.** Legendary Treasures introduced entirely gold
  Full Art cards (the two secret rares).
- **Reprints.** The set has alternate-art reprints from the whole Black &
  White Series. Ten Pokémon-EX are direct reprints.
- **English only.** Bulbapedia says the set came out only in English.

## Open topics

- **Pack direction.** Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: no source found.
- **Pack order evidence.** One judge's half box, and one repeat of the
  same list. No photo or video source. Searched 2026-09-12: no photo or
  video source found.
- **Box and case size.** Resolved. ToyWiz and Potomac Distribution
  confirm 36 packs per box and 6 boxes per case.
- **RC Full Art collation.** Box reports show about 2 copies of each RC
  Full Art per box. No source says if this count is guaranteed.
- **Holo rare odds per pack.** The holo counts per box mix holo rares
  from the reverse holo slot and the rare slot. No source splits them.
- **Gold secret rare odds.** Only 6 boxes and one prerelease count.
- **Bulbapedia vs. box reports.** Bulbapedia names only blisters as
  merchandise. Members report booster boxes.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare, Rare Holo and Radiant Collection — Uncommon. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, a Radiant Collection Full Art card (1 in 4) comes more often than a Rare Holo EX card (1 in 6). The rarity list therefore puts Radiant Collection — Full Art below Rare Holo EX.
