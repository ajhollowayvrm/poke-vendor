# BREAKthrough (2015)

Set-specific data for XY—BREAKthrough. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: November 4, 2015 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: BKT (Limitless TCG, Bulbapedia expansion list). A summary of
  the Bulbapedia set page gave "BRT". This file uses BKT.
- Card count: 162 cards plus 2 secret rares (164 total).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons (ThePriceDex).
- **The BREAK card replaces the reverse holo, not the rare slot.** An
  Elite Fourum thread states BREAK cards "took the place of the
  reverse holo". Confidence: **community forum report**. See the
  resolved conflict under
  [Rarities and hit odds](#rarities-and-hit-odds).

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
EX, Rare BREAK. The Bulbapedia expansion list and ThePriceDex also
show Ultra Rare and Secret Rare cards.

| Rarity | Per pack | Per box (36) | Confidence |
|--------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Not retrieved | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.6 | Not retrieved | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 11.3 | 3.2 | Community estimate |
| Rare BREAK | 1 in 12.9 | 2.8 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 17.0 | 2.1 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 125 | 0.3 | Community estimate |

All figures come from ThePriceDex.

**Resolved.** Rare BREAK sits in the reverse holo slot, not the rare
slot (Elite Fourum; see [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sets-that-break-the-template)).
Without Rare BREAK, the remaining rare-slot rows add up to about 1.00
card per pack: Rare (0.667) + Rare Holo (0.179) + Rare Holo EX (0.088)
+ Ultra Rare (0.059) + Secret Rare (0.008). Posters on Elite Fourum
report "2 or 3" BREAK cards per booster box (36 packs), which matches
the ThePriceDex rate of 1 in 12.9.

Flipside Gaming gives BREAK at "around 1:12 booster packs" and secret
rares at 1:72 for the era (**community estimate**).

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 16.4 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 10.9 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 2.7 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 2.5 packs".

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Pokémon BREAK starts in this set. Bulbapedia: the cards are "placed
  sideways over the artwork window of the existing Pokémon". The
  artwork is "completely covered in gold, with sparks surrounding it".
- BREAK cards include Chesnaught BREAK, Raichu BREAK, Marowak BREAK,
  Zoroark BREAK, Florges BREAK, and Noivern BREAK (Bulbapedia).
