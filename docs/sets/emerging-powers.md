## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Confidence: official rule, via Bulbapedia.
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons.
  Confidence: community template for the era. No source covers this set
  by name.
- **1 code card.** Bulbapedia says "Beginning with this expansion, all
  Theme Decks and booster packs came with a QR code card".
- **No basic Energy card.**

## Special mechanics

- Emerging Powers has no Japanese main expansion equivalent. It holds
  cards from the Battle Strength Decks and the rest of Black Collection
  and White Collection.
- Emerging Powers introduced a new reverse holo pattern. The pattern
  shows Energy symbols on Pokémon and a Poké Ball on Trainer cards.
