# HeartGold & SoulSilver era (2010–2011)

This file gives the pack template that the English sets of this era share.
Each set file records its own data and its differences from this template.
See [../../13-sets.md](../../13-sets.md) for what a set file must cover.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| HeartGold & SoulSilver | HGSS1 | HS | February 10, 2010 | [heartgold-soulsilver.md](../heartgold-soulsilver.md) |
| Unleashed | HGSS2 | UL | May 12, 2010 | [unleashed.md](../unleashed.md) |
| Undaunted | HGSS3 | UD | August 18, 2010 | [undaunted.md](../undaunted.md) |

Bulbapedia gives Call of Legends its own series code (COL1). The official
Pokémon website lists it with the HeartGold & SoulSilver Series. This doc
puts it in this era because it uses the same pack format.

### Set list check

- The Bulbapedia expansion list shows the same five English sets with booster
  packs. This research adds no set to the starting list.
- These products are not in scope, because they had no booster packs of
  their own:
  - HGSS Black Star Promos (HSP). The promos came in blisters, tins, and
    collections.
  - HS Trainer Kit (early May 2010). It holds two half decks and one
    HeartGold & SoulSilver booster pack. It is not a set.
- Pokémon Rumble (December 2, 2009, 16 cards) is in the Platinum Series on
  Bulbapedia. It is not in this era.
- Sampling Packs started with HeartGold & SoulSilver. A Sampling Pack has
  three cards: two commons and one reverse holo. Stores did not sell them.
  Magazines and events gave them out. The game does not model them.

## Pack template

### Structure

| Item | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cards per pack | 10 | Retailer listings (Loose Packs, Potomac, ToyWiz) |
| Packs per booster box | 36 | Retailer listings for HS and CL; community box reports for UD |
| Boxes per case | Unknown | No source found |
| Code card | None | NinePocket: packs before 2011 have no code card |

Slot breakdown (community description, consistent across sources):

- 5 common slots.
- 3 uncommon slots. Trainer cards can fill a common or an uncommon slot.
- 1 reverse holo slot. Any card of the set can be the reverse holo, a rare
  included. A **Pokémon Prime replaces the reverse holo** when a pack has one.
- 1 rare slot. It holds a non-holo rare, a holo rare, a LEGEND half, or a
  secret rare.

A pack can hold two hits: for example, a Prime in the reverse holo slot and a
holo rare in the rare slot.

### Energy cards

- English packs of this era have **no guaranteed energy slot**. The
  Japanese packs had one, and Bulbapedia describes only the Japanese pack
  that way.
- PokeBeach wrote in 2017 that Sun & Moon was "reintroducing a Basic Energy
  as the 11th card". This supports a 10-card English pack with no energy slot.
- A basic energy card can come out of an English pack only when the set list
  includes basic energy cards:
  - HeartGold & SoulSilver: basic energy cards 115–122 are Common.
  - Unleashed, Undaunted, Triumphant: no basic energy cards in the set list.
  - Call of Legends: basic energy cards 88–95 come only as reverse holos.

### Pack order

Card 1 is the first card that the player sees with the card fronts facing
them. Two community sources agree on this order. Neither source shows a
documented sample of opened HGSS-era packs.

| Card | Slot | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common or uncommon, random order | Community claim |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (or Prime) | Community claim, one source |
| 7 | Rare slot | Community claim, two sources |
| 8–10 | Common or uncommon, random order | Community claim |

- **The rare slot is card 7, the fourth card from the back. It is not one of
  the last three cards.** This is an exception to the game default in
  [../../18-ripping.md](../../18-ripping.md#where-the-hit-sits).
- A Prime or a reverse holo rare is card 6. It is also not one of the last
  three cards.

How the sources support this order:

- cardcollector.co.uk lists all five sets as "3 cards to the back". Its
  method: hold the backs toward you, "Take the card that's closest to you,
  and put it to the front. Do this another two times." Then "the rare card
  will be the last one you reveal". Moving three cards to the front makes
  the rare last, so the rare starts with three cards behind it.
- A forum post from August 28, 2010 describes 10-card "D/P/P and HG/SS"
  packs. It says: "They only put the reverse and rare cards in a specific
  spot in the pack, the rest are random". After the poster moves three cards
  from the back, the reverse holo is card 9 and the rare is card 10. Before
  the move, they were cards 6 and 7. The example pack is Stormfront, a
  Diamond & Pearl era set, not an HGSS-era set.
- NinePocket disagrees: "Packs from Black & White and earlier did not follow
  one reliably documented internal order, so nothing consistently puts the
  rare last."

## Rarity system

| Rarity | Where it sits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Common slots, reverse holo slot | |
| Uncommon | Uncommon slots, reverse holo slot | |
| Rare | Rare slot, reverse holo slot | |
| Rare Holo | Rare slot | Community estimate: 1 in 3 packs for "any set" |
| Rare Prime | Reverse holo slot | HS–TM only |
| Rare Holo LEGEND | Rare slot | Two cards (top half and bottom half) make one LEGEND. HS–TM only |
| Rare Secret (Alph Lithograph) | Rare slot (community claim) | One per set, HS–TM only |
| Shiny Legendary (SL1–SL11) | Unknown | Call of Legends only. Official rarity "Rare Holo" |

Odds per set are in each set file. Wizards of the Coast and The Pokémon
Company did not publish odds for these sets, so every figure is
community-derived.

### Pokémon Prime

- Prime cards have a holofoil border, gold foil name text, and spikes on the
  artwork border. They have no Pokédex entry.
- Bulbapedia: "They replaced the Reverse Holofoil in booster packs."

### Pokémon LEGEND

- One LEGEND is two cards: a top half and a bottom half. A player must play
  both halves to the Bench at the same time.
- Each half is a separate pull from the rare slot. One pack cannot give a
  full LEGEND.
- Community box reports count "Legend pieces", which are halves.

## Sets that break the template

- **HeartGold & SoulSilver:** it has a shiny Gyarados (123/123) in addition
  to the normal holo rares. It is the only set in this era with basic energy
  cards in the common pool.
- **Undaunted and Triumphant:** each theme deck holds one booster pack.
- **Call of Legends:** it has no Prime and no LEGEND cards. It adds 11
  Shiny Legendary cards (SL1–SL11). Its basic energy cards come only as
  reverse holos.

## Open topics

- **Pack order conflict.** Two community sources put the rare at card 7.
  NinePocket says this era has no reliable order. No source shows a
  documented sample of opened HGSS-era packs. A video study of real packs
  must confirm the order.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found. Elite Fourum threads on pack slots
  and card rarity guides do not cover a documented HGSS-era pack order.
- The forum source uses a Stormfront pack as its example. No source gives
  an example pack from an HGSS-era set.
- Unknown: whether the order holds for every print run and every pack
  artwork.
- Unknown: whether a player can open a pack from either end. If so, the
  order reverses and the rare becomes card 4.
- Unknown: the order of the 5 commons and 3 uncommons in cards 1–5 and 8–10.
- Unknown: boxes per case for every set in this era.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found. Current retailer listings for this
  era sell single boxes, not sealed cases.
- Unknown: whether the holo rare figure "1 in 3 packs for any set" applies
  to this era. Undaunted box reports show 8–10 holos per box, not 12.
- Not checked: McDonald's Collection 2011 and its era.
- Not read: a PokéGym thread named "HGSS Undaunted 'Sample Pack'" (the
  server blocked the request).
