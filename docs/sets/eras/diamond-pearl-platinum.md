# Diamond & Pearl and Platinum era (2007–2009)

This file holds the pack template that the Diamond & Pearl Series and the
Platinum Series share. Each set file records only its own data and its
exceptions. See [../../13-sets.md](../../13-sets.md) for what a set file
covers.

The era starts with Diamond & Pearl (May 23, 2007). It ends with Arceus
(November 4, 2009). HeartGold & SoulSilver (February 10, 2010) starts the
next era, so it is not in this file.

## The pack template

### Structure

- 10 cards in each booster pack. Diamond & Pearl changed the pack from 9
  cards to 10 cards. Source: Bulbapedia, set page and booster pack page.
- Slots: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, and 1 Rare or Rare
  Holo. Source: Bulbapedia, Diamond & Pearl set page.
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, a Rare Holo LV.X, or a
  secret card. Confidence: community reference (PokeBeach forum posts).
- The reverse holo slot can hold a Shiny (SH) card, a Rotom (RT) card, or
  an Arceus (AR) card in the sets that have them. Confidence: community
  estimate (PokeBeach Card Ratios).
- No pack has a separate Energy slot. No pack has a code card.

### Pack order

The rip screen shows cards in the order they come out, with the front of
the pack toward the player.

| Claim | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| The rare slot card has 3 cards behind it. It is card 7 of 10. | [Card Collector](https://cardcollector.co.uk/pokemon-card-trick-pack-opening/) lists "3" under "Cards to The Back" for all 11 sets. | Community guide (published 2023-03-14). No method stated. The card position is derived. |
| No reliable internal order exists for these packs. | [NinePocket](https://www.ninepocket.net/guides/pokemon-pack-trick): "Packs from Black & White and earlier did not follow one reliably documented internal order, so nothing consistently puts the rare last." | Community guide. No author, date, or method. |
| No code card is in the pack. | NinePocket: "Packs printed before 2011 have no code card to remove in the first place." | Community guide. |
| The order of the commons, the uncommons, and the reverse holo is Unknown. | No source found. | Unknown. |
| Which end of the wrapper the cards face is Unknown. | No source found. | Unknown. |

**How the card 7 position is derived.** Card Collector tells the reader to
hold the cards with "the back of the cards facing you". The reader takes
"the card that's closest to you, and put it to the front". The reader does
this once for each card in the "Cards to The Back" figure. Then the rare
is "the last one you reveal". Each move takes the last card of the
front-facing stack and makes it the first card. Three moves put the rare
last only if three cards were behind it. So the rare is card 7 of 10.

**Conflict with the game default.** The game default puts the hit in the
last three cards (see
[../../18-ripping.md](../../18-ripping.md#where-the-hit-sits)). If Card
Collector is correct, every set in this era breaks that default. The rare
is the fourth card from the back. A hit in the reverse holo slot (SH, RT,
or AR) has an Unknown position.

### Rarity system

| Rarity (Bulbapedia label) | Slot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Common | Diamond & Pearl lists its 8 Energy cards as Common. |
| Uncommon | Uncommon | Later sets list their Energy cards as Uncommon or Rare. |
| Rare | Rare | Non-holo rare. |
| Rare Holo | Rare | About 1 in 3 packs. Community estimate. |
| Rare Holo LV.X | Rare | Rate changes during the era. See the table below. |
| Rare Secret | Rare | Mysterious Treasures, Stormfront, Platinum, Rising Rivals, Supreme Victors. |
| Rare Holo (SH, RT, AR subsets) | Reverse holo | Numbered outside the main set. |

### LV.X odds across the era

All figures are community estimates. No official odds exist for this era.

| Set | PokeBeach Card Ratios (2010), first post | Correction reply in the same thread |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond & Pearl | 1 per box (1:36) | 1 |
| Mysterious Treasures | 1 per box (1:36) | 1 |
| Secret Wonders | 0–1 per box (1:36) | 0–1 |
| Great Encounters | 1 per box (1:36) | 1 |
| Majestic Dawn | 1 per box (1:36) | 1 |
| Legends Awakened | 3 per box (1:12) | 2 |
| Stormfront | 3 per box (1:12) | 2 |
| Platinum | 3 per box (1:12) | 2 |
| Rising Rivals | 2–3 per box (1:12–1:18) | 2–3 |
| Supreme Victors | 3–4 per box (1:9–1:12) | 3–4 |
| Arceus | 3 per box (1:12) | 3 |

Flipside Gaming gives "approximately 1:36 packs for the first sets" and
"1:18 pull rate for later sets". A PokeBeach reply (September 2012) says
one LV.X per box for Diamond & Pearl and Mysterious Treasures. It says
"two, rarely three per box" for the Platinum block.

### Box collation

- 36 packs in each booster box. Retailer listings confirm this for
  Diamond & Pearl, Stormfront, Supreme Victors, and Arceus. PokeBeach Card
  Ratios treats "one per box" as 1:36 for every set in the era.
- Boxes per case: Unknown.
- Collation is **variable**. Forum reports for Stormfront show 0 to 5 LV.X
  in one box. Some posters claim a fixed ratio per box. No study confirms
  it.

### Excluded products (no booster packs)

- **DP Black Star Promos** (56 cards, May 2007 – December 2009). These are
  promotional cards in other products.
- **Pokémon Rumble** (16 cards, December 2, 2009). Bulbapedia describes a
  game collection with a game board and damage counters. No source shows
  booster packs.
- Theme Decks, Trainer Kits, and World Championships Decks. These have
  fixed contents, not booster packs.

## Sets that break the template

- **Diamond & Pearl:** Energy cards are Common, so an Energy card can come
  in a common slot. LV.X is 1 per box.
- **Mysterious Treasures:** first secret card of the era (124/123), in the
  rare slot, about 1:108.
- **Secret Wonders:** LV.X is 0–1 per box. Some reverse holos come only in
  blister packs.
- **Legends Awakened:** the LV.X rate rises. Sources give 2 or 3 per box.
- **Stormfront:** adds SH1–SH3 in the reverse holo slot and 3 secret cards
  in the rare slot.
- **Platinum:** adds Pokémon SP, SH4–SH6, and 3 secret cards.
- **Rising Rivals:** adds Rotom RT1–RT6 in the reverse holo slot (about
  1:18) and 3 secret cards.
- **Supreme Victors:** adds SH7–SH9 and 3 secret cards. LV.X is 3–4 per box.
- **Arceus:** adds AR1–AR9 in the reverse holo slot (about 1:4) and
  SH10–SH12. No secret cards.
- **POP Series:** 2-card packs (POP 1–7) or 3-card packs (POP 8–9). A
  production error put one rare in every POP Series 6 pack.

## Open topics

- **Pack order is the biggest gap.** One community guide gives a rare
  position. A second guide says no reliable order exists. No source shows
  real opened packs. A pack-opening video review must confirm the order
  before the rip screen uses it.
- The position of the reverse holo slot is Unknown. SH, RT, and AR hits
  sit in that slot.
- Boxes per case: Unknown for every set.
- No source gives the non-holo rare rate. No source says if the 1 in 3
  holo rate includes LV.X or secret cards.
- The LV.X figures for Legends Awakened, Stormfront, and Platinum conflict
  (2 or 3 per box).
- No source confirms whether an LV.X can come in a reverse holo form.
