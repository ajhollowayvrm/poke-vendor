# EX era (2003–2007)

This file holds the pack template that the 16 English EX Series sets
share. Each set file under `docs/sets/` records the data for one set and
every exception to this template. See [../../13-sets.md](../../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

POP Series 1–5 are EX-era promotional sets. They already have a file:
[../pop-series.md](../pop-series.md). That file also covers POP Series
6–9, which fall in the next era.

## Pack template

### Pack contents

An English EX Series booster pack holds 9 cards. This count starts with
the e-Card era and continues through the whole EX Series (Bulbapedia).

| Slot | Count | Can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 or 5 | Common |
| Uncommon | 2 or 3 | Uncommon |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare or Rare Holo, including Rare Holo ex where present |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse Holo of a Common, Uncommon, or Rare |

Sources disagree on the common and uncommon counts.

- One source count for EX Ruby & Sapphire: 5 commons, 2 uncommons, 1
  reverse holo, 1 rare (9 total).
- A second, more general source for the EX Series: "at least 1 Rare
  card, generally 3 Uncommon cards, and the remainder are Common," plus
  1 reverse holo (9 total with 4 commons, or 10 with 5 — the source does
  not resolve this).

**Confidence: community estimate.** No official Nintendo or Pokémon USA
pack-slot table was found for this era. The set files record the
per-set count when a source states one.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a card of any rarity, including a rare.
  So one pack can hold two rare-or-better cards: the rare slot and a
  reverse holo rare (Bulbapedia).
- Reverse holo cards in this era carry a stamp of the expansion logo in
  the bottom right corner of the picture (Bulbapedia).
- Box: 36 packs (Bulbapedia merchandise page, and confirmed per-set by
  sealed-product listings for EX Deoxys).

### Rarity system

In order from most common to rarest:

| Rarity | Symbol | First set | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | — | EX Ruby & Sapphire | |
| Uncommon | — | EX Ruby & Sapphire | |
| Rare | 1 black star | EX Ruby & Sapphire | Non-holo |
| Rare Holo | 1 black star, holofoil | EX Ruby & Sapphire | |
| δ Delta Species | δ symbol on a Rare Holo | EX Delta Species | A Pokémon printed with a type other than its usual type. Not a separate print rarity: a δ card can be Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, or Rare Holo ☆. |
| Rare Holo ex | Cosmos holofoil border | EX Ruby & Sapphire | Pokémon-ex. A Knockout on this card gives the opponent 2 Prizes instead of 1 (Bulbapedia). |
| Rare Holo ☆ (Gold Star, Pokémon ☆) | ☆ | EX Team Rocket Returns | A different-colored version of an existing Pokémon. Only one Pokémon ☆ is legal per deck (Bulbapedia). |
| Secret Rare | Varies | EX Dragon | Numbered past the set's printed total, for example 100/97. |

- **Crystal-type cards are not part of this era.** The task brief listed
  them as an example special rarity, but Bulbapedia's rarity page places
  Crystal Pokémon only in Aquapolis and Skyridge, both e-Card era sets
  (see [e-card.md](e-card.md)). No EX-era set file should carry a
  Crystal-type entry.
- **Holon is not a rarity.** "Holon" names a fictional region and a
  group of Special Energy and Trainer cards (the Holon Engine: Holon
  Energy FF, WP, GL, and others) introduced in EX Delta Species and
  continued through EX Holon Phantoms and EX Dragon Frontiers. These
  cards sit in the normal Common/Uncommon/Rare rarities. δ Delta
  Species is the era's actual special rarity-adjacent mechanic.
- **Pokémon Star and Gold Star are the same rarity.** The task brief
  named them separately; the research found one rarity, "Rare Holo ☆"
  a.k.a. Pokémon ☆ a.k.a. Gold Star, all describing the same card type.

### Pack order

**Confidence: community estimate, very low.** NinePocket's pack-trick
guide, which the e-Card era file also relies on, states for this era:
"No reliable trick — internal order varied and vintage packs are best
left sealed," and adds that packs from this period and earlier "did not
follow one reliably documented internal order, so nothing consistently
puts the rare last."

- No set file in this era can record a confirmed card-by-card order.
- One secondary source (an aggregated retail description of EX Ruby &
  Sapphire) states that the reverse holo and the rare card are, "in most
  case, the last two cards in the pack." This is the only positional
  claim found. It is not a primary or research source, so its
  confidence is low.

### Where the hit sits

- If the one secondary claim above holds, the hit sits at position 8 or
  9 of 9, inside the last three cards, matching the game default in
  [../../18-ripping.md](../../18-ripping.md#where-the-hit-sits).
- No source confirms this for any specific EX-era set. Each set file
  must mark its pack order and hit position as Unknown unless later
  research finds a primary source (a photo or video of an opened pack).

### Conflicts in the template

1. **Common and uncommon counts.** Sources disagree between "5 commons,
   2 uncommons" and "4 commons, 3 uncommons" (see Pack contents above).
2. **δ Delta Species end date.** Bulbapedia's EX Crystal Guardians page
   calls that set "the last expansion to feature dual-type Pokémon δ
   Delta Species." Bulbapedia's EX Dragon Frontiers page, for the set
   released after Crystal Guardians, says Dragon Frontiers "heavily
   features Pokémon-ex Delta Species" with the δ symbol "present on
   virtually all Pokémon cards." These two claims conflict. The set
   files must record both and pick a position.

  who made that claim gave no supporting sample, and another poster

  anecdote.

  ~1:72 Gold Star rate, calling out "unusually frequent gold stars."

- A further research pass (2026-09-12) confirms no box-count source

## Sets that break the template

- **EX Ruby & Sapphire and EX Sandstorm:** no Rare Holo ☆ and no Secret
  Rare. Only Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, and Rare Holo ex exist.
- **EX Dragon:** the first set with a Secret Rare (a 3-card Charmander
  evolution line at 98–100/97, printed past the set's 97-card total).
  Still no Rare Holo ☆.
- **EX Team Rocket Returns:** the first set with Rare Holo ☆ (Mudkip ☆,
  Torchic ☆, Treecko ☆), plus one Secret Rare Pokémon and one Secret
  Rare Trainer card (110–111/109).
- **EX Unseen Forces:** 3 Rare Holo ☆ (Entei ☆, Raikou ☆, Suicune ☆) and
  2 Secret Rares (Rocket's Persian ex, Celebi ex, at 116–117/115).
- **EX Delta Species:** introduces the δ Delta Species mechanic. 3 Rare
  Holo ☆ (Groudon ☆, Kyogre ☆, Metagross ☆) and 1 Secret Rare (Azumarill,
  114/113).
- **EX Legend Maker:** 3 Rare Holo ☆ (Regice ☆, Regirock ☆, Registeel ☆)
  and 1 Secret Rare (Pikachu δ, 93/92).
- **EX Holon Phantoms:** 3 Rare Holo ☆ (Gyarados ☆ δ, Mewtwo ☆, Pikachu
  ☆) and 1 Secret Rare, Mew (111/110), both from packs. Bulbapedia also
  lists a non-holo Mew (111/110) that came only with the "Lucario and
  the Mystery of Mew" DVD. That card is not in packs.
- **EX Crystal Guardians:** its two Rare Holo ☆ (Alakazam ☆, Celebi ☆)
  sit inside the printed 100-card count, at 99/100 and 100/100, so they
  are not Secret Rares by the numbering convention this file uses
  elsewhere. Bulbapedia calls this "the last expansion to feature
  dual-type Pokémon δ Delta Species" — a claim that conflicts with the
  next set (see Conflicts in the template).
- **EX Dragon Frontiers:** 2 Rare Holo ☆ (Charizard ☆ δ, Mew ☆ δ), both
  themselves δ Delta Species cards. Bulbapedia describes δ as present on
  "virtually all Pokémon cards" in this set.
- **EX Power Keepers:** 3 Rare Holo ☆ (Flareon ☆, Jolteon ☆, Vaporeon ☆),
  with a Gold Star rate that Flipside Gaming calls unusually high next
  to the rest of the era ("1 to 2 per box").

## Open topics

- **Common/uncommon split.** Two sources give different counts (5/2 vs.
  4/3). No set file should assume one without checking its own set.
- **Pack order.** No primary source (photo or video of a real opened
  pack) was found for any EX-era set. NinePocket rates this whole era as
  having no reliable trick. Searched 2026-09-12: no source found.
- **Boxes per case.** Searched 2026-09-12: no source found for the
  original print run.
- **Collation.** Searched 2026-09-12: no source found for any set.
- **Full box-count breakdown for EX Dragon Frontiers.** Resolved
  2026-09-12: extracted from the Elite Fourum "Guide to early EX
  Series pull rates" thread (see the Odds by set table and
  [../dragon-frontiers.md](../dragon-frontiers.md)).
- **Pull rates for EX Emerald, EX Unseen Forces, EX Delta Species, and
  EX Legend Maker.** Searched 2026-09-12: no source found beyond the
  general ~1:72 Gold Star estimate.
- **Pull rates for EX Holon Phantoms, EX Crystal Guardians, and EX
  Power Keepers.** Partly resolved 2026-09-12: set-specific Rare Holo
  ex and Rare Holo ☆ figures found for each (see the Odds by set
  table). No full box-count breakdown found for any of the three; see
  each set's own Open topics.
- **Theme Deck pack contents.** Bulbapedia's merchandise page implies an
  attached booster pack for some Theme Decks, but this research could
  not confirm whether that applies to every EX-era Theme Deck or to a
  specific bundled product. Searched 2026-09-12: unresolved.
- **δ Delta Species end date.** EX Crystal Guardians and EX Dragon
  Frontiers make conflicting claims about which set is last to feature
  δ Delta Species (see Conflicts in the template). Unresolved.
- **EX Ruby & Sapphire release date.** June 18, 2003 vs. July 18, 2003
  (see Sets in the era). Unresolved; this file recommends June 18, 2003.
