# Mega Evolution era (2025 to 2026)

The pack template that most English Mega Evolution era sets share. Each
set file under `docs/sets/` records its own data and exceptions. See
[../../13-sets.md](../../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

Research date: 2026-09-12. Data for the newest sets is thin. Most odds
come from community and retailer studies, not from The Pokemon Company.

- **30th Celebration**: a special set of the Mega Evolution Series. The
  English release is 2026-09-16, after the research date. Its packs

  no booster boxes. Research it after release.

  (Mega Gengar ex, Mega Diancie ex) and the Mega Lucario ex League

## The pack template

### Contents

TPCi describes each pack as "10 cards, 1 Basic Energy, and 1 Pokémon TCG
Live code card" (official, from the Perfect Order, Chaos Rising, and
Pitch Black product pages).

| Slot | Count | What the slot can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Reverse holo common, uncommon, or rare |
| Reverse holo 2 | 1 | Reverse holo, or an Illustration Rare or Special Illustration Rare |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | 1 | Pokemon TCG Live code card |

Confidence:

- The 4 common and 3 uncommon counts: community estimate (ThePriceDex
  lists them for every set in the era).
- IR and SIR in the second reverse holo slot: community source
  (Obsidia for Perfect Order and Chaos Rising). The Scarlet & Violet
  template used the same rule (PokeBeach, 2023).
- Mega Hyper Rare in the rare slot: community source (Obsidia, Perfect
  Order). No official source.

A July 2025 box label said that Mega Evolution packs hold an Energy
**or** a code card. TPCi then said that this was incorrect. Packs keep
both cards (PokeBeach, July 2025).

A cross-check supports the two reverse holo slots. ThePriceDex gives
about 1.85 reverse holos per Mega Evolution pack. Two slots, minus the
~12% of packs with an IR or SIR, give about 1.88. This is arithmetic on
community estimates, not a confirmed fact.

### Pack order

The order of the cards when the player takes the stack out of the pack.
Position 1 is the front card. Position 12 is the back card.

| Position | Card | Hit? |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 | No (price can make one a hit) |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 | No (price can make one a hit) |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 | Sometimes, by price |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2 or IR / SIR | IR and SIR are hits |
| 10 | Rare slot | Yes (rare or higher) |
| 11 | Basic Energy | No |
| 12 | Code card | No |

Sources and confidence:

- NinePocket (community guide, Mega Evolution): "the rare sits at the
  back, behind only a basic Energy and the code card." It also says
  "the one before it is a reverse holo."
- PokeBeach (2023, Scarlet & Violet): 4 commons, 3 uncommons, 2 reverse
  holos, 1 holo, then the Basic Energy and the code card. NinePocket says
  that Mega Evolution packs "follow the Scarlet & Violet-era layout."
- PokéPatch (community guide, Scarlet & Violet): remove the code card,
  then "move 1 card from the back to the front." That card is the Energy.
- Confidence: **community guide**. No source in this research shows a
  recorded opening of a Mega Evolution era pack card by card.

### Where the hit sits

- The rare slot is position 10 of 12. It is one of the last three cards.
- The IR / SIR slot is position 9 of 12. **It is not one of the last
  three cards** when the rip screen shows the Energy and the code card.
- If the rip screen skips the code card (11 cards), positions 9 and 10
  are both in the last three.
- The last two cards are always non-hits: the Basic Energy, then the
  code card. This is the main exception to the default rule in
  [../../18-ripping.md](../../18-ripping.md#where-the-hit-sits).

### Rarity system

| Rarity | Typical odds per pack | Slot |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 per pack | Common |
| Uncommon | 3 per pack | Uncommon |
| Rare | 1 in 1.4 | Rare slot |
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 | Rare slot |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 12 | Rare slot |
| Illustration Rare | 1 in 9 | Reverse holo 2 |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 80 to 1 in 101 | Reverse holo 2 |
| Mega Hyper Rare | 1 in 956 to 1 in 1,786 | Rare slot |

These are ranges across the set files. See each set file for the figure,
the sample, and the confidence.

## How the era differs from Scarlet & Violet

- **Mega Hyper Rare** replaces the gold Hyper Rare. PokeBeach reports it
  as "7x rarer than the rarest gold cards in the Scarlet & Violet series."
  A set has only 1 or 2 Mega Hyper Rares.
- **Mega Evolution Pokémon ex** cards use a black-and-yellow ex symbol.
  They give up 3 Prize cards (Bulbapedia).
- **Mirror Holofoil** variants return. They were last seen in the Black &
  White Series (Bulbapedia, Mega Evolution).
- **Full-art Ultra Rare Item, Pokémon Tool, and Stadium cards** first
  appear in Mega Evolution (Bulbapedia).
- **SIR odds** in Mega Evolution were 1 in 101. PokeBeach gives about
  1 in 90 for Scarlet & Violet. Later sets in the era are near 1 in 80.
- **Pack contents, slot model, and pack order** stay the same as Scarlet
  & Violet (community guide, NinePocket).

## Sets that break the template

- **Ascended Heroes** (special set):
  - No booster boxes.
  - A new rarity, **Mega Attack Rare** (7 cards). It takes rare-slot
    share from Ultra Rares (UR odds fall to 1 in 21).
  - Two reverse holo patterns: Energy and Poké Ball (or Team Rocket "R").
  - **God packs** exist. Every card in the pack is a high rarity.
  - 2 Mega Hyper Rares at 1 in 540, which is much more frequent than
    the other sets.
- **Mega Evolution**: 2 Mega Hyper Rares. It also has an 18-pack half
  booster box and an Enhanced Booster Box.
- No other released set breaks the template on the data found.

## Open topics

- **Pack order has no recorded-opening source.** Only community guides
  describe it. Find a video or photo series of opened era packs.
- **Face direction is unclear.** PokéPatch does its trick with the cards
  "face down". NinePocket does not say. Confirm which way the card faces
  point in the position table.
- **The code card end is unclear.** Sources say "peel off the code card"
  first, and also place it behind the Energy. Confirm that it is at the
  back.
- **Mega Hyper Rare slot**: only one community source places it in the
  rare slot.
- **Collation** for every era product: Unknown.
- **Set codes**: the three-letter codes come from a search summary. The
  ME numbers come from TCGplayer and ThePriceDex naming. No official
  list was read.
- **30th Celebration** and **Delta Reign**: research after release.
