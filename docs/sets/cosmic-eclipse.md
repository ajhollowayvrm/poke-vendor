# Cosmic Eclipse (2019)

Set data for Cosmic Eclipse, the last main set of the Sun & Moon Series.
The era template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: November 1, 2019.
- Abbreviation: CEC. English set number: 82.
- Card count: 236 main set + 35 secret rares = 271.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a character rare instead of a reverse
  holo (Elite Fourum post 157).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); character
  rare slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or character rare) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no (rare slot
  card 7, character rare card 6). Pack-trick order: yes (rare slot last,
  character rare second to last).
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a character rare is a second hit in the reverse slot.**

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX (includes TAG
TEAM Pokémon-GX), Character Rare, Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.9 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 7.8 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Character Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 10 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 26.2 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 71.4 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 111.1 | Empirical study, weaker data |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 9.7 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 5.6 | Estimate |
| TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX (any version) | Unknown | Unknown |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 4.6 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.0 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.2 full art Trainers and secret rares. The
shared sheet has 32 cards (9 full art Trainers, 23 secret rares). Most
print 3 times, and 4 cards (assumed full art Trainers) print 4 times. In
this model, 28 cards are 1 in 1,000 packs each.

The author says the data "is not quite as good as for the earlier sets".
No large Derium opening exists for this set.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- **Character rares**: 12 full art cards of Pokémon drawn with a
  Trainer (Bulbapedia). They come in the reverse slot from their own
  11x11 sheet (Elite Fourum model).
- Reverses come from two sheets in turn, with the character rares
  inserted separately.
- Triple TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX and TAG TEAM Supporter cards (Bulbapedia).
- Kyogre and Groudon (theme deck exclusive holos) are short printed.
