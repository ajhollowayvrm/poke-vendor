# EX Holon Phantoms (2006)

Set-specific data for EX Holon Phantoms, the thirteenth set of the EX
Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and every
exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

EX Holon Phantoms continues the δ Delta Species mechanic and the Holon
Engine, and adds three Holon location cards: Holon Adventurer, Holon
Fossil, and Holon Lake (Bulbapedia).

## Rarities and hit odds

An Elite Fourum rarity-guide post gives a per-box shape for the
reverse holo and rare slots together: "11 holos and 1 Pokémon-ex or 10
holos, 1 Pokémon-ex and 1 Gold Star" per 36-pack box, plus 12
holofoil Energy cards in the reverse slot, "usually 2 of each kind."
**Confidence: community estimate.**

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Uncommon | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Rare | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo ex | ~1 per box (36 packs) | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo ☆ (pack-pullable: Gyarados ☆ δ, Mewtwo ☆, Pikachu ☆) | ~1 in 108 packs (about 1 in 3 boxes) | Community estimate |

- The ~1 in 108 packs Rare Holo ☆ figure is specific to this set,
  from the Elite Fourum rarity-guide post. It is lower than the era's
  general ~1 in 72 packs estimate ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#odds-by-set)).
  See Open topics.
- The same post suggests the Gyarados ☆ δ variant sits on a rarer
  holofoil sheet than Mewtwo ☆ and Pikachu ☆, calling it "possibly
  the rarest card so far" in the set. Confidence: community estimate,
  not a stated per-pack figure.

**Default for the simulation:** treat the rare slot as one card per
pack, drawn from Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, Rare Holo ☆, or Secret
Rare in that order of commonness, with Rare Holo ex at about 1 per box
and Rare Holo ☆ at about 1 in 108 packs. This default carries no other
set-specific confirmation for the Rare, Rare Holo, and Secret Rare
rows.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **δ Delta Species.** A Pokémon printed with a type other than its
  usual type. Not a separate print rarity: a δ card can be Rare, Rare
  Holo, Rare Holo ex, or Rare Holo ☆
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#rarity-system)).
- **Holon Engine and Holon location cards.** Holon Adventurer, Holon
  Fossil, and Holon Lake are Trainer cards that name the fictional
  Holon region (Bulbapedia). They sit in the normal Common, Uncommon,
  and Rare rarities, not a separate rarity
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#rarity-system)).
- **Reverse holo cards.** This set's reverse holo slot can also hold
  a holofoil Energy card, at "usually 2 of each kind" per box (Elite
  Fourum rarity guide).
- **Rare Holo ☆.** Gyarados ☆ δ, Mewtwo ☆, and Pikachu ☆.
- **Secret Rare.** Mew (111/110). A non-holo version came only with the
  "Lucario and the Mystery of Mew" DVD (Bulbapedia).
- **God packs:** no source found. Confidence: unknown.
