## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Bulbapedia gives 10 cards for the era. The
  ToyWiz box listing says "10 cards per pack". Confidence: official rule
  and retailer data.
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo or ACE SPEC, 1 rare slot, 3
  uncommons. Confidence: community (PokeBeach, December 16, 2013). The
  post names Plasma Storm.
- **1 code card.**
- **No basic Energy card.**

## Pack order

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common |
| 6 | Reverse holo, or ACE SPEC (a hit) |
| 7 | Rare slot (a hit) |
| 8–10 | Uncommon |
| 11 | Code card |

- **Where the hits sit:** the rare slot is card 7 of 10 in the listed
  order, or card 4 in reverse order. An ACE SPEC is card 6, or card 5 in
  reverse order. No hit is one of the last three cards.
- **Direction:** Unknown.
- **Confidence:** community. A 2013 PokeBeach post gives this order and
  names Plasma Storm. A reply confirms that ACE SPEC cards take the
  reverse holo slot. No photo or video confirms the order.
- One member pulled "2 RH from 1 pack" in a Plasma Storm box (February
  18, 2013). The post does not say what the two cards were.

## Special mechanics

- Plasma Storm introduced Team Plasma cards, with blue borders and a
  Team Plasma bar.
- Bulbapedia records a card order error: Whismur, Loudred, and Exploud
  come before Lugia.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

The TCGdex rarity `Rare` holds the plain Rares, the Rare Holos, the
regular print of each Pokémon-EX, and the 3 ACE SPEC cards. Cards 14,
18, 25, 48, 93, 95, 96, and 108 are the 8 Pokémon-EX; the 3 ACE SPECs
are the only Trainer cards in this rarity (confirmed by name against
the Rarities table above). The Reverse holo/ACE SPEC slot holds the
ACE SPEC outcome; the Rare slot excludes it with a Category filter.

Confidence: the slots follow the pack structure above. No source gives
odds for the Rare Holo outcome.
