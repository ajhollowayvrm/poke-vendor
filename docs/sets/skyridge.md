lost the licence. Bulbapedia says that this is why it had one print run.

How the 182 cards add up (Bulbapedia set list, PSA):

| Group | Count |
|---|---|
| Main set, 1/144 to 144/144 | 144 |
| Holo subset, H1/H32 to H32/H32 | 32 |
| Crystal cards: Celebi 145, Charizard 146, Crobat 147, Golem 148, Ho-Oh 149, Kabutops 150 | 6 |

Rarity counts from the Bulbapedia set list: 38 Rare Holo (32 H cards and
6 Crystal cards), 35 Rare, 36 Uncommon, 73 Common. Each H card also exists
as a non-holo rare in the main set, for example Alakazam H1 and 2/144.

## Pack structure

9 cards per pack: "one was a reverse holographic foil card, one a rare,
two were uncommons, and five were commons" (PSA).

The holo is an extra card (Model B). PSA says: "If there is a regular
holographic card in the pack, there will also be another rare card. In
these instances, the regular holographic card takes the place of one of
the common cards". This PSA sentence is the same as the one in its
Aquapolis article. Confidence: community estimate.

The reverse holo slot can hold any card except the H cards. The Crystal
cards also exist as reverse holos (Bulbapedia, Flipside Gaming). So a
Crystal card can come from the holo slot or from the reverse holo slot.

**Default for the rip screen:** card 6 is the rare. This breaks the game
default of the last three cards. Put the reverse holo and the extra holo
in the last three cards until a source says otherwise. Flag all positions
as low confidence.

## Rarities and hit odds

| Figure | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Non-holo rare per pack | 1 per pack (100%) | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Holo per pack | About 1 in 3 | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Holos in 36 packs (1 box) | 11 (about 31%) | Empirical study, very small (Loose Packs) |
| Crystal card per pack | About 1 in 12 to 1 in 18 | Community estimate (Flipside Gaming) |
| Crystal cards per box | 1 to 3 | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Crystal cards per box | "1-4 crystal with an average of 2-3" | Community anecdote (Elite Fourum forum post) |
| Reverse holo that is a Crystal card | About 5% of packs | Community estimate from sample data (Elite Fourum: "5% reverse Crystal Types, 25% reverse rares, 23% reverse uncommons and 47% reverse commons") |
| Reverse holo that is a rare | About 25% of packs | Same Elite Fourum sample |
| Rarest reverse holos ("RE2", 10 cards) | 1 in 220 packs each | Community estimate (Elite Fourum) |

The Elite Fourum guide suggests that "the four H2 cards are all Crystal
Types". So 4 of the 6 Crystal cards may be rarer than the other holos. The
guide does not name the 4 cards.

A pack can hold three hits: the rare, a holo, and a rare-rarity or Crystal
reverse holo.

Wizards of the Coast published no odds. PSA says that there "was no
official indication from WOTC as to how many Crystal cards were included
in each booster box".

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **H-numbered holos:** the second set with holos numbered H1 to H32 (PSA).
- **Crystal Pokémon:** 6 cards with the "Crystal Type" Poké-Body. All are
  holo secret rares. Unlike Aquapolis, they also exist as reverse holos
  (Bulbapedia, Flipside Gaming).
- **Mystery Plate:** a new mechanic in this set (Bulbapedia).
- **Crystal Shard:** a Pokémon Tool, uncommon, 122/144 (Bulbapedia).
- **Box toppers:** Charizard, Crobat, Ho-Oh, and Kabutops. They are cards
  09–12 of the 12-card e-Card box topper subset (PSA, Bulbapedia).
- **Wrappers:** Poliwrath, Vaporeon, Kabutops, and Ho-Oh (Bulbapedia).

## Rarity list

The stop rule menu on the rip screen shows this list (see
[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). The list goes from
the most common entry to the rarest entry. The rare slot always holds a
non-holo rare (Model B); a Rare Holo (H card) is an extra card, and a
Crystal card is rarer still. A Crystal card can also come from the
reverse holo slot, but it still counts under the Crystal entry.

| # | Entry | Type | Odds per pack | Default stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common | Rarity | Every pack | No |
| 2 | Uncommon | Rarity | Every pack | No |
| 3 | Rare | Rarity | Every pack | Yes |
| 4 | Reverse holo | Variant | Every pack | No |
| 5 | Rare Holo | Rarity | 1 in 3 | Yes |
| 6 | Crystal (secret rare) | Rarity | 1 in 12 to 1 in 18 | Yes |

## Open topics

- **Pack order.** One trick guide only. It puts the rare at card 6, outside
  the last three cards. The reverse holo and extra holo positions are
  Unknown.
- **Crystal odds.** Flipside (1 in 12 to 18 packs) and PSA (1 to 3 per box,
  so 1 in 12 to 36 packs) overlap only in part. Unknown whether Flipside
  counts reverse holo Crystal cards.
- **Which Crystal cards are rarer.** The Elite Fourum guide suggests 4 of 6
  are an "H2" tier. It does not name them.
- **Holo slot model.** PSA's Model B text for Skyridge copies its Aquapolis
  text. No second source confirms it for Skyridge.
- **Holo odds.** Loose Packs has only 1 box (11 holos).
- **PokéBeach threads.** PokéBeach blocked the fetch (HTTP 403).
- **Blister, case size, and theme deck contents.** Not found or not
  checked.
