# Ancient Origins (2015)

Set-specific data for XY—Ancient Origins. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: August 12, 2015 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: AOR. Bulbapedia also numbers it XY7.
- Card count: 98 cards plus 2 secret rares (100 total). Sources: the
  Bulbapedia expansion list, Limitless TCG (100), and Cardrake (98
  base cards and 2 secret rares).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons (ThePriceDex).

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
EX, Rare Ultra, and secret rares.

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Per specific card | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 1 in 19.5 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | 1 in 51.4 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 9 | 1 in 99 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 21.4 | 1 in 257.1 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 50 | 1 in 250 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | — | Derived from the rows above |

All figures come from ThePriceDex.

**Conflict.** Flipside Gaming: "By Ancient Origins, pull rates had
dropped to approximately 1:6 packs for EXs and 1:12 packs for URs."
It also gives 1:72 for secret rares. ThePriceDex gives 1 in 9 (EX), 1
in 21.4 (UR), and 1 in 50 (secret). Both are **community estimates**.

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 15.1 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 9.3 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 2.2 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 2.6 packs".

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Pokémon-EX, M Pokémon-EX, and Ancient Traits (Bulbapedia).
- Bulbapedia names "Shiny versions of Primal Kyogre-EX, Primal
  Groudon-EX, and Mega Rayquaza-EX".
- Hoopa appears in its Hoopa Unbound form (Bulbapedia).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**Rare Holo EX and Ultra Rare split one TCGdex rarity.** TCGdex records
both the regular Pokémon-EX cards and the full-art secret cards under
the single rarity "Ultra Rare". The Cards column splits them: the
full-art cards sit in a number block at the end of the Ultra Rare
range; every other Ultra Rare number is a regular Rare Holo EX.

Confidence: this map follows the pack template in
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#the-pack-template). No source shows a real
opened pack for this set; the slot contents are the era-wide community
guide.

| Slot | Count | Outcome | Rarity list entry | TCGdex rarity | Variant | Cards | Odds in slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare | Reverse holo | All | 100% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Secret Rare | Secret Rare | Secret Rare | Holo or Normal | All | 2% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Ultra Rare | Ultra Rare | Ultra Rare | Holo or Normal | Nos. 84–95 | 4.67% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo EX | Rare Holo EX | Ultra Rare | Holo or Reverse holo | Not nos. 84–95 | 11.11% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Rare | Holo | All | 15.62% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare | Rare | Rare | Normal | All | Rest |

## Open topics

- The EX, UR, and secret rare rates conflict between Flipside Gaming
  and ThePriceDex.
- Bulbapedia names 3 shiny cards, but the set has 2 secret rares. The
  card numbers of the 3 shiny cards are Unknown.
- No source shows a real opened Ancient Origins pack. The pack order
  is an era-wide community claim.
- Boxes per case: 6 (see [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sealed-products)).
  Collation is Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo EX, Ultra Rare, and Secret Rare use 1 in 9, 1 in 21.4, and 1 in 50 from ThePriceDex. Flipside Gaming gives about 1 in 6, 1 in 12, and 1 in 72 for the era.
