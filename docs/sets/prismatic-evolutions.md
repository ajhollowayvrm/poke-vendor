# Prismatic Evolutions (2025)

Set-specific data for Prismatic Evolutions. This is one file per set.
See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach and the
index. This set is a priority set for the first playable loop.
The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md). This file records the set
data and every exception.

Prismatic Evolutions is a **special expansion**. It changes the
Scarlet & Violet pack template in three ways: two pattern reverse
holos (Poké Ball and Master Ball), no Illustration Rare rarity, and no
booster box in the official product list.

## Pack structure

Official product text: each pack holds "10 cards and 1 Basic Energy"
(Pokémon Center Elite Trainer Box). The official Pokémon Support page
says every Scarlet & Violet pack also holds a code card. No fetched
source confirms the code card for this set.

| Slot | Count | What the slot can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common (assumed from the Scarlet & Violet template) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon (assumed from the Scarlet & Violet template) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Regular reverse holo, Poké Ball pattern, or ACE SPEC Rare |
| Reverse holo 2 | 1 | Regular reverse holo, Master Ball pattern, Special Illustration Rare, or Hyper Rare |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Double Rare, or Ultra Rare |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | 1 | Pokémon TCG Live code (not confirmed for this set) |

Confidence:
- **Official:** 10 cards plus 1 Basic Energy.
- **Secondary report (search extract of PokeBeach set guide and a
  forum thread):** the slot contents of the two reverse holo slots and
  the rare slot. PokeBeach blocks direct fetches.
- The search extract says: "If you're lucky, you'll pull a Poke Ball
  reverse holo in the first slot. Much rarer is a Master Ball reverse
  holo in the second slot. With this seeding it's possible to pull a
  Poke Ball and Master Ball from the same pack."

## Pack order

Confidence: **secondary report** for the slot names, plus the Scarlet
& Violet template order (PokeBeach 2023 extract). No source shows an
opened Prismatic Evolutions pack.

With the front card toward the player, the expected order is:

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 4 Commons |
| 5–7 | 3 Uncommons |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 (Poké Ball pattern or ACE SPEC if the pack has one) |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2 (Master Ball pattern, Special Illustration Rare, or Hyper Rare if the pack has one) |
| 10 | Rare slot (Double Rare or Ultra Rare if the pack has one) |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

Where the hit sits:
- **Rare-slot hit** (Double Rare, Ultra Rare): position 10 of 12, the
  third-last card. It **is** one of the last three cards.
- **Master Ball, Special Illustration Rare, Hyper Rare:** position 9
  of 12, the fourth-last card. It is **not** one of the last three
  cards.
- **Poké Ball pattern, ACE SPEC:** position 8 of 12, the fifth-last
  card. It is **not** one of the last three cards.
- A pack can have up to three hits: a Poké Ball at 8, a Master Ball at
  9, and a Double Rare at 10.
- If the rip screen skips the Energy and the code card, the three
  hit slots are the last three cards.

Exceptions and conflicts:
- **Energy and code card position:** the same conflict as the base
  Scarlet & Violet template. One extract puts the code card last.
  Another extract says the code card is the first card the player
  sees. TheGamer's account gives a resolution (one source, Community estimate): see
  [eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md#conflicts-in-the-template).
- **Is the order of slots 8 and 9 fixed?** The sources say "first
  slot" and "second slot". They do not say that "first" means the
  card nearer the front.

## Rarities and hit odds

The Pokémon Company does not publish odds. tcgtalk calls some figures
"semi-official", but it names no official document. PokeBeach
published a pull-rate article in January 2025 ("Special Illustration
Rares Twice as Easy to Pull"). Its figures were not readable.

Three data sets exist:
- **A:** TCGplayer study, 1,200 packs, January 2025, as reported by
  PokeBeach. Read through pullrates.gg. pullrates.gg notes that the
  sample "is below the preferred 8,000+".
- **B:** a fan data set from about 2,700 packs opened on Whatnot
  streams (Pokemon-Switch Stock on X). Read through Dot Esports and
  Joseph Writer Anderson.
- **C:** Miraj Trading. It gives no source or sample size.

| Rarity | A: TCGplayer 1,200 | B: 2,700 Whatnot | C: Miraj | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 | — | — | Empirical study, small sample |
| Ultra Rare (full-art Supporter) | 1 in 13 | 1 in 16 (6.2%) | 1 in 16 | Empirical study; sources conflict |
| ACE SPEC Rare | 1 in 21 | 1 in 29 (3.4%) | 1 in 21 (4.7%) | Empirical study; sources conflict |
| Master Ball pattern | — | 1 in 19 (5.2%) | 1 in 20 (5%) | Empirical study (B); community estimate (C) |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 45 | 1 in 53 (1.9%) | 1 in 45 (2.2%) | Empirical study; sources conflict |
| Hyper Rare | 1 in 167 | 1 in 152 (0.6%) | 1 in 180 (0.5%) | Empirical study; sources conflict |
| Poké Ball pattern | about 1 in 3 (D) | Unknown | Unknown | Empirical study (D) |
| Illustration Rare | Not in this set | — | — | — |

**D:** CardDeckr's copy of the same TCGplayer 1,200-pack study (column A) gives
"approximately one in three packs" for the Poké Ball pattern. The same
CardDeckr copy gives Master Ball as "approximately one in ten packs", which
conflicts with column B (1 in 19) and column C (1 in 20).

Specific-card odds:

| Card | B: 2,700 Whatnot | C: Miraj | Other | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specific Ultra Rare | 1 in 192 | 1 in 192 | — | Community estimate |
| Specific ACE SPEC | 1 in 174 | — | — | Community estimate |
| Specific Master Ball | 1 in 1,273 | 1 in 1,362 | 1 in 2,073 (tcgtalk) | Community estimate; conflict |
| Specific Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 1,696 | 1 in 1,440 | — | Community estimate; conflict |
| Specific Hyper Rare | 1 in 760 | 1 in 900 | — | Community estimate; conflict |

**Default for the simulation:** use column A for Double Rare, Ultra
Rare, ACE SPEC, Special Illustration Rare, and Hyper Rare. Use column B
for the Master Ball pattern (1 in 19). Use column D (about 1 in 3) for
the Poké Ball pattern. This value matches the 1 in 3 rate that Black
Bolt and White Flare report for the same pattern (see
[black-bolt.md](black-bolt.md)), which supports using it here too.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Poké Ball and Master Ball pattern reverse holos.** This is the
  first English set with them. The official expansion page says "the
  Poké Ball is rare, and the Master Ball is even rarer."
- **No Illustration Rares.** Every secret art card is a Special
  Illustration Rare, an Ultra Rare, or a Hyper Rare.
- **God packs:** tcgtalk says "God packs are confirmed to exist in
  English Prismatic Evolutions". It also says "No official odds are
  published for god packs". Its estimate is 1 in 1,500 packs
  (optimistic) to 1 in 4,000 packs (conservative). Confidence:
  community estimate. The extracts disagree on the contents: one says
  "all eight Eevee evolution SARs", another says "all 9 Eeveelution
  Special Illustration Rares".
- **No booster box.** The official expansion page lists no Booster
  Display Box. tcgtalk mentions "booster boxes (~36 packs)". That
  statement conflicts with the official product list. Treat the set
  as having no booster box.
- **Stellar Tera Pokémon ex:** Eevee and its evolutions appear as
  Stellar Tera Pokémon ex (Bulbapedia).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure above. The slot contents are a secondary report, and the Poké Ball pattern odds are a community-derived default (see Open topics). This set has no Illustration Rares.

  study (column A) gives about 1 in 3. This file now uses that figure. No
  source gives a specific-card figure for the Poké Ball pattern.
- **Pack order:** no source shows an opened pack. Confirm the positions
  of reverse holo 1, reverse holo 2, the rare slot, the Energy, and the
  code card.
- **Hits outside the last three cards:** the Poké Ball and Master Ball
  slots sit before the rare slot. Decide how the rip screen handles
  this.
- **Code card:** not confirmed for this set.
- **Common and Uncommon counts per pack:** assumed from the Scarlet &
  Violet template (4 and 3). No set-specific source.
- **ACE SPEC slot:** only a search extract puts it in reverse holo 1.
- **Pull-rate conflicts:** Ultra Rare (1 in 13 or 1 in 16), ACE SPEC
  (1 in 21 or 1 in 29), Special Illustration Rare (1 in 45 or 1 in 53),
  Hyper Rare (1 in 152, 1 in 167, or 1 in 180), and Master Ball pattern
  (1 in 10, 1 in 19, or 1 in 20).
- **PokeBeach pull-rate figures:** the article was not readable.
- **Regular reverse holo count:** 108 or 100.
- **God pack:** no official confirmation. The odds are a wide community
  range. The contents (8 or 9 cards) conflict.
- **Booster box:** tcgtalk mentions one. The official list has none.
- **Pack counts:** Binder Collection, Poster Collection, Accessory Pouch
  Special Collection, and Premium Figure Collection.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives odds for Rare. The era's rarity system decides its place in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** CardDeckr's about 1 in 3 rate for the Poké Ball
  pattern places it above Double Rare (1 in 5). This uses the TCGplayer
  1,200-pack study, the same source as the other odds in this file.
