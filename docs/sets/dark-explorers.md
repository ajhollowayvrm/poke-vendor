## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Confidence: official rule, via Bulbapedia.
  The Bulbapedia merchandise page also calls it "a standard Dark
  Explorers 10-card booster pack".
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons.
  Confidence: community template for the era. No source covers this set
  by name.
- **1 code card.**
- **No basic Energy card.**

## Pack order

The era template order applies. No source describes an opened Dark
Explorers pack.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common |
| 6 | Reverse holo |
| 7 | Rare slot (the hit) |
| 8–10 | Uncommon |
| 11 | Code card |

- **Where the hit sits:** card 7 of 10 in the listed order, or card 4 of
  10 in reverse order. The hit is **not** one of the last three cards.
- **Direction:** Unknown.
- **Confidence:** community, extrapolated. The 2013 order post names
  four later sets as examples, but not Dark Explorers.

## Special mechanics

- From this set on, secret cards can be Item cards (gold Pokémon
  Catcher).
- Dark Explorers was the first English set with a BW Black Star Promo
  as its prerelease promo.
- Bulbapedia records a card order error: Vanillite and Vanillish come
  before Ducklett and Swanna.

## Open topics

- **Pack order.** No source covers Dark Explorers by name. Searched
  2026-09-12: no photo, video, or named source found.
- **Pack direction.** Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: no source found.
- **Holo rare odds.** Only one box gives a holo count. Searched
  2026-09-12: no other source found.
- **Secret rate conflict.** Flipside gives ~1 in 2 boxes. A PokeBeach
  member gives ~1 in 3 boxes. Four box reports gave 2 secrets.
- **Box report sample.** Only four boxes, and one post is hard to split.
- **Case size.** Resolved. Potomac Distribution confirms 6 boxes per
  case and 36 packs per box.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare and Rare Holo. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare and Rare Holo. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the Rare or Rare Holo rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Secret uses 1 in 72, about 1 in 2 boxes, from Flipside Gaming. A PokeBeach member gives about 1 in 3 boxes, about 1 in 108 packs.
