# Dragon Vault (2012)

Set data for Dragon Vault, a special expansion of the Black & White era.
Dragon Vault does **not** use the era pack template. The template is in
[eras/black-white.md](eras/black-white.md). The general approach is in
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md).

## Pack structure

- **5 cards per pack.** Bulbapedia: "Each booster pack contained 5
  Holofoil cards." The official blurb says "ALL of them foil!—in special
  5-card booster packs". Confidence: official.
- **All 5 cards are holo.** Bulbapedia's merchandise page says the set
  "comprised 21 Tinsel Holofoil cards".
- **Slot breakdown: Unknown.** No source says if each pack has fixed
  slots, random cards, or the same 5 cards.
- **No reverse holo slot.** All cards are already holo.
- **Code card:** one code card per blister, not per pack. Source:
  Bulbapedia merchandise page.

## Pack order

- **Unknown.** No source describes an opened Dragon Vault pack.
- **Where the hit sits.** Every card in the pack has Rare Holo rarity in
  the pokemontcg.io data. So every card is a hit under the rule in
  [18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#what-counts-as-a-hit). The position of
  the secret Kyurem is Unknown.

## Rarities

Rarity data from pokemontcg.io.

| Rarity | Cards | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Rare Holo | 18 | Dratini to Druddigon (1–17), Super Rod (20) |
| No rarity in the data | 3 | Exp. Share (18), First Ticket (19), Kyurem (21) |

The set has no printed rarity split into common, uncommon, and rare in
any source I found. PokeBeach members in 2012 could not tell the
rarities either.

## Hit odds

| Hit | Odds | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Holo card | Every card | Official |
| Secret rare (Kyurem 21/20) | Unknown | Unknown |
| Any single named card | Unknown | Unknown |

Context only, not English data: one PokeBeach member opened one box of
the Japanese Dragon Selection (January 31, 2012). The three Trainer
cards came out 2, 2, and 1 times. Most Pokémon came out 3 to 6 times.
The member wrote that "trainers being the rarest of all" may be random
chance.

## Special mechanics

- Dragon Vault was the first English special expansion. Its packs were
  not sold in booster boxes.
- The official site first listed it as the "Dragon Vault Series", and
  later with the Black & White Series (Bulbapedia).
- All Pokémon in the set are Dragon type.

[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). Dragon Vault has no
booster box; it comes only in a 3-pack blister with 5 all-holo cards per
pack. Every card in a pack is Rare Holo, so this entry always matches
the stop rule.
