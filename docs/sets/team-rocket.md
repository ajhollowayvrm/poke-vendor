# Team Rocket (2000)

Set data for Team Rocket. The era template is in
[eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md](eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md). This file
records the Team Rocket values and the differences from the template.

## Release

- **English release date:** April 24, 2000.
- **Set symbol:** yes (Bulbapedia). Abbreviation: Unknown. ThePriceDex
  uses the database ID `base5`.
- **Card count:** 82 in the main set, plus 1 secret rare: Dark Raichu
  (83/82). Total 83.
- **1st Edition vs. Unlimited:** both exist. PSA says "more 1st Edition
  cards were printed for this series than there were of the 1999
  Pokémon 1st Edition (Base) series".

## Pack structure

- 11 cards per pack.
- 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons, 7 commons (PSA, ThePriceDex).
- Energy card: Unknown. ThePriceDex lists no energy row.

## Pack order

Team Rocket follows the era pack order. See
[eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md](eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md#pack-order).

- **Claim A** ([CardCollector](https://cardcollector.co.uk/pokemon-card-trick-pack-opening/)):
  "3" for Team Rocket. The rare is card 8 of 11, the fourth card from
  the end (our derivation). **Not** one of the last three.
  Confidence: low.
- **Claim B** ([NinePocket](https://www.ninepocket.net/guides/pokemon-pack-trick)):
  no reliable order for this era. Confidence: low.
- **Dark Raichu position:** Unknown. The Elite Fourum guide gives it
  the same rarity as the other holos. This suggests that it uses the
  rare slot, but no source says so directly. Searched 2026-09-12: no
  source found for whether Dark Raichu fills the rare slot or adds a
  12th card to the pack.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Dark Pokémon:** "Dark Pokémon cards have a brown and black artwork
  window, Evolution box and Pokédex box" (Bulbapedia).
- **Firsts:** the first holo Trainer and Energy cards, and the first
  secret card (Bulbapedia).
- **Dark Dragonite error:** the uncommon sheet held a "Dark Dragonite
  with the number 5/82 (the holo version's number)" in the place of
  Dark Vaporeon. The Elite Fourum guide says the factory corrected
  this during the print run. It says the fix differs between 1st
  Edition and Unlimited.
