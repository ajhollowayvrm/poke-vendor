## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Confidence: official rule, via Bulbapedia.
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo or ACE SPEC, 1 rare slot, 3
  uncommons. Confidence: community (PokeBeach, December 16, 2013). The
  post names Plasma Freeze.
- **1 code card.**
- **No basic Energy card.**

## Pack order

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common |
| 6 | Reverse holo, or ACE SPEC (a hit) |
| 7 | Rare slot (a hit) |
| 8–10 | Uncommon |
| 11 | Code card |

- **Where the hits sit:** the rare slot is card 7 of 10 in the listed
  order, or card 4 in reverse order. An ACE SPEC is card 6, or card 5 in
  reverse order. No hit is one of the last three cards.
- **Direction:** Unknown.
- **Confidence:** community. A 2013 PokeBeach post gives this order and
  names Plasma Freeze first. A reply confirms that ACE SPEC cards take
  the reverse holo slot. No photo or video confirms the order.

Averages: 2.1 regular EX, 1.6 Full Art, 1.2 ACE SPEC, and 0.24 secret
per box. Confidence: empirical, self-reported. This is the largest
sample in the era.

Observations from these reports:

- 15 of 17 boxes had exactly 2 regular EX.
- The Full Art count per box went from 0 to 3.

Other reports:

- One member opened 7 boxes. The boxes had 3, 0, 2, 2, 2, 1, and 1 Full
  Art (May 9, 2013). That is 11 Full Art in 7 boxes.
- A friend of that member opened 2 boxes with no Full Art.
- One member opened 2 boxes, and each box had a secret rare (May 10,
  2013).
- Members called the set "a nightmare" and "so damn inconsistent" for
  box rates. Confidence: community.

## Special mechanics

- Plasma Freeze continues the Team Plasma cards from Plasma Storm.
- Most Pokémon have shards of ice in their artwork (Bulbapedia).

## Open topics

- **Pack direction.** Unknown.
- **Pack order evidence.** One forum post. No photo or video source.
- **Regular EX per box.** 15 of 17 reports show exactly 2. No source says
  if boxes guarantee 2 regular EX.
- **Rate conflict.** Flipside gives 1 Full Art and 1 ACE SPEC per box.
  Box reports give 1.6 Full Art and 1.2 ACE SPEC per box.
- **Missing ACE SPEC data.** Five box reports do not list ACE SPEC cards.
- **Holo rare odds.** Only two boxes give holo counts.
- **Case size for this set.** No set-specific source.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Reverse holo, Rare and Rare Holo. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills the same slot as ACE SPEC, so no source gives its own rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Ultra (Full Art) and Rare ACE (ACE SPEC) tie at about 1 in 36. This file lists Full Art first, matching the era's rarity system order.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Ultra (Full Art) uses 1 in 36 from Flipside Gaming. Seventeen box reports average 1.6 per box, about 1 in 22 packs.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare ACE (ACE SPEC) uses 1 in 36 from Flipside Gaming. Twelve box reports average 1.2 per box, about 1 in 30 packs.
