# Surging Sparks (2024)

Set-specific data for Surging Sparks. This is one file per set. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach and the index.
This set is a priority set for the first playable loop.
The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md). This file records the set
data and every exception.

## Pack structure

Official product text: each pack holds "10 cards and 1 Basic Energy".
The pack also holds a Pokémon TCG Live code card. The physical pack
thus has 12 cards.

| Slot | Count | What the slot can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Reverse holo of a Common, Uncommon, or Rare |
| Reverse holo 2 | 1 | Reverse holo, or a hit: Illustration Rare or Special Illustration Rare |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, ACE SPEC Rare, or Hyper Rare (see conflict below) |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | 1 | Pokémon TCG Live code |

Confidence:
- **Official:** 10 cards plus 1 Basic Energy (Pokémon Center and
  pokemon.com product text). The official Pokémon Support page gives
  "4 commons, 3 uncommons, and 3 foils (at least one of which will be
  rare or higher)", plus 1 Energy card and 1 code card.
- **Secondary report of the official configuration:** PokeBeach
  (March 2023) describes the Scarlet & Violet template. It gives the
  two reverse holo slots, and says an Illustration Rare or Special
  Illustration Rare replaces the second reverse holo. PokeBeach blocks
  direct fetches, so this file uses search-result extracts of the
  article.
- **Conflict (Hyper Rare slot):** the PokeBeach extract puts Hyper Rares
  in the rare slot. The Card Shop Live study (1,728 packs of the base
  Scarlet & Violet set) counts Hyper Rares in the "reverse holo+"
  slot, with the Illustration Rares and Special Illustration Rares.
  No source for Surging Sparks settles this.
- **Unknown:** the slot for ACE SPEC Rares in Surging Sparks. No source
  confirms it. Put it in the rare slot until a source says otherwise.

## Pack order

Confidence: **secondary report** (PokeBeach 2023 extract) for the
Scarlet & Violet template. No source shows an opened Surging Sparks
pack.

The pack is "ordered with the rarest cards in the back". With the
front card toward the player, the order is:

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 4 Commons |
| 5–7 | 3 Uncommons |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2 (Illustration Rare or Special Illustration Rare if the pack has one) |
| 10 | Rare slot (Double Rare, Ultra Rare, ACE SPEC, Hyper Rare, or Rare) |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

Where the hit sits:
- **Rare-slot hit** (Double Rare, Ultra Rare, ACE SPEC, Hyper Rare):
  position 10 of 12. It is the third-last card. It **is** one of the
  last three cards.
- **Reverse holo 2 hit** (Illustration Rare, Special Illustration
  Rare): position 9 of 12. It is the fourth-last card. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. It is one of the last three cards
  only if the rip screen skips the Energy and the code card.
- A pack can have two hits: an Illustration Rare at 9 and a Double
  Rare at 10.

Exceptions and conflicts:
- **Energy and code card position:** the PokeBeach extract puts both
  cards after the rare. Another extract of the same search says "The
  first card that you will see is the redeem code card". The two
  statements agree only if the player opens the pack so that the
  back of the stack faces them. No source says which face of the
  stack faces the front of the wrapper.
- **Opening end:** no source says whether the order changes when a
  player tears the bottom of the wrapper.

figures come from a TCGplayer study. The TCGplayer article needs
JavaScript, so this file uses copies of its figures from PokéPatch
and pullrates.gg. pullrates.gg gives the sample as "~8,640 Surging
Sparks packs".

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Stellar Tera Pokémon ex:** 9 Stellar Tera Pokémon ex and 9
  Pokémon ex (Bulbapedia). This is a card mechanic. It does not
  change the pack.
- **ACE SPEC Rares:** 8 cards (Bulbapedia).
- **God packs:** tcgtalk says "No confirmed god pack mechanic has been
  documented". Social media posts claim "demi-god packs". No
  reliable source confirms them. Confidence: unknown.
- The set includes cards from the Japanese Super Electric Breaker and
  Paradise Dragona expansions (Bulbapedia).
