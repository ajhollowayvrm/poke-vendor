# Pitch Black (2026)

The fifth main set of the Mega Evolution era, and the newest released
set at the research date (2026-09-12). Data is thinner than for older
sets. One file per set (see [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared
era template is in [eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2026-07-17 (Bulbapedia, Pokemon TCG expansion
  page, TCGplayer).
- **Set code**: PBL. Product line code: ME05 (TCG Talk, PokéPatch).
- **Card count**: 84 main set + 36 secret rares = 120 cards (Bulbapedia).
  TCGplayer gives 84 main set cards. ThePriceDex gives 120 in total.
- TCG Talk gives 81 main set cards and numbers up to 118/081. This
  conflicts with Bulbapedia, TCGplayer, and ThePriceDex.
- **Rarity counts**:
  - Common, Uncommon, Rare: Unknown.
  - 10 Double Rare: 6 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 4 Pokémon ex
    (TCGplayer, Obsidia).
  - 18 Ultra Rare, 11 Illustration Rare, 6 Special Illustration Rare,
    1 Mega Hyper Rare (Bulbapedia, Obsidia). These add up to 36.
- The Mega Hyper Rare is Mega Darkrai ex.

## Pack structure

Official: "Each booster pack contains 10 cards, 1 Basic Energy, and
1 Pokémon TCG Live code card" (Pokemon TCG expansion page).

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Reverse holo 2 (can be IR or SIR) | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Rare slot (Rare, DR, UR, MHR) | 1 | Community source (TCG Talk, era template) |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Official |
| Code card | 1 | Official |

TCG Talk says that the rare slot "can contain regular Rare, ex, IR, UR,
SIR, or MHR". This conflicts with the era template, where IR and SIR use
the second reverse holo slot. TCG Talk also counts "shiny/holo energy"
at 5.6% per pack, as an "energy insert bonus". No other source confirms
holo Basic Energy in this set.

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide, from the era template**. No source
describes the pack order of this set directly.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three.
- If TCG Talk is correct that IR and SIR use the rare slot, then all
  hits are at position 10. This conflict is open.

## Rarities and hit odds

Main source: more than 4,000 opened packs, with 95% ranges (Obsidia).
Obsidia does not name who opened the packs.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Rare | 1 in 1.4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 (21.02% ±1.21%) | Empirical study (4,000+ packs) |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 12 (8.30% ±0.82%) | Empirical study (4,000+ packs) |
| Illustration Rare | 1 in 9 (11.01% ±0.93%) | Empirical study (4,000+ packs) |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 80 (1.25% ±0.33%) | Empirical study (4,000+ packs) |
| Mega Hyper Rare | 1 in 1,081 (0.09% ±0.09%) | Empirical study, very low confidence |
| Reverse holo common | 1 in 1.1 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo uncommon | 1 in 1.5 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo rare | 1 in 3.6 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |

The Mega Hyper Rare range (±0.09%) is as wide as the figure. The study
saw very few Mega Hyper Rares.

### Conflicting figures

- **ThePriceDex** (community estimate): Mega Hyper Rare 1 in 1,000,
  SIR 1 in 97, UR 1 in 13, IR 1 in 9, DR 1 in 5.
- **TCG Talk** (753 packs, about 21 boxes, community sample): ex 25.0%,
  IR 11.6%, UR 3.6%, SIR 0.8%, Mega Hyper Rare 0.13% (1 pull), Rare only
  53.4%. A 500-pack hunt in that sample found no Mega Darkrai ex SIR and
  no Mega Hyper Rare.
- The TCG Talk UR figure (3.6%) is less than half of the 4,000-pack
  figure (8.30%). The categories may differ.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **God packs**: TCG Talk says that an unverified all-gold pack photo
  appeared on Reddit. It also says that "no official god-pack mechanic
  is known for English Mega Evolution sets." Treat god packs as absent.
- **Holo Basic Energy**: TCG Talk reports "shiny/holo energy" at 5.6%.
  Unconfirmed.

## Open topics

- Pack order: no set-specific source. The file uses the era template.
- The IR and SIR slot: second reverse holo slot (era template) against
  the rare slot (TCG Talk).
- Card count conflict: 84 + 36 = 120 (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer, ThePriceDex)
  against 81 main and 118 total (TCG Talk).
- Common, Uncommon, and Rare card counts: Unknown.
- The source of the 4,000-pack study is not named.
- The Mega Hyper Rare odds rest on very few pulls.
- Holo Basic Energy at 5.6%: one unconfirmed source.
- Box collation and per-card weights: Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Illustration Rare (11.01%) is more common than Ultra Rare (8.30%), even though Ultra Rare is the higher secret-rare tier by name.
