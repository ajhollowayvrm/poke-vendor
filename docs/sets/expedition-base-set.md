## Pack structure

9 cards per pack. Packs went from 11 cards to 9 cards. PSA gives the reason
as the added cost of Dot-Code printing. The Elite Fourum rarity guide gives
the cost of the e-Reader licence.

The sources conflict on the holo slot:

- **Model A (PSA, Bulbapedia):** "5 Common, 2 Uncommon, 1 Reverse
  Holofoil, and 1 Rare or Rare Holo card". The holo is the rare.
- **Model B (Elite Fourum rarity guide):** "Every pack now contains a
  regular rare. Holos are still one in every three packs, and they take
  the place of a common." Average pack: "4 2/3 commons, 2 uncommons, 1
  rare, 1/3 holos and 1 reverse".

**Default for the simulation:** Model B, the same as the era default. See
[eras/e-card.md](eras/e-card.md#where-the-holo-goes-a-conflict).
Confidence: community estimate.

The reverse holo slot can hold a card of any rarity from 1–159 (PSA).

## Pack order

**Confidence: community estimate, low. Sources conflict.**

| Source | Cards to move (N) | Rare position (of 9) | In the last three? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PokéPatch | 2 | 7 | Yes |
| Card Collector | 3 | 6 | No (fourth from the end) |

- PokéPatch says that after the trick, "your 2nd to last card will
  generally be a reverse holo". With N = 2, the reverse holo is card 6.
- The position of the extra holo under Model B is Unknown.
- The rare positions come from our reading of the trick guides. See
  [eras/e-card.md](eras/e-card.md#pack-order).
- No source shows a real opened pack in order.

**Default for the rip screen:** card 6 is the reverse holo. Card 7 is the
rare. Put the extra holo in the last three cards until a source says
otherwise.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **e-Reader Dot Codes:** cards carry a Dot Code strip for the Nintendo
  e-Reader. A player can see Pokédex data, play a minigame, play tunes,
  or unlock a hidden attack. The hidden attacks are not legal for
  tournament play (Bulbapedia).
- **New reverse holo pattern:** a plain "refractor" foil, not the
  Legendary Collection fireworks pattern (Bulbapedia, PSA).
- **Box toppers:** Alakazam, Feraligatr, Tyranitar, and Venusaur. They are
  cards 01–04 of a 12-card e-Card box topper subset (PSA, Bulbapedia).
- **Promos outside packs:** Pichu (58/165) and Hoppip (112/165) with
  Japanese card backs, from the E3 Convention 2002 (Bulbapedia).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**This map uses Model B, the era default** (see
[eras/e-card.md](eras/e-card.md#where-the-holo-goes-a-conflict)): every
pack has a non-holo Rare in the rare slot, and a Rare Holo is an extra
card that takes the place of a common in about 1 pack in 3. The
"Common/Holo" slot below is the one common draw that can be replaced
this way; the other 4 commons are fixed.

Confidence: this map follows the era's Model B default and the pack
structure above (5 Common, 2 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 Rare).

| Slot | Count | Outcome | Rarity list entry | TCGdex rarity | Variant | Cards | Odds in slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Common/Holo | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Holo Rare | Holo | All | 1 in 3 |
| Common/Holo | 1 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | Rest |
| Uncommon | 2 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare | Rare | Rare | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | All | 100% |

## Open topics

- **Holo slot model.** PSA and Bulbapedia say the holo is the rare. The
  Elite Fourum guide says the holo replaces a common. Searched 2026-09-12:
  no source found that settles it.
- **Pack order.** The trick guides conflict (N = 2 or 3). If N = 3, the
  rare is not in the last three cards. Searched 2026-09-12: NinePocket
  says the whole era, including this set, has "no reliable trick". It
  gives no N value.
- **Holo odds.** No English box counts found. A PokéBeach pull-rate thread
  is not checked, because PokéBeach blocked the fetch (HTTP 403). Searched
  again 2026-09-12: still HTTP 403.
- **Reverse holo rarity split.** Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found.
- **Blister contents.** Only retail listings in search summaries. Unknown
  whether blisters held a promo or a coin.
- **Case size and theme deck contents.** Not found or not checked.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found for case size.
- **Wrapper designs.** Unknown count and art.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare ranks with Common and Uncommon at every pack. Model B guarantees a non-holo Rare in every pack; Rare Holo is a separate, rarer card.
