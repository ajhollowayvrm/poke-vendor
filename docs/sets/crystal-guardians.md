EX Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are
in [eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and every
exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

## Pack structure

Follows the era's pack template ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#pack-contents)):
9 cards per pack, 36 packs per box (Bulbapedia and a web search both
confirm 36 packs per box for this set). **Confidence: community
estimate** for the common and uncommon split; the era's two
conflicting source counts (5/2 vs. 4/3) both remain open for this set.

## Rarities and hit odds

An Elite Fourum rarity-guide post gives two per-box samples for this
set. **Confidence: community estimate**, and the post itself flags this
set as unusually inconsistent.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Uncommon | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Rare | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo ex | About 2.4 to 3 per box (36 packs), across two samples | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo ☆ | 1 in 141.4 packs, per one sample; 0.57 or 0.17 per box across two samples | Community estimate |

- Sample 1 (the post's author): 8.43 holos per box, 2.43 Rare Holo ex
  per box, 0.57 Rare Holo ☆ per box.
- Sample 2 ("TheDarkTwins," 24 boxes): 3.04 Rare Holo ex per box, 0.17
  Rare Holo ☆ per box.
- The post states "the number of Pokémon-ex per box varies
  considerably more than for other sets," and proposes the holofoil
  sheets insert unevenly. It calls per-pack prediction unreliable for
  this set specifically.
- The Rare Holo ☆ figures conflict with the era's general ~1 in 72
  packs estimate ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#odds-by-set)). See Open
  topics.

**Default for the simulation:** treat the rare slot as one card per
pack, drawn from Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, or Rare Holo ☆ in that
order of commonness, with Rare Holo ex at about 1 in 13 packs (2.43 per
36-pack box) and Rare Holo ☆ at about 1 in 141 packs. This default
carries a stated warning from its own source that this set's real
insertion is unusually uneven.

  records both and does not pick a position.
- **Rare Holo ☆.** Alakazam ☆ and Celebi ☆, both inside the printed
  100-card count, at 99/100 and 100/100
