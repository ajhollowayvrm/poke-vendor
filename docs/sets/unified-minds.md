# Unified Minds (2019)

Set data for Unified Minds. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: August 2, 2019.
- Abbreviation: UNM. English set number: 81.
- Card count: 236 main set + 22 secret rares = 258.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- No Prism Star cards (Elite Fourum post 140 names Ultra Prism to Team
  Up only). The reverse slot holds only reverse holos.
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX (includes TAG
TEAM Pokémon-GX), Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 7.2 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 7.8 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 22.4 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 76.9 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 111.1 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 9.3 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 5.2 | Estimate |
| TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX (any version) | Unknown | Unknown |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 4.6 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.1 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.3 full art Trainers and secret rares. The GX
ratio is higher because the set has 17 "regular" Pokémon-GX (Unbroken
Bonds has 14). On the shared sheet, each of the 22 secret rares prints 3
times, 2 full art Trainers print 5 times, and 4 print 6 times. That
gives 0.86 secret rares per box. Data includes Derium (1,080 packs) and
TheGameCapital (2,592 packs).

## Special subsets or mechanics

- TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX continue.
- Dragonite and Necrozma (theme deck exclusive holos) are short printed.
- The 23 uncommons at the "U1" weight are Trainers and Special Energy
  cards. Confidence: empirical study.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure and the ThePriceDex
odds above. The card list holds no Basic Energy card, so the Basic
Energy slot does not draw from it. TCGdex records Rare Holo GX (this
includes TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX) and Ultra Rare (full art) under one
rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare and Secret Rare under one rarity,
Secret Rare. The card numbers tell the four outcomes apart: Rare Holo
GX is the first appearance of each GX card, Ultra Rare is the full art
or alternate art repeat, Rainbow Rare is the next repeat, and Secret
Rare is the trailing Trainer cards. The Rare outcome draws from the
Rare rarity's Normal variant, all 59 cards; 21 of those also carry a
Holo variant, used by the Rare Holo outcome.
