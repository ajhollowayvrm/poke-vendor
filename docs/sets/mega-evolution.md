# Mega Evolution (2025)

The base set of the Mega Evolution era. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2025-09-26 in most regions. 2025-10-10 in Europe,
  the Middle East, and Africa (TCGplayer, Bulbapedia).
- **Set code**: MEG. Product line code: ME01 (TCGplayer).
- **Card count**: 132 main set + 56 secret rares = 188 cards.
- **Rarity counts** (TCGplayer Quick Facts): 67 Common, 43 Uncommon,
  12 Rare, 10 Double Rare, 22 Ultra Rare, 22 Illustration Rare,
  10 Special Illustration Rare, 2 Mega Hyper Rare.
- The TCGplayer article gives a total of 198. Its own rarity counts add
  up to 188, which agrees with Bulbapedia. Use 188.
- **Reverse holos**: 132, one for each Common, Uncommon, Rare, and
  Double Rare (search-result summary; community source).
- Most cards come from the Japanese sets Mega Brave and Mega Symphonia
  (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

"10 cards, 1 Basic Energy, and 1 Pokémon TCG Live code card" (official
wording on era product pages; retailer listings repeat it for this set).

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Community guide |
| Reverse holo 2 (can be IR or SIR) | 1 | Community guide (era template) |
| Rare slot (Rare, DR, UR, MHR) | 1 | Community guide |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Official |
| Code card | 1 | Official |

**Configuration change that did not happen**: in July 2025, the booster
box fine print said that each pack holds an Energy **or** a code card.
TPCi then said that this was incorrect. Packs hold both (PokeBeach).

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide** (NinePocket describes this set).

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three
  cards, because the Energy and the code card come after the rare.
- NinePocket: "the rare sits at the back, behind only a basic Energy and
  the code card. Peel off the code card, move the one Energy to the
  front, and your last card is the hit."
- No source says that a pack can open from either end. No source shows
  a recorded opening card by card.

## Rarities and hit odds

Main source: TCGplayer, from more than 5,000 opened packs, as reported
by PokeBeach and ThePriceDex.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Rare | 1 in 1.4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 | Empirical study (TCGplayer, 5,000+ packs) |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 12 | Empirical study (TCGplayer) |
| Illustration Rare | 1 in 9 | Empirical study (TCGplayer) |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 101 | Empirical study (TCGplayer) |
| Mega Hyper Rare | 1 in 1,260 | Empirical study (TCGplayer) |
| Any secret rare | 19% | Empirical study (PokeBeach from TCGplayer data) |
| Reverse holo common | 1 in 1.0 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo uncommon | 1 in 1.5 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo rare | 1 in 5.4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |

- **Mega Hyper Rare** is new in this set. It is the gold card rarity of
  the era. The set has 2. PokeBeach says that it is "7x rarer than the
  rarest gold cards in the Scarlet & Violet series."
- PokeBeach: about 35 booster boxes for one Mega Hyper Rare.
- No per-card weighting data. ThePriceDex assumes equal weight inside
  each rarity.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Mega Evolution Pokémon ex**: 10 cards, black-and-yellow ex symbol,
  worth 3 Prize cards (Bulbapedia).
- **Mirror Holofoil** variants return from the Black & White Series
  (Bulbapedia).
- **Full-art Ultra Rare Item, Pokémon Tool, and Stadium cards** first
  appear in this set (Bulbapedia).
- **God packs**: no source reports god packs for this set. Unknown.

## Open topics

- The TCGplayer total of 198 conflicts with its own rarity counts (188).
- The pack order has only a community-guide source, with no recorded
  opening. The face direction and the code card end need confirmation.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found.
- The Mega Hyper Rare slot (rare slot or not) has no direct source for
  this set. Searched 2026-09-12: no source found beyond the era-wide
  community estimate.
- Box collation: Unknown.
- Reverse holo odds are estimates, not study data.
- The mix of the 2 Mega Hyper Rares, and per-card weights: Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Illustration Rare (1 in 9) is more common than Ultra Rare (1 in 12), even though Ultra Rare is the higher secret-rare tier by name.
