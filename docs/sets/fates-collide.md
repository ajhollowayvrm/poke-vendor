# Fates Collide (2016)

Set-specific data for XY—Fates Collide. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: May 2, 2016 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: FCO.
- Card count: 124 cards plus 1 secret rare (125/124). Total 125.
- Japanese counterpart: Awakening Psychic King (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons (ThePriceDex).
- **The BREAK card replaces the reverse holo, not the rare slot.** An
  Elite Fourum thread states BREAK cards "took the place of the
  reverse holo". Confidence: **community forum report**.

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common through Rare Holo, Rare Ultra, Rare
Secret, plus Pokémon BREAK.

| Rarity | Per pack | Per box (36) | Confidence |
|--------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 24 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.9 | 5.2 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 8.2 | 4.4 | Community estimate |
| Rare BREAK | 1 in 15 | 2.4 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 17.0 | 2.1 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 125 | 0.3 | Community estimate |

All figures come from ThePriceDex.

**Resolved.** Rare BREAK sits in the reverse holo slot, not the rare
slot (Elite Fourum; see [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sets-that-break-the-template)).
Without Rare BREAK, the remaining rare-slot rows add up to about 1.00
card per pack: Rare (0.667) + Rare Holo (0.145) + Rare Holo EX (0.122)
+ Ultra Rare (0.059) + Secret Rare (0.008).

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 20.8 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 5.2 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 2.3 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 4.0 packs".

Flipside Gaming gives BREAK at "around 1:12 booster packs" and secret
rares at 1:72 for the era (**community estimate**).

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Pokémon-EX, M Pokémon-EX, and Pokémon BREAK (Bulbapedia).
- Zygarde in multiple forms (Bulbapedia).
- Notable cards include Mega Alakazam-EX, Lugia BREAK, Mega
  Altaria-EX, and Umbreon-EX (Bulbapedia).
