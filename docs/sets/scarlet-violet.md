# Scarlet & Violet (2023)

Set data for Scarlet & Violet, the first English set of the Scarlet &
Violet era. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md). This file records the set
data and every exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

## Release

- **English release:** March 31, 2023.
- **Set code:** SVI.
- **Card count:** 258 cards. The main set has 198 cards. The secret rares are
  60 cards, numbered 199/198 to 258/198.

| Part | Rarity | Cards |
|---|---|---|
| Main set | Common | 105 |
| Main set | Uncommon | 60 |
| Main set | Rare | 21 |
| Main set | Double Rare | 12 |
| Secret rares | Ultra Rare | 20 |
| Secret rares | Illustration Rare | 24 |
| Secret rares | Special Illustration Rare | 10 |
| Secret rares | Hyper Rare | 6 |

The counts come from the Bulbapedia set list. TCGplayer says the set has 12
Special Illustration Rares. See Open topics.

## Pack structure

12 cards per pack: 10 set cards, 1 Basic Energy card, and 1 code card.

| Slot | Count | Can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Common |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon |
| Reverse holo slot 1 | 1 | Reverse Holo |
| Reverse holo slot 2 | 1 | Reverse Holo, Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, Hyper Rare |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy card |
| Code card | 1 | Pokémon TCG Live code card |

**Confidence:** PokeBeach reported the structure before release. Card Shop
Live confirmed the same 12 cards and slots in 1,728 opened packs. Bulbapedia
shows the same table.

## Pack order

| Position | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Common |
| 5–7 | Uncommon |
| 8 | Reverse holo slot 1 (Reverse Holo only) |
| 9 | Reverse holo slot 2 (Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, Hyper Rare) |
| 10 | Rare slot (Double Rare, Ultra Rare) |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

**Confidence: industry news report before release** (PokeBeach, March 7,
2023). No source in this research shows an opened pack of this set card by
card.

- **Double Rare and Ultra Rare:** card 10, the third card from the end. It is
  one of the last three cards.
- **Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, and Hyper Rare:** card 9,
  the fourth card from the end. It is not one of the last three cards. If the
  rip screen hides the code card, card 9 is the third card from the end.
- **Exceptions for this set:** none found beyond the era conflicts.
- **Conflict:** PokeBeach puts the Hyper Rare in the Rare slot. TCGplayer and
  Card Shop Live put it in reverse holo slot 2. This file uses reverse holo
  slot 2.
- **Conflict:** PokeBeach puts the code card last. TCGplayer (151 article)
  says the Basic Energy is the last card in every Scarlet & Violet Series pack.

## Rarities and hit odds

Primary source: the TCGplayer Authentication Center opened more than 8,000
packs. The ranges are 95% confidence intervals. The specific-card figures
assume that each card of a rarity has the same pull rate.

Other figures:

- **Double Rare or better:** "around 30%" of packs (TCGplayer). Empirical
  study.
- **Any Ultra Rare, Illustration Rare, Special Illustration Rare, or Hyper
  Rare:** about 18% of packs (TCGplayer, Paldea Evolved article). Empirical
  study.
- **Equal secret rare rates:** TCGplayer found that all secret rares had
  overlapping confidence intervals between 0.27% and 0.36%. It suggests the
  same rate of about 0.315% for each. TCGplayer says: "The data can't confirm
  that conclusion". Community estimate.

Second study, Card Shop Live (retailer, 1,728 opened packs):

| Rarity | Hits | Per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double Rare | 234 | 13.54% | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare | 112 | 6.48% | Empirical study |
| Illustration Rare | 130 | 7.52% | Empirical study |
| Special Illustration Rare | 52 | 3.01% | Empirical study |
| Hyper Rare | 32 | 1.85% | Empirical study |

The two studies agree within the TCGplayer confidence intervals.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- This set introduced the Scarlet & Violet rarity system. All cards of Rare
  rarity or higher are holofoil.
- PokeBeach reports that Rainbow Rares are gone from this era.
- No ACE SPEC Rare and no shiny subset.
- No source in this research reports a god pack or a demi-god pack for this
  set.

  1 Scarlet & Violet pack.
- Cyclizar ex Box: 4 packs, usually 2 Scarlet & Violet.
- Paldea Legends Tins: 5 or 4 packs, usually 1 Scarlet & Violet.

Collation:

- Boxes per case: 6 (era-wide retailer consensus; see
  [eras/scarlet-violet.md](eras/scarlet-violet.md#sealed-product-template)).
- Hits per booster box: Unknown. No source says whether the hit count per box
  is fixed or variable.

## Open topics

- **Special Illustration Rare count.** Bulbapedia lists 10. TCGplayer says 12
  ("Six are Pokémon ex ... the other six are Trainers"). With 10 cards, the
  TCGplayer rarity counts sum to 60, the secret rare total. The specific-card
  odds for this rarity assume 12 cards.
- **Hyper Rare slot.** PokeBeach says the Rare slot. TCGplayer, Card Shop
  Live, and Bulbapedia say reverse holo slot 2.
- **Last card.** Code card (PokeBeach) or Basic Energy (TCGplayer)?
- **Physical pack order.** No opened-pack source confirms it for this set.
- **Boxes per case:** 6 (era-wide retailer consensus, not official).
- **Hits per box:** Unknown.
- **Build & Battle Stadium.** The merchandise page says 3 extra packs. The
  Build & Battle Box page says 4.
- **Blister contents** for this set: Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Illustration Rare (7.67%) is more common than Ultra Rare (6.57%), even though Ultra Rare is the higher secret-rare tier by name.
