# Phantasmal Flames (2025)

The second main set of the Mega Evolution era. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2025-11-14 (Bulbapedia, Cardrake).
- **Set code**: PFL. Product line code: ME02 (Cardrake, ThePriceDex).
- **Card count**: 94 main set + 36 secret rares = 130 cards (Bulbapedia).
- **Rarity counts** (Cardrake): 43 Common, 31 Uncommon, 10 Rare,
  10 Double Rare, 17 Ultra Rare, 13 Illustration Rare, 5 Special
  Illustration Rare, 1 Mega Hyper Rare. These add up to 130.
- Cardrake also shows "214" as a total. This number probably includes
  reverse holo variants. The rarity counts agree with Bulbapedia.
- The Mega Hyper Rare is Mega Charizard X ex.

## Pack structure

"10 trading cards, 1 Basic Energy card, 1 Pokémon TCG Live code"

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide, from the era template**. No source
describes the pack order of this set directly.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three.
- The last two cards are the Basic Energy and the code card.

## Rarities and hit odds

Main source: TCGplayer, from more than 5,000 opened packs (PokeBeach
summary). TCG Protectors merges that data with other openings into more
than 10,000 packs, and gives 95% ranges.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Rare | 1 in 1.4 (ThePriceDex); ~30% (TCG Protectors) | Community estimate, sources conflict |
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 (20.1% ±1.2%) | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 12 (8.06% ±0.75%) | Empirical study |
| Illustration Rare | 1 in 9 (10.97% ±0.86%) | Empirical study |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 80 (1.25% ±0.22%) | Empirical study |
| Mega Hyper Rare | 1 in 1,260 (0.079% ±0.03%) | Empirical study |
| Mega Charizard X ex SIR (one card) | 1 in 400 | Empirical study (derived from the SIR rate) |
| Reverse holo common | 1 in 1.0 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo uncommon | 1 in 1.4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo rare | 1 in 4.5 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |

The Rare figures conflict. ThePriceDex gives 1 in 1.4 (about 71%).
TCG Protectors gives about 30%. The rare slot must hold a card in every
pack, so a non-hit Rare near 60-70% is more consistent. Treat both
figures as low confidence.
