# Stormfront (2008)

Set data for Diamond & Pearl—Stormfront. This set uses the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: November 5, 2008. Japanese release: July 10, 2008, as
  Intense Fight in the Destroyed Sky.
- Set number: DP7. Abbreviation: SF.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 100. Secret cards: 3 (101/100 to 103/100). Shiny cards: 3
  (SH1 to SH3).
- Last set of the Diamond & Pearl Series.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 rare slot. Source:
  Bulbapedia (era template). DA Card World: "36 packs per box, 10 cards per
  pack".
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, a Rare Holo LV.X, or a secret
  card. PokeBeach Card Ratios says the reprints (secret cards) come "as a
  Rare". Confidence: community estimate.
- **Exception:** the SH cards come in the reverse holo slot. PokeBeach Card
  Ratios: "Comes as a Reverse Holo". Bulbapedia: the English SH print "was
  Reverse Holofoil". Confidence: community estimate, with Bulbapedia
  support.

## Pack order

The era template applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period. The
  two sources conflict.
- Secret cards and LV.X cards sit in the rare slot position.
- **Exception:** an SH card is a hit in the reverse holo slot. Its position
  is Unknown. It can be outside the last three cards.
- The common and uncommon order: Unknown. No code card. No separate Energy
  slot. Which end the cards face: Unknown.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Retro cards:** Bulbapedia says Stormfront introduced retro cards. These
  are cards from Base Set, Jungle, and Wizards Black Star Promos with new
  art. They are secret cards.
- **Shiny Pokémon (SH):** they return as a subset numbered SH#. The English
  print is reverse holofoil. Flipside Gaming says their official rarity was
  "Rare Holo Lv. X", changed to "Rare" in Platinum. This conflicts with the
  Bulbapedia label (Rare Holo).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**The Shiny (SH) cards share the "Rare Holo LV.X" TCGdex rarity with
the LV.X cards.** The Cards column splits them: SH1–SH3 use the
"Reverse holo" variant and sit in the reverse holo slot; Nos. 96–100
use the "Holo" variant and sit in the rare slot.

**The 3 secret cards (101–103) share the "Rare" TCGdex rarity with the
non-secret Rares.** They use the "Holo" variant; every non-secret Rare
uses "Normal".

Confidence: this map follows the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md), with
the set's own exception that Shiny (SH) cards come in the reverse holo
slot.

| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |

## Open topics

- Pack order: the two community guides conflict. No source shows real
  opened packs. The SH card position (reverse holo slot) is Unknown.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found (era-wide search, see the era
  file).
- SH cards per box: 1 or 2. The sources conflict. Searched 2026-09-12: a
  PokeBeach box-ratio post repeats "2-1-1" (2 Shiny). The conflict stands;
  the simulation default is 2.
- LV.X per box: 2 or 3. Most reports say 2.
- The name of SH1. A forum post mentions a shiny Drifloon. No source
  confirms the number.
- The official rarity label of SH cards: Bulbapedia and Flipside Gaming
  conflict.
- Whether a box has a fixed LV.X and secret card count. Reports suggest
  partial collation, but no study confirms it.
- The non-holo rare rate. Boxes per case. Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo LV.X uses 1 in 18. PokeBeach Card Ratios' first post gives 1 in 12 (3 per box).
- **Rarity list order:** Shiny (SH) uses 1 in 18 (2 per box), the simulation default from PokeBeach news and a second "2-1-1" report. PokeBeach Card Ratios gives 1 in 36. Shiny (SH) ties with Rare Holo LV.X, so the rarity comes first.
