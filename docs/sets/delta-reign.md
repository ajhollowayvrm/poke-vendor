# Delta Reign (2026)

The sixth main set of the Mega Evolution era. The research date
(2026-11-05) is one day before the English release, so data is thin.
No large pull rate study exists yet. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2026-11-06 (TPCi press release 2026-08-20,
  Pokemon.com, Bulbapedia, TCGplayer).
- **Set code**: DLR. Product line code: ME06 (TCGplayer, TCG Talk).
- The set is the English version of the Japanese set Storm Emeralda
  (M6). It also holds cards from the Japanese ex Starter Sets 2026
  (Bulbapedia, TCG Talk).
- **Card count**: 103 main set + 38 secret rares = 141 cards
  (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer group 24831, TCG Talk). TPCi says "more than
  135 cards".
- **Rarity counts** (TCGplayer, 2026-11-05): 52 Common, 31 Uncommon,
  10 Rare, 10 Double Rare, 17 Ultra Rare, 14 Illustration Rare,
  6 Special Illustration Rare, 1 Mega Hyper Rare. These add up to 141.
- **Rarity counts** (Bulbapedia raw wiki text, 2026-11-05): 50 Common,
  33 Uncommon. The other counts are the same as TCGplayer. Two cards
  cause the difference: Kilowattrel (041/103) and Darkrai (063/103).
  TCGplayer gives Common, and Bulbapedia gives Uncommon. See Open topics.
- The 10 Double Rare are 4 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 6 Pokémon ex
  (TPCi press release). The Mega Evolution Pokémon ex are Mega
  Golisopod ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and Mega Rayquaza ex.
- The Mega Hyper Rare is Mega Rayquaza ex (141/103).

## Pack structure

No official source gives the pack contents for this set at the
research date. The TPCi expansion page does not list them. This file
uses the era template.

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (era template) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (era template) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Reverse holo 2 (can be IR or SIR) | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Rare slot (Rare, DR, UR, MHR) | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Era template (official for Pitch Black) |
| Code card | 1 | Era template (official for Pitch Black) |

TCG Talk says that "each pack's rare slot determines the hit", and it
lists IR and SIR in the rare slot. This conflicts with the era
template, where IR and SIR use the second reverse holo slot. TCG Talk
made the same claim for Pitch Black. Default: the second reverse holo
slot, per the era template.

### Legendary Stadiums in the pack

TPCi: "Booster packs containing a Legendary Stadium include both halves
required to form this new feature" (press release, 2026-08-20). A
Legendary Stadium is a pair of Stadium cards with one artwork across
the two cards. The set has 3 pairs, 6 cards: 094–099. All 6 are
Uncommon.

- No source says which slots the two halves use.
- TCG Talk counts a pack with "a cosmetic 'connecting art' Legendary
  Stadium half" in the rare slot as a pack with no hit. This can mean
  that a half takes the rare slot. It can also mean only that the pack
  had a half somewhere. The text does not say.
- Default for the game: the halves are in the Uncommon slot. When the
  game draws one half, the next Uncommon card must be the other half of
  the same pair. The slot map cannot show this rule (see Slot map).

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide, from the era template**. No source
describes the pack order of this set directly.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three.
- The two halves of a Legendary Stadium sit next to each other in the
  Uncommon positions. This is the game default, not a sourced fact.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Legendary Stadiums** (new mechanic): 3 pairs of Stadium cards,
  played as a pair. The pairs are Legendary Pyroduct (094, 095),
  Legendary Summit (096, 097), and Legendary Trench (098, 099). A pack
  with one half has both halves (TPCi). See Pack structure.
- **Build & Battle Box exclusives**: Non Holo Magmortar (021/103) and
  Kommo-o (075/103). In booster packs these two cards are Rare holo
  (Bulbapedia).
- **"What's Your Favorite?" stamped promos**: holo Kyogre (026/103),
  Giratina (045/103), and Groudon (053/103), as retailer gifts with
  purchase (Bulbapedia). Not from booster packs.
- **God packs**: no source reports a god pack for this set. Treat god
  packs as absent, as for the other main sets of the era.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack template in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md#the-pack-template). The
slot contents are a community guide, not confirmed for this set. The
odds are the Pitch Black odds (see Rarities and hit odds).

The map does not show the Legendary Stadium rule. The Uncommon row
includes the 6 halves (094–099). `tools/export/rip_set.py`
(`PAIRED_CARDS`) writes the pairs to the set data, and the game applies
the rule in `PackBuilder.swift`. When the Uncommon slot draws one half,
the slot takes the other half as its next card, with the left half
first. The last Uncommon card is never a half, so a pack never holds a
lone half. With the map's equal weight for each Uncommon, about 35% of
packs hold a pair. No source gives the real rate.

## Open topics

- **Odds**: every hit row uses the Pitch Black odds. Replace them when
  a large English study exists. Searched 2026-11-05: only the TCG Talk
  152-pack sample exists.
- **Card list source**: TCGdex does not have this set at the research
  date. The card list comes from TCGCSV. Rebuild it from TCGdex when
  TCGdex adds the set.
- **Rarity conflict**: Kilowattrel (041/103) and Darkrai (063/103).
  TCGplayer gives Common, and Bulbapedia gives Uncommon. The card list
  uses TCGplayer, so the slot map puts both cards in the Common slot.
  Check a card scan or TCGdex after release.
- **Legendary Stadium slots**: no source says which slots the two
  halves use, or how often a pack has a pair. TCG Talk can mean that a
  half takes the rare slot. Default: the Uncommon slot, as a pair.
  The game gives about 35% of packs a pair. Check this rate when a
  pack study is available.
- **Legendary Stadium reverse holos**: TCGplayer lists the 6 halves
  with a Normal price only. No source says if a reverse holo half
  exists. The card list gives Normal only.
- **Talonflame ex (088/103 and 124/103)**: TCGplayer had no price row
  for these cards. The card list gives Holo only, as for the other ex
  cards.
- **The IR and SIR slot**: second reverse holo slot (era template)
  against the rare slot (TCG Talk). Default: the second reverse holo
  slot.
- **Half Booster Box**: the pack count (18) is not confirmed for this
  set.
- Box collation and per-card weights: Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Illustration Rare (11.01%)
  is more common than Ultra Rare (8.30%), even though Ultra Rare is the
  higher secret-rare tier by name. The TCG Talk sample shows the other
  order (UR 9.9%, IR 7.9%).
