## Pack structure

11 cards per pack.

- **PSA:** "one reverse foil card, one rare single, three uncommon cards,
  and six commons". The holo is in the rare slot.
- **Elite Fourum rarity guide:** Wizards of the Coast made the set for
  booster drafts. It printed "the 36 commons and the 36 uncommons on the
  same sheet". Each pack got "9 consecutive cards from the sheet". The
  110-card sheet gives "nearly 3 uncommons per pack on average".

So the 9 common/uncommon cards come in a fixed run from one sheet. The
uncommon count per pack is not always 3. Packs "include Pokémon from
evolution families that fit together" (Elite Fourum).

**Default for the simulation:** 1 rare slot (holo or non-holo), 1 reverse
holo, and 9 cards in a row from a common/uncommon sheet. The sheet layout
is Unknown, so use 3 uncommons and 6 commons until better data exists.

Four wrapper designs exist: Venusaur/Charizard/Blastoise,
Vaporeon/Jolteon/Flareon, Articuno/Zapdos/Moltres, and
Alakazam/Machamp/Mewtwo (Bulbapedia).

## Pack order

**Unknown.** Confidence: unknown.

- The Card Collector trick guide lists Legendary Collection as "No card
  trick". This suggests that the rare has no fixed position. The guide
  gives no reason.
- No source gives the position of the reverse holo.
- No source shows a real opened pack in order.

**Default for the rip screen:** put the rare slot card in the last three
cards, as the game default says. Flag it as unsourced. The common/uncommon
run from the sheet can keep its sheet order.

| One named reverse holo | 1 in 110 packs | Community estimate (Elite Fourum: all 110 reverses "extremely likely" on one sheet at equal rarity) |

| Reverse holo of a Rare card | 19 in 110 packs (about 17%) | Derived from the 1:110 figure |

A pack can hold two hits: the rare slot card and a rare-rarity reverse holo.

Wizards of the Coast published no odds. No empirical study for this set
was found. Loose Packs shows the set only in a photo, with no data.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Reverse holo:** the first English set with reverse holos. The pattern
  looks like fireworks. Every card in the set has a reverse holo version
  (Bulbapedia, PSA).
- **Box toppers:** the first English box toppers. They are jumbo reverse
  holo cards: Charizard (S1), Dark Blastoise (S2), Dark Raichu (S3), and
  Mewtwo (S4) (Bulbapedia, PSA).
- **Theme deck exclusives:** non-holo Charizard (3/110) comes only in the
  Lava theme deck. Non-holo Dark Blastoise (4/110) and Dark Raichu (7/110)
  come only in the Turmoil theme deck (Bulbapedia).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**This set breaks the e-Card pack template.** The 11-card pack has 1
rare slot, 1 reverse holo, and 9 cards in a row from one common/
uncommon print sheet. The real sheet layout is Unknown, so this map
uses the file's default of 6 commons and 3 uncommons.

Confidence: the pack structure and hit odds above give the slot
counts and the Rare Holo odds. The sheet-run cards use the file's
default split, flagged as unsourced.

| Slot | Count | Outcome | Rarity list entry | TCGdex rarity | Variant | Cards | Odds in slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 6 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | All | 100% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Holo Rare | Holo | All | 1 in 3 |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare | Rare | Rare | Normal | All | Rest |

## Open topics

- **Pack order.** Unknown for the rare and the reverse holo. One guide
  says the set has no card trick. Searched 2026-09-12: NinePocket agrees
  that the whole e-Card era, including this set, has "no reliable
  trick". No source names a position or a stack direction.
- **Uncommons per pack.** PSA says 3. The Elite Fourum sheet model gives
  "nearly 3" on average. The real sheet layout is Unknown. Searched
  2026-09-12: no source found.
- **Holo odds.** "1 in 3" has no official or empirical source for this set.
- **Rare slot sheet.** Unknown whether the 19 holos and 19 rares share a
  sheet, and at what ratio.
- **Box toppers.** Unknown whether the 4 toppers have equal odds.
- **Case size and blister products.** No source found. Searched
  2026-09-12: no source found.
- **Turmoil theme deck contents.** Not checked.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare and Rare Holo. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; the rare slot splits between Rare and Rare Holo.
- **Slot map:** the 9-card sheet run uses this file's default split of
  6 commons and 3 uncommons. The real sheet layout is Unknown, so a
  pack's uncommon count is not always exactly 3.
