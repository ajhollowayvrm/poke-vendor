## Pack structure

11 cards per pack.

- **PSA:** "one reverse foil card, one rare single, three uncommon cards,
  and six commons". The holo is in the rare slot.
- **Elite Fourum rarity guide:** Wizards of the Coast made the set for
  booster drafts. It printed "the 36 commons and the 36 uncommons on the
  same sheet". Each pack got "9 consecutive cards from the sheet". The
  110-card sheet gives "nearly 3 uncommons per pack on average".

So the 9 common/uncommon cards come in a fixed run from one sheet. The
uncommon count per pack is not always 3. Packs "include Pokémon from
evolution families that fit together" (Elite Fourum).

**Default for the simulation:** 1 rare slot (holo or non-holo), 1 reverse
holo, and 9 cards in a row from a common/uncommon sheet. The sheet layout
is Unknown, so use 3 uncommons and 6 commons until better data exists.

Four wrapper designs exist: Venusaur/Charizard/Blastoise,
Vaporeon/Jolteon/Flareon, Articuno/Zapdos/Moltres, and
Alakazam/Machamp/Mewtwo (Bulbapedia).

## Pack order

**Unknown.** Confidence: unknown.

- The Card Collector trick guide lists Legendary Collection as "No card
  trick". This suggests that the rare has no fixed position. The guide
  gives no reason.
- No source gives the position of the reverse holo.
- No source shows a real opened pack in order.

**Default for the rip screen:** put the rare slot card in the last three
cards, as the game default says. Flag it as unsourced. The common/uncommon
run from the sheet can keep its sheet order.

## Rarities and hit odds

| Figure | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rare or better in the rare slot | 1 per pack (100%) | Community estimate (PSA, Bulbapedia) |
| Holo in the rare slot | 1 in 3 packs | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Non-holo rare in the rare slot | 2 in 3 packs | Derived from the holo figure |
| Holos per 36-pack box | About 12 (mean) | Derived from 1 in 3. No box counts found. |
| One named reverse holo | 1 in 110 packs | Community estimate (Elite Fourum: all 110 reverses "extremely likely" on one sheet at equal rarity) |
| Reverse holo of a Rare Holo card | 19 in 110 packs (about 17%) | Derived from the 1:110 figure |
| Reverse holo of a Rare card | 19 in 110 packs (about 17%) | Derived from the 1:110 figure |
| Reverse holo that is rare or better | 38 in 110 packs (about 35%) | Derived from the 1:110 figure |

A pack can hold two hits: the rare slot card and a rare-rarity reverse holo.

Wizards of the Coast published no odds. No empirical study for this set
was found. Loose Packs shows the set only in a photo, with no data.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Reverse holo:** the first English set with reverse holos. The pattern
  looks like fireworks. Every card in the set has a reverse holo version
  (Bulbapedia, PSA).
- **Box toppers:** the first English box toppers. They are jumbo reverse
  holo cards: Charizard (S1), Dark Blastoise (S2), Dark Raichu (S3), and
  Mewtwo (S4) (Bulbapedia, PSA).
- **Theme deck exclusives:** non-holo Charizard (3/110) comes only in the
  Lava theme deck. Non-holo Dark Blastoise (4/110) and Dark Raichu (7/110)
  come only in the Turmoil theme deck (Bulbapedia).
