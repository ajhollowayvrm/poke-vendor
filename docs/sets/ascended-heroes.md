# Ascended Heroes (2026)

The first special set of the Mega Evolution era. It breaks the era
template in several ways. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2026-01-30 (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer). The Elite
  Trainer Boxes came later, on 2026-02-20 (TCGplayer).
- **Set code**: ASC. Product line code: ME2.5, written "me2pt5" by
  ThePriceDex. TCGplayer uses "ME: Ascended Heroes".
- **Card count**: 217 main set + 78 secret rares = 295 cards. Bulbapedia
  calls it "the largest Pokémon TCG expansion" in English.
- **Rarity counts** (Cardrake): 84 Common, 69 Uncommon, 25 Rare,
  39 Double Rare, 14 Ultra Rare, 7 Mega Attack Rare, 33 Illustration
  Rare, 22 Special Illustration Rare, 2 Mega Hyper Rare. These add up
  to 295.
- The Japanese counterpart is MEGA Dream ex (CardCodex).

## Pack structure

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Reverse holo slots | Probably 2 (see below) | Unknown, inferred from estimates |
| Rare slot | 1 | Community source |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex "11 cards per pack") |
| Code card | Unknown | No source read for this set |

**Reverse holo patterns**: each non-ex Pokémon has two reverse holos. One
has an Energy pattern. One has a Poké Ball pattern (Poké Ball, Love Ball,
Friend Ball, Quick Ball, or Dusk Ball). Team Rocket's Pokémon get an "R"
pattern in place of the ball (PokeBeach set guide, search summary;
Cardrake).

ThePriceDex estimates add up to about 0.92 Energy-pattern reverse holos
and 0.94 Poké Ball reverse holos per pack. This suggests one slot of
each pattern. **No source confirms this.** Treat it as an open topic.

## Pack order

Confidence: **Unknown for this set**. No source describes the Ascended
Heroes pack order. The table below is the era template. The two reverse
holo patterns may change positions 8 and 9.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo (pattern Unknown) |
| 9 | Reverse holo (pattern Unknown), or an IR / SIR |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, Mega Attack Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12, one of the last three cards.
- An IR or SIR at position 9 is **not** one of the last three.
- **God packs break this order.** Every card is a hit, so the first card
  is already a hit. The order of cards in a god pack: Unknown.
- The Mega Attack Rare slot is not confirmed. PokeBeach and EternaCards
  say that Mega Attack Rares "replace Ultra Rares in the pack
  structure", which points to the rare slot.

## Special subsets and mechanics

### Mega Attack Rare

A new rarity. Bulbapedia: "Full Art Mega Evolution Pokémon ex with
artworks rendered in a pop art style, and the card's highlight attack
written with large text." The set has 7.

### God packs

- A god pack holds only high-rarity cards and no commons or uncommons.
- **Contents conflict**:
  - CardCodex: every slot holds an Illustration Rare, a Special
    Illustration Rare, or a Mega Attack Rare "or higher".
  - A search-result snippet (source page not confirmed): 3 Mega Attack
    Rares and 7 Special Illustration Rares.
- **Odds conflict** (all community estimates, no official figure):
  - CardCodex: about 1 in 950 to 1 in 2,000 packs.
  - TCG Talk: 1 in 1,500 to 1 in 4,000 packs. This range comes from god
    packs "across multiple SV era sets", not from this set alone.
- **Pack weight**: TCG Talk reports one confirmed god pack at 23.80 g.
  Normal packs weigh 22.45 to 22.86 g. This is one sample.
- TCG Talk also says, in its Pitch Black guide, that "no official
  god-pack mechanic is known for English Mega Evolution sets." This
  conflicts with the Ascended Heroes reports.

### Other

- 13 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, 6 Tera Pokémon ex, 20 other Pokémon ex
  (Bulbapedia).
- **No booster boxes.** This is a special set.

## Open topics

- **Pack order**: no source for this set. The order of the two reverse
  holo patterns and of the cards in a god pack is Unknown.
- **Reverse holo slots**: one Energy slot and one Poké Ball slot is an
  inference from estimates, not a sourced fact.
- **Code card and Basic Energy**: no source read confirms both for this
  set. ThePriceDex gives 11 cards, which suggests 10 cards + 1 Energy.
- **Mega Attack Rare slot**: rare slot is likely but not confirmed.
- **God pack contents** conflict (any IR/SIR/Mega Attack Rare against
  3 Mega Attack Rares + 7 SIRs).
- **God pack odds** conflict, and no study measures them for this set.
- **Booster box**: TCG Talk lists a 36-pack booster box. Cardrake says
  that there are no booster boxes, and the TCGplayer product list has
  none. Treat "no booster box" as correct, and confirm.
- **Release month**: Cardrake says February 2026. Bulbapedia and
  TCGplayer say 2026-01-30. The February date is probably the ETB delay.
- Box collation and per-card weights: Unknown.
- **Rarity list odds:** ThePriceDex estimates suggest close to one Energy-pattern and one Poké Ball-pattern reverse holo per pack, but no source confirms a guaranteed slot for each pattern.
- **Rarity list order:** by this file's odds, Illustration Rare (1 in 9) is more common than Ultra Rare (1 in 21), even though Ultra Rare is the higher secret-rare tier by name.
