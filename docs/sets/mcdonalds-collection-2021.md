# McDonald's Collection 2021 (2021)

Set data for McDonald's Collection 2021, a promotional Sword & Shield era
set sold only in McDonald's Happy Meals. The era's booster-box template
does not apply. The era file is
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md). The general approach is in
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md). The era file lists this set as "Sets in
this era" with "No set file yet" before this file existed.

## How the product is sold

McDonald's Collection 2021 has no booster box and no retail booster pack.
It came only inside McDonald's Happy Meals.

- **Happy Meal pack.** A cardboard envelope with a puzzle on it, holding 4
  cards, bundled with one of four novelty Pokémon items (Bulbapedia).
- **Cards per pack:** 4.
- **Packs per product:** 1 pack per Happy Meal. No larger sealed product
  (box, tin, or bundle) was sold at retail (Bulbapedia).
- **Confidence:** official, from Bulbapedia's description of the promotion.

## Odds

Titan Cards ran an empirical study of 450 packs (3 sealed cases). Pull
rates for each of the 25 cards, across both finishes, ranged from 3.09%
to 4.64% per pack, with Pikachu (25/25) at 3.54% and the lowest, Popplio
(23/25), at 3.09%. Titan Cards: "The distribution appears that it was
intended to be as even as it could be."

| Figure | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Confetti Holofoil per pack | 1 of 4 cards, every pack (25% of cards in a pack, 100% of packs) | Official (pack structure), empirical study (Titan Cards) |
| A specific card's Confetti Holofoil, any pack | About 1 in 25 packs (roughly even across all 25 cards, 3.09% to 4.64% observed) | Empirical study, 450 packs |
| Pikachu pull rate specifically | 3.54%, in line with other cards | Empirical study, 450 packs |

Titan Cards notes that Pikachu commands a higher resale price only because
it is Pikachu, not because it is rarer to pull.

## Special mechanics

- **25th Anniversary tie-in.** The set marks the Pokémon TCG's 25th
  anniversary and reprints one first-partner Pokémon line per generation.
- **Card scalping and scam risk.** Titan Cards documents sellers who use
  "X-ray" techniques to find valuable cards before selling packs, and
  flags packs sent loose or with skewed pull histories as red flags.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: the pack structure table above is an empirical study of 450
packs (Titan Cards). Every card has close to an even chance within its
slot.

| Slot | Count | Outcome | Rarity list entry | TCGdex rarity | Variant | Cards | Odds in slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-Holofoil | 3 | Non-Holofoil | Non-Holofoil | None | Normal (25th celebration) | All | 100% |
| Confetti Holofoil | 1 | Confetti Holofoil | Confetti Holofoil | None | Holo (25th celebration) | All | 100% |

## Rarity list

The stop rule menu on the rip screen shows this list (see
[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). McDonald's Collection
2021 has no booster box; every pack is a 4-card Happy Meal pack with one
guaranteed Confetti Holofoil card.

| # | Entry | Type | Odds per pack | Default stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-Holofoil | Variant | Every pack (3 of 4 cards) | No |
| 2 | Confetti Holofoil | Variant | Every pack (1 of 4 cards, guaranteed) | Yes |

Confidence: the set has no rarity above Confetti Holofoil, so this list
has only two entries. Every card has an equal, roughly 1-in-25 chance of
being the specific card pulled in the Confetti Holofoil slot.
