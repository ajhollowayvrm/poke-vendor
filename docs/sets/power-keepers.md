# EX Power Keepers (2007)

Set-specific data for EX Power Keepers, the sixteenth and final set of
the EX Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system
are in [eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and
every exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

Bulbapedia calls this set "the sixteenth and final main expansion of
cards from the EX Series" and "the tenth and final expansion from the
PCG era." It is the last EX set to carry the set logo on Reverse
Holofoil card artwork (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

Follows the era's pack template ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#pack-contents)):
9 cards per pack, 36 packs per box. **Confidence: community estimate**
for the common and uncommon split; the era's two conflicting source
counts (5/2 vs. 4/3) both remain open for this set.

- A separate Japanese distribution existed: first-edition packs sold
  only through the Pokémon Card Game website and the Summer Battle
  Road 2007 event, each holding 15 cards (Bulbapedia). This is a
  Japanese-only product and does not apply to the English pack count
  above.

## Rarities and hit odds

**Confidence: community estimate**, and sources conflict on both the
Rare Holo ex and the Rare Holo ☆ figures for this set.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Uncommon | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Rare | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo | Unknown | Unknown |
| Rare Holo ex | About 3 to 7 per 36-pack box, across conflicting reports | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo ☆ | 1 to 2 per box (Flipside Gaming), or 1 in 72 packs (an Elite Fourum poster) | Community estimate |

- Flipside Gaming's rarity review calls out EX Power Keepers by name
  for "unusually frequent gold stars," giving "1 to 2 per box"
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#odds-by-set)).
- An Elite Fourum thread on this set's pull rate gives a conflicting
  view: one post states "Gold Stars are 1 in 72," and adds "People
  keep saying 1 in 2 or 3 boxes," treating the higher figure as
  overstated.
- The same thread gives conflicting Rare Holo ex figures: "the earlier
  e-series sets and Power Keepers you could get 5-7 ex per box," while
  another poster reports "3-4 seemed like my average for all the
  boxes." Neither poster gives a per-pack figure.
- No poster in either source gives a stated sample size or method.

**Default for the simulation:** treat the rare slot as one card per
pack, drawn from Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, or Rare Holo ☆ in that order of commonness, with Rare Holo ex at about 5 per
36-pack box and Rare Holo ☆ at the higher end, 1 to 2 per box, per
Flipside Gaming's named-set claim. This default carries no
set-specific confirmation of a per-pack figure and sources disagree on
both rows.

[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). The list goes
from the most common entry to the rarest entry. No box-count source
separates Rare and Rare Holo for this set, so the era rarity system
decides their order. The set has no Secret Rare (Bulbapedia set list).
