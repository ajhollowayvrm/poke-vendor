# Celestial Storm (2018)

Set data for Celestial Storm. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: August 3, 2018.
- Abbreviation: CES. English set number: 77.
- Card count: 168 main set + 15 secret rares = 183.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a Prism Star card (Elite Fourum post
  140).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); Prism Star
  slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or Prism Star) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no (rare slot
  card 7, Prism Star card 6). Pack-trick order: yes (rare slot last,
  Prism Star second to last).
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a Prism Star is a second hit in the reverse slot.**

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Prism
Star, Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.2 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 9 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star (reverse slot) | 1 in 18 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 24.5 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 82.4 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 123.6 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 8.1 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 8.5 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 4 "regular" Pokémon-GX, 0.8
full art Pokémon-GX, 1.4 full art Trainers and secret rares, 2 Prism
Stars. On the shared sheet, 8 secret rares print 3 times, 7 print 4
times, and the 8 full art Trainers print 6 times each. That gives 0.73
secret rares per box.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Sceptile and Swampert (theme deck exclusive holos) are short printed.
- Three uncommons (Rainbow Energy, Rare Candy, Shrine of Punishment)
  are rarer than all non-holo rares, by the study's rebuilt uncommon
  sheet. Confidence: empirical study.
- Uncommons come from two sheets in turn: two runs of 54 uncommons per
  box.
