# EX Ruby & Sapphire (2003)

Set-specific data for EX Ruby & Sapphire, the first set of the EX
Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and every
exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

EX Ruby & Sapphire and EX Sandstorm are the only two sets in the era
with no Rare Holo ☆ and no Secret Rare (see
[eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#sets-that-break-the-template)).

The Common count (168 per box) divides to 4.67 per pack, not a whole
number, and the Uncommon count (72) divides to exactly 2 per pack. This

elsewhere for this set (see Pack structure). See Open topics.

**Default for the simulation:** use the box-count column above.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon-ex.** This set introduces Rare Holo ex, "Pokémon-ex." A
  Knockout on this card gives the opponent 2 Prizes instead of 1
  (Bulbapedia).
- **No Rare Holo ☆ and no Secret Rare.** Confirmed by
  [eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#sets-that-break-the-template).
- **Updated card design.** Bulbapedia notes modified borders, relocated
  Evolution boxes, and smaller, linear energy symbols, compared to
  earlier eras.
- **God packs:** no source found. Confidence: unknown.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

TCGdex splits Rare Holo (non-ex) into its own rarity, "Holo Rare," so
the Common/Uncommon/Rare/Rare Holo split in the pack structure above
maps to Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Holo Rare here. The "Rare" rarity
holds both the non-holo Rares (Normal variant) and the 8 Rare Holo ex
cards (Holo variant); the Cards column picks the ex cards out by
number, from the 96–103/109 range Bulbapedia gives for them.

| Slot | Count | Outcome | Rarity list entry | TCGdex rarity | Variant | Cards | Odds in slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 2 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | All | 100% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo ex | Rare Holo ex | Rare | Holo | Nos. 96–103 | 1 in 6 |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Holo Rare | Holo | All | 1 in 6 |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare | Rare | Rare | Normal | Not nos. 96–103 | Rest |

## Open topics

- **Common/uncommon per-pack split.** The box counts (168 commons, 72
  uncommons over 36 packs) give 4.67 commons and 2 uncommons per pack,
  which conflicts with the 5-commons/2-uncommons split stated
  elsewhere for this set. Searched 2026-09-12: unresolved.
- **Set code.** Not confirmed in a fetched source.
- **Pack order and hit position.** Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found beyond the one low-confidence secondary claim in
  [eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#pack-order).
- **Theme Deck names.** Searched 2026-09-12: no source found for this
  set specifically.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives odds for Rare beyond "every
  pack" (1 per pack, per the rare slot). The era's rarity system
  decides its place in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo and Rare Holo ex tie at 6 per box.
  This file lists Rare Holo before Rare Holo ex, following the era
  rarity system's listed order. This is an arbitrary tie-break, not a
  measured difference.
