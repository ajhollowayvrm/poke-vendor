# Team Up (2019)

Set data for Team Up, the first set with TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX. The era
template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: February 1, 2019.
- Abbreviation: TEU. English set number: 79.
- Card count: 181 main set + 15 secret rares = 196.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a Prism Star card. Team Up is the last
  main set with Prism Stars (Elite Fourum posts 140 and 153).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); Prism Star
  slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or Prism Star) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no (rare slot
  card 7, Prism Star card 6). Pack-trick order: yes (rare slot last,
  Prism Star second to last).
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a Prism Star is a second hit in the reverse slot.**

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX (includes TAG
TEAM Pokémon-GX), Rare Prism Star, Rare Ultra (includes alternate art
full arts), Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 10 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star (reverse slot) | 1 in 18 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 24 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 60 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 120 | Empirical study |
| TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX (any version) | Unknown | Unknown |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum model:

- The full art Pokémon sheet includes the alternate arts. The study
  sees no sign that alternate arts are rarer.
- Every full art Trainer and secret rare prints 4 times on the shared
  sheet, so all have the same rarity.
- Data includes two large Derium opening videos.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX: two Pokémon on one Basic card. The opponent takes
  3 Prize cards on a Knock Out (Bulbapedia).
- Blastoise and Charizard (theme deck exclusive holos) are heavily short
  printed. Galvantula is the only rare at the "R5" weight. Confidence:
  empirical study.
- Part of the rebuilt uncommon sheet (Kakuna followed by Rapidash) often
  flips in cutting. That part of the order is not sure.

[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). The list goes from
the most common entry to the rarest entry. A Prism Star card can be a
second hit alongside the rare slot, in the reverse holo slot. TAG TEAM
Pokémon-GX sit inside the Rare Holo GX, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare
