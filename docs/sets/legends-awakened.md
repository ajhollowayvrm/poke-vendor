# Legends Awakened (2008)

Set data for Diamond & Pearl—Legends Awakened. This set uses the era
template in [eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md).
See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: August 20, 2008. Japanese release: March 14, 2008, as
  Cry from the Mysterious and Temple of Anger.
- Set number: DP6. Abbreviation: LA.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 146. Secret cards: 0.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 rare slot. Source:
  Bulbapedia (era template).
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, or a Rare Holo LV.X.
  Confidence: community reference.

## Pack order

The era template applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period. The
  two sources conflict.
- The reverse holo position and the common and uncommon order: Unknown.
- No code card. No separate Energy slot. Which end the cards face: Unknown.

## Open topics

- Pack order: the two community guides conflict. No source shows real
  opened packs. Searched 2026-09-12: no source found (era-wide search, see
  the era file).
- LV.X per box: 2 or 3. The sources conflict. Searched 2026-09-12: a
  PokeBeach forum reply says 2 is the average, with reports of 1 and 3.
  The conflict stands; the simulation default is 2.
- The non-holo rare rate. Boxes per case. Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo LV.X uses 1 in 18 (2 per box), the simulation default. The first PokeBeach Card Ratios post gives 1 in 12 (3 per box).
