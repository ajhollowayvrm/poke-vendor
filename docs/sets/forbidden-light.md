# Forbidden Light (2018)

Set data for Forbidden Light. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: May 4, 2018.
- Abbreviation: FLI. English set number: 76.
- Card count: 131 main set + 15 secret rares = 146.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- The reverse holo slot can hold a Prism Star card (Elite Fourum post
  140 names all sets from Ultra Prism to Team Up).
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template); Prism Star
  slot: empirical study.

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (reverse holo or Prism Star) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no (rare slot
  card 7, Prism Star card 6). Pack-trick order: yes (rare slot last,
  Prism Star second to last).
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- **Exception: a Prism Star is a second hit in the reverse slot.**

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Prism
Star, Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.3 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star (reverse slot) | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 24.3 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 83.3 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 125 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 10.4 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.6 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum model: 2.2 full art Pokémon-GX, full art Trainers, and
secret rares per 36 packs in total. The earlier sets have 2.4. Each
secret rare prints 4 times on the shared sheet (60 of 100 slots). That
gives 0.72 secret rares per box. The share of secret rares on that sheet
was 65.3% (author), 61.9% (@burpies), and 58.8% (Derium). The Derium
video is about 1,000 packs.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure and the ThePriceDex
odds above. The card list holds no Basic Energy card, so the Basic
Energy slot does not draw from it. TCGdex records Rare Holo GX and
Ultra Rare (full art) under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare
and Secret Rare under one rarity, Secret Rare. The card numbers tell
the four outcomes apart: Rare Holo GX is the first appearance of each
GX card, Ultra Rare is the full art repeat, Rainbow Rare is the second
repeat, and Secret Rare is the trailing Trainer and Energy cards.
TCGdex tags Prism Star cards with the ◇ mark in the card name, inside
the Rare rarity; the map finds them by name and number, so the reverse
holo slot's Prism Star outcome and the rare slot's Rare Holo outcome do
not share cards.

## Open topics

- **Slot map:** TCGdex records Rare Holo GX and Ultra Rare (full art)
  under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare and Secret Rare under
  one rarity, Secret Rare. The slot map splits each pair by card
  number, from the repeating pattern in the card names. No source
  confirms the split.
- **Slot map:** TCGdex tags Prism Star cards with a ◇ mark in the card
  name, inside the Rare rarity. The map lists the 5 Prism Star cards by
  number, so Rare Prism Star and Rare Holo do not share cards.
- **Slot map:** the Ultra Rare Normal variant (21 cards) and the Secret
  Rare Normal variant (15 cards) stay unused. TCGplayer added these
  Normal prints to cards that pull only as Holo in a pack.
- Pack order: no source shows a real opened Forbidden Light pack.
  Searched 2026-09-12 at the era level: no card-by-card video or
  photo source was found. See eras/sun-moon.md#orientation for the
  resolved raw-order vs pack-trick-order question.

  box ratios only in an image. The figures above come from ThePriceDex.

  to the odds table above.
