# Neo Revelation (2001)

Set data for Neo Revelation. The era template is in
[eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md](eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md). This file
records the Neo Revelation values and the differences from the
template.

## Release

- **English release date:** September 21, 2001.
- **Set symbol:** yes (Bulbapedia). Abbreviation: Unknown. ThePriceDex
  uses the database ID `neo3`.
- **Card count:** 64 in the main set, plus 2 secret rares: Shining
  Gyarados (65/64) and Shining Magikarp (66/64). Total 66.
- **1st Edition vs. Unlimited:** both exist. PSA says Unlimited was
  "manufactured in much higher quantities".
- **Theme decks:** "It was the first expansion to not have
  preconstructed Theme Decks associated with the expansion"
  (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

- 11 cards per pack.
- 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons, 7 commons (PSA, ThePriceDex).
- Energy card: Unknown. ThePriceDex lists no energy row.

## Pack order

Neo Revelation follows the era pack order. See
[eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md](eras/wizards-of-the-coast.md#pack-order).

- **Claim A** ([CardCollector](https://cardcollector.co.uk/pokemon-card-trick-pack-opening/)):
  "3" for Neo Revelation. The rare is card 8 of 11, the fourth card
  from the end (our derivation). **Not** one of the last three.
  Confidence: low.
- **Claim B** ([NinePocket](https://www.ninepocket.net/guides/pokemon-pack-trick)):
  no reliable order for this era. Confidence: low.
- **Shining Pokémon position:** Unknown. The Elite Fourum author
  guesses that the Shining cards "were printed on the same sheet" as
  the holos. If so, a Shining card fills the rare slot. No source
  confirms this.

## Rarities and hit odds

| Figure | Value | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare slot | 1 per pack, always | PSA | High |
| Holo | "approximately one of every three packs" | [PSA](https://www.psacard.com/articles/articleview/9458/psa-set-registry-collecting-2001-poke-mon-neo-revelation-1st-edition) | Community estimate |
| Holo | 1 in 3.4 packs | [ThePriceDex](https://www.thepricedex.com/set/neo3/neo-revelation/pull-rates) | Community estimate |
| Holo, 1st Edition | 8 holos in 35 packs (1 box) = 22.9% | [Loose Packs](https://loosepacks.com/blogs/guides/pokemon-pack-weight-guide) | Empirical study (small sample) |
| Non-holo rare | About 1 in 1.5 packs | ThePriceDex | Community estimate |
| Shining Pokémon (either card) | About 1 per box | PSA (a collector's personal estimate) | Community estimate |
| Shining Pokémon (either card) | 1 in 24 packs | ThePriceDex | Community estimate |
| Shining Pokémon (either card) | 1.309 per box, if both are H6 cards | [Elite Fourum rarity guide](https://www.elitefourum.com/t/the-english-pokemon-card-rarity-guide/39762) | Empirical study, with a stated assumption |
| Holo sheet model | 14 cards at H7 and 2 cards at H6 (110 cards) | Elite Fourum | Empirical study |

The Elite Fourum author writes: "I am assuming the H6 are the two
shining Pokémon (which would amount to 1.309 shining Pokémon per
box)." The author bases this on 10 boxes from recorded openings.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Shining Pokémon:** "a type of Pokémon with different coloration".
  "All Shining Pokémon are Basic Pokémon, and there can only be 1
  Shining Pokémon per name in a deck" (Bulbapedia).
- **Few non-holo rares:** the Elite Fourum guide counts only 13
  different non-holo rares. Each one is common inside the rare slot.

## Open topics

- Pack order: see the era file. No Neo Revelation-specific source
  exists.
- Shining slot: the rare slot is likely but not confirmed.
- Shining odds conflict: about 1 per box (PSA), 1.5 per box
  (ThePriceDex, 1 in 24 packs), or 1.309 per box (Elite Fourum). The
  figures are close, but no source is firm.
- Holo rate conflict: about 33% (PSA) vs. 1 in 3.4 (ThePriceDex) vs.
  22.9% (Loose Packs, one box).
- The count of uncommons and commons: Unknown.
- Energy in the pack, boxes per case, and blister formats: Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo uses 1 in 3 from PSA. ThePriceDex gives 1 in 3.4. Loose Packs gives 22.9% (8 holos in 35 packs).
- **Rarity list order:** Shining Pokémon (secret rare) uses 1 in 24 from ThePriceDex. PSA gives about 1 per box. Elite Fourum gives 1.309 per box.
