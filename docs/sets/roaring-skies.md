# Roaring Skies (2015)

Set-specific data for XY—Roaring Skies. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: May 6, 2015 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: ROS. Bulbapedia also numbers it XY6.
- Card count: 108 cards plus 2 secret rares (110 total).
- Japanese counterpart: Emerald Break (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons.

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common through Rare Ultra and Rare Secret.

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Per specific card | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 1 in 27 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | 1 in 64.3 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 9 | 1 in 99 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 17.0 | 1 in 187.5 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 125 | 1 in 250 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | — | Derived from the rows above |

All figures come from ThePriceDex. They match Primal Clash exactly.
This suggests template values (see
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#thepricedex-data-quality)).

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 12.1 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 6.7 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 3.0 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 2.3 packs".

Flipside Gaming gives a secret rare rate of 1:72 for the whole era
(**community estimate**).

## Special subsets and mechanics

- New support cards "Mega Turbo and Double Dragon Energy"

## Open topics

- No source shows a real opened Roaring Skies pack. The pack order is
  an era-wide community claim.
- The ThePriceDex figures match Primal Clash exactly. They may not be
  set-specific.
- The secret rare rate conflicts: 1 in 125 (ThePriceDex) against 1:72
  (Flipside Gaming).
- Bulbapedia mentions "Cracked Ice Holo" and "Cosmos Holo" versions.
  Which products had them is Unknown.
- Boxes per case: 6 (see [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md#sealed-products)).
  Collation is Unknown.
- **Rarity list order:** Secret Rare uses 1 in 125 from ThePriceDex. Flipside Gaming gives 1 in 72 for the era.
