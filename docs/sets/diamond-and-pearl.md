# Diamond & Pearl (2007)

Set data for Diamond & Pearl. This set uses the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md). This file
records the set data and every exception. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: May 23, 2007. Japanese release: November 30, 2006, as
  Diamond Collection and Pearl Collection.
- Set number: DP1. Abbreviation: DP.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 130. Secret cards: 0.
- First set of the Diamond & Pearl Series. First set with Generation IV
  Pokémon and Pokémon LV.X.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 Rare or Rare Holo.
  Source: Bulbapedia set page.
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, or a Rare Holo LV.X.
  Confidence: community reference.
- Diamond & Pearl lists its 8 Energy cards (123/130 to 130/130) as Common.
  A PokeBeach post says "Diamond and Pearl can have a basic energy card in
  a pack, because it counts as a common". Confidence: community reference.
- A PokeBeach poster reports error packs, for example "8 commons, an
  uncommon and a Rare". Confidence: anecdote.

## Pack order

The template in the era file applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period.
  Confidence: community guide. The two sources conflict.
- The position of the reverse holo and the order of the commons and
  uncommons: Unknown.
- An Energy card has no own slot. It can come in a common slot, at an
  Unknown position.
- No code card is in the pack.
- Which end of the wrapper the cards face: Unknown.
- A PokeBeach poster wrote: "Pretty much but not always a trainer commens
  before you hit the rare and stuff then rare and uncommens". The meaning
  is not clear. Confidence: anecdote.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Pokémon LV.X debut. A Pokémon LV.X is a "Level-Up" card that the player
  puts on the Active Pokémon with the same name.
- New reverse holo design: foil on the entire card except the artwork.
- Darkness Energy and Metal Energy got new basic Energy forms.

[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). The list goes from

  opened packs. The reverse holo and Energy positions are Unknown.
- Whether the Energy cards 123–130 are all basic Energy. The PokeBeach
  post says basic. Bulbapedia lists them only as Energy with Common rarity.
- The non-holo rare rate and the per-card weighting: Unknown.
- Boxes per case: Unknown.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
