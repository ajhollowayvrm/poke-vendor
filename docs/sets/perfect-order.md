# Perfect Order (2026)

The third main set of the Mega Evolution era. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2026-03-27 (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer, Pokemon.com).
- **Set code**: POR. Product line code: ME03 (Ultima Supply, ThePriceDex).
- **Card count**: 88 main set + 36 secret rares = 124 cards (Bulbapedia).
- **Rarity counts** (Cardrake, 2026-09-12): 44 Common, 24 Uncommon,
  11 Rare, 9 Double Rare, 18 Ultra Rare, 11 Illustration Rare,
  6 Special Illustration Rare, 1 Mega Hyper Rare. These add up to 124.
  - Pokémon ex: 4 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 9 other Pokémon ex
    (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer).
  - Cardrake's 9 Double Rare count agrees with Obsidia. Bulbapedia's
    13 ex figure counts Pokémon ex across every rarity, not only Double
    Rare.
- The Mega Hyper Rare is Mega Zygarde ex.
- Regulation mark: H (Ultima Supply).

## Pack structure

Official: "10 cards, 1 Basic Energy, and 1 Pokémon TCG Live code card"
(Pokemon.com product showcase).

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Reverse holo 2 (can be IR or SIR) | 1 | Community source (Obsidia, this set) |
| Rare slot (Rare, DR, UR, MHR) | 1 | Community source (Obsidia, this set) |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Official |
| Code card | 1 | Official |

Obsidia names the slots for this set: the rare slot holds "DR, UR, and
MHR cards", and the "second reverse holo slot" holds "IR and SIR cards".

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide, from the era template**. No source
describes the pack order of this set directly.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three.
- The last two cards are the Basic Energy and the code card.

## Rarities and hit odds

Main source: 3,500+ opened packs. A search summary attributes the data
to the TCGplayer Authentication Center. Obsidia does not name the source.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 per pack | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Rare | 1 in 1.4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Double Rare | 1 in 5 | Empirical study (3,500+ packs) |
| Ultra Rare | 1 in 12 | Empirical study (3,500+ packs) |
| Illustration Rare | 1 in 9 | Empirical study (3,500+ packs) |
| Special Illustration Rare | 1 in 81 | Empirical study (3,500+ packs) |
| Mega Hyper Rare | 1 in 1,786 | Empirical study (3,500+ packs) |
| Reverse holo common | 1 in 1.0 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo uncommon | 1 in 1.8 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |
| Reverse holo rare | 1 in 3.8 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex, "estimated") |

A release-day community sample (Ultima Supply, 381 packs from three
content creators) agrees on the main figures:

| Rarity | Result | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| Pokémon ex | ~1 in 5 | 46 of 216 packs |
| Illustration Rare | ~1 in 9 | 25 of 216 packs |
| Full-art Pokémon (UR) | ~1 in 24 | 9 of 216 packs |
| Full-art Trainer (UR) | ~1 in 24 | 9 of 216 packs |
| Special Illustration Rare | ~1 in 75 | 5 of 381 packs |
| Mega Hyper Rare | none | 0 of 381 packs |

The two Ultra Rare halves add up to about 1 in 12. This agrees with the
3,500-pack study.

The Mega Hyper Rare figure (1 in 1,786) is the rarest in the era. With
only 1 or 2 hits expected in 3,500 packs, its confidence is low.
