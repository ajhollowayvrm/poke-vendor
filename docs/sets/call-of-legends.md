# Call of Legends (2011)

Set data for Call of Legends. This set uses the era template in
[eras/heartgold-soulsilver.md](eras/heartgold-soulsilver.md), with the
differences below. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file
must cover.

Bulbapedia notes that the set first launched as the only set of a "Call of
Legends Series". By May 2013, the official website listed it with the
HeartGold & SoulSilver Series.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards per pack (Loose Packs).
- 36 packs per booster box (Loose Packs, ToyWiz, Amazon listing title).
- Slots: 5 common, 3 uncommon, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare. This follows the era
  template (community description). No source describes the slots of this
  set directly.
- **No Prime and no LEGEND cards.** The Bulbapedia set list has none.
- **Basic energy cards come only as reverse holos** (Bulbapedia). A basic
  energy card can come out only in the reverse holo slot.
- Box reports count 0 to 5 reverse holo basic energy cards per box
  (PokéBeach, March 2011).

## Pack order

This set follows the era pack order. Confidence: community claim.

| Card (front facing the player) | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common or uncommon, random order |
| 6 | Reverse holo (any card, including a basic energy) |
| 7 | Rare slot: rare or holo rare |
| 8–10 | Common or uncommon, random order |

- **The rare slot is card 7. It is not one of the last three cards.**
- The slot of a Shiny Legendary card is **Unknown**. It can be card 6 or
  card 7.
- cardcollector.co.uk lists "Call of Legends" by name as "3 cards to the
  back".
- NinePocket names Call of Legends in the group with "No reliable trick".
  See the era file.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Shiny Legendary cards (SL1–SL11).** Shiny prints of the legendary Pokémon
  that appeared as Pokémon LEGEND in this era. Rayquaza SL10 is a well-known
  chase card (Kanto Shark).
- **Reverse holo basic energy cards.** The basic energy cards exist only as
  reverse holos.
- **Lost World.** Box reports also count the Lost World Stadium card, a
  gameplay chase card: 0 to 8 per box.
- **New art reprints.** Many cards reprint earlier HGSS-era cards with new
  artwork (Bulbapedia).

## Open topics

- The pack order is a community claim. Sources conflict. See the era file.
- **Unknown: the slot of a Shiny Legendary card.** It can be the rare slot
  (card 7) or the reverse holo slot (card 6).
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found. PokéBeach box-report threads and
  Kanto Shark's set guide give per-box Shiny Legendary counts but do not
  name the slot.
- Unknown: the holo rare odds for this set. This set has 22 holo rares and 17
  non-holo rares, so the generic "1 in 3" figure is doubtful.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found.
- Conflict: Shiny Legendary odds. Flipside Gaming gives 1 in 18 (2 per box).
  Box reports average about 2.6 per box. Kanto Shark gives 1 per box or
  fewer.
- Unknown: whether the basic energy cards 88–95 take the reverse holo slot
  at the same rate as other cards.
- Unknown: boxes per case.
  Searched 2026-09-12: no source found. Current retailer listings sell
  single boxes, not sealed cases, for this set.
- Unknown: the pack counts and release dates of the Three Pack Blisters and
  the Knock Out Collection.
- Unknown: whether the Recon and Retort theme decks hold a booster pack.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare and Rare Holo. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Shiny Legendary (SL) uses 1 in 18 from Flipside Gaming. Box reports average about 1 in 14 packs; Kanto Shark gives about 1 per box or fewer.
