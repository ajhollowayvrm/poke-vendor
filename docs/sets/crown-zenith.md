# Crown Zenith (2023)

Set data for Crown Zenith, the last special expansion of the Sword & Shield
era. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the approach. See
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md) for the era template and the
evidence behind the pack order. **This set breaks the template:** a hit is in
the reverse holo slot in about one third of packs.

## Release

- English release: January 20, 2023.
- Set code: SWSH12.5 (official website and checklist). Code card sequence
  code: CZ. Abbreviation: CRZ. TCGplayer set code: CRZ.
- Cards: 230 total. 159 in the main numbering, plus 1 secret rare (Pikachu
  160/159), plus 70 Galarian Gallery cards (GG01–GG70).

## Pack structure

- TCGplayer: "Each Crown Zenith booster pack contains 10 random cards."
- CardShopLive: "5 common cards, 3 uncommon cards, 1 reverse holo card, 1 rare
  card. Packs also contain, 1 energy card and 1 code card".
- Reverse holo slot can hold: a reverse holo, a Galarian Gallery card, or a
  Radiant card (CardShopLive; TCGplayer confirms Galarian Gallery and Radiant).
- Rare slot can hold: a Rare, a Holo Rare, an Ultra Rare, the secret Pikachu,
  or a textured Energy card (CardShopLive).
- Bulbapedia: the set has reverse holo Energy cards without set numbers
  (copyright 2022). The textured Energy cards (copyright 2023) are in the set
  numbering.

## Pack order

**Confidence: community guide.** PokePatch names the "highly popular Crown
Zenith card trick" with 4 cards. NinePocket lists Crown Zenith and the
Galarian Gallery under the same method.

| Position | Reveal starts at the commons end |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (Galarian Gallery or Radiant hit in about 40% of packs) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | Uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare is **not** one of the last three cards in either direction.
- The reverse holo slot is next to the rare slot. It is often a hit in this
  set.
- With the community pack trick, the rare is last and the reverse holo slot
  is second-to-last. Both hits then fall in the last three cards.
- Which end faces the wrapper front: Unknown.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Galarian Gallery: 70 cards in the reverse holo slot. TCGplayer: it "works
  the same as the Trainer Gallery subsets ... but it's 40 cards bigger".
- Radiant Pokémon in the reverse holo slot: Radiant Charizard, Radiant
  Eternatus, Radiant Charjabug.
- Signature Trainers: 6 Holo Rare Supporters with a printed signature.
- No booster box. Packs came only in set products. A Booster Bundle came to
  Walmart later.

## Open topics

- Galarian Gallery rate: TCGplayer 35.20% (1,900+ packs). CardShopLive 30.10%
  (sum of its three rows, 1,000 packs). Use TCGplayer.
- Secret Rare Pikachu: TCGplayer 0.75%, CardShopLive 1.50%, DigitalTQ 0.98%.
- Galarian Gallery groups: TCGplayer counts 31 "Pokémon V or Trainer" cards.
  Bulbapedia lists 32 GGU cards. Total 34 + 31 + 4 = 69, not 70.
- CardShopLive "Yellow" and "Silver" groups do not match the TCGplayer groups.
- CardShopLive says "102 cards" per pack. This is a typing error for 10 cards
  plus Energy and code card.
- VSTAR marker in Crown Zenith packs: Unknown.
- Pack order: which end faces the wrapper front is Unknown.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides its place in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list odds:** TCGplayer gives one combined figure (5.30%) for Pokémon VMAX or VSTAR. No source splits Rare VSTAR from Rare VMAX, so both entries use the combined figure.
