# Chaos Rising (2026)

The fourth main set of the Mega Evolution era. One file per set (see
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)). The shared era template is in
[eras/mega-evolution.md](eras/mega-evolution.md).

## Release

- **English release**: 2026-05-22 (Bulbapedia, TCGplayer, TCG Talk).
- **Set code**: CRI. Product line code: ME04 (TCGplayer).
- **Card count**: 86 main set + 36 secret rares = 122 cards (Bulbapedia).
  ThePriceDex also gives 122.
- TCG Talk gives 83 main set cards and numbers up to 120/083. This
  conflicts with Bulbapedia and ThePriceDex.
- **Rarity counts** (Cardrake, 2026-09-12): 38 Common, 26 Uncommon,
  12 Rare, 10 Double Rare, 18 Ultra Rare, 11 Illustration Rare,
  6 Special Illustration Rare, 1 Mega Hyper Rare. These add up to 122,
  which agrees with Bulbapedia and ThePriceDex, against TCG Talk's 120.
  - The 10 Double Rare are 5 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 5 Pokémon ex
    (Bulbapedia, Obsidia).
- The Mega Hyper Rare is Mega Greninja ex.

## Pack structure

Official: "Each booster pack contains 10 cards, 1 Basic Energy, and
1 Pokémon TCG Live code card" (Pokemon TCG expansion page).

| Slot | Count | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Uncommon | 3 | Community estimate (ThePriceDex) |
| Reverse holo 1 | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Reverse holo 2 (can be IR or SIR) | 1 | Community source (Obsidia, this set) |
| Rare slot (Rare, DR, UR, MHR) | 1 | Community source (era template) |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Official |
| Code card | 1 | Official |

Obsidia: Illustration Rares appear "in the second Reverse Holo slot",
separate from the rare slot. A pack can hold an IR and a Double Rare
together.

## Pack order

Confidence: **community guide, from the era template**. No source
describes the pack order of this set directly.

| Position | Card |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Common x4 |
| 5-7 | Uncommon x3 |
| 8 | Reverse holo 1 |
| 9 | Reverse holo 2, or an Illustration Rare / Special Illustration Rare |
| 10 | Rare slot: Rare, Double Rare, Ultra Rare, or Mega Hyper Rare |
| 11 | Basic Energy |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare-slot hit is position 10 of 12. It **is** one of the last
  three cards.
- An IR or SIR is position 9 of 12. It is **not** one of the last three.
- The last two cards are the Basic Energy and the code card.
- One pack can hold two hits: an IR at position 9 and a Double Rare or
  higher at position 10.

### Conflicting figures

- **ThePriceDex** (community estimate): SIR 1 in 90, Mega Hyper Rare
  1 in 1,100, UR 1 in 12, IR 1 in 9, DR 1 in 5.
- **TCG Talk** (168 packs, community sample): ex 23.2%, IR 10.7%,
  full-art ex 3.6%, full-art Trainer or Energy 3.0%, SIR 0.6%, Rare only
  57.7%. A second sample of 100 packs gave about 10-12 IRs.
- **CardDeckr** (community estimate): "Double Rares pull at approximately
  one in every 3.5 packs, making them the most reliably frequent hit in
  the set." This conflicts with the TCGplayer figure (1 in 5).
- Prefer the TCGplayer figures. They have the largest sample.
