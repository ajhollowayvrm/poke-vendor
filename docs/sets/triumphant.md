Triumphant is the last main expansion of the HeartGold & SoulSilver Series.

## Pack structure

- 10 cards per pack. This follows the era template.
- 36 packs per booster box. Confidence: community. A PokéBeach reply
  calculates "3:36 = 1:12" for LEGENDs per box. No retailer page for this box
  was checked.
- Slots: 5 common, 3 uncommon, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare (community
  description).
- A Prime replaces the reverse holo (Bulbapedia, PokéBeach).
- The set list has no basic energy cards. It has Rescue Energy 90, a special
  energy card with the Uncommon rarity.

## Pack order

This set follows the era pack order. Confidence: community claim.

| Card (front facing the player) | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common or uncommon, random order |
| 6 | Reverse holo, or a Prime |
| 7 | Rare slot: rare, holo rare, LEGEND half, or Alph Lithograph |
| 8–10 | Common or uncommon, random order |

- **The rare slot is card 7. It is not one of the last three cards.**
- A Prime is card 6. It is not one of the last three cards.
- cardcollector.co.uk lists "HS Triumphant" by name as "3 cards to the back".
- NinePocket says this era has no reliable pack order. See the era file.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon LEGEND.** Darkrai & Cresselia LEGEND and Palkia & Dialga LEGEND.
- **Pokémon Prime.** Eight Prime cards take the reverse holo slot. That is
  the most Primes in one set of this era.
- **Lost Link cards.** The set includes cards from the Japanese Lost Link
  subset.
- **Known errors.** Psyduck 74 and Weepinbell 53 spell "Flip" as "Filp" in
  their attacks. The errors were not corrected.
