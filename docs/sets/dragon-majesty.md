# Dragon Majesty (2018)

Set data for Dragon Majesty, a special expansion with no booster boxes.
The era template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: September 7, 2018.
- Abbreviation: DRM. No English set number ("special expansion").
- Set symbol: "the letter D, stylized to look like a dragon"
  (Bulbapedia).
- Card count: 70 main set + 8 secret rares = 78.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

| Slot | Count | Contents |
|------|-------|----------|
| Common | 5 | Commons |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommons |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse holos, including reverse Basic Energy |
| Rare slot | 1 | Holo rare or higher. The set has no non-holo rares. |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | Unknown | No source says |

- Sources: ThePriceDex (11 cards; 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 Energy);
  Bulbapedia rarity list (no plain Rare); Elite Fourum post 149 (65
  reverses, including reverse Basic Energy).
- Confidence: community data site plus Bulbapedia. Bulbapedia gives no
  card count per pack.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare Holo, Rare Prism, Rare
Ultra, Rare Secret, Rare Rainbow. Pokémon-GX also appear.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare Holo | 1 in 1.3 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 6.7 | Empirical study |
| Rare Prism Star | 1 in 10 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 17.4 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 46.9 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 46.9 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 6.7 | Estimate |
| Reverse Energy (reverse slot) | 1 in 14.9 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum model assumptions:

- A hit (Pokémon-GX, full art, or secret rare) in every fourth pack.
- A Prism Star in every tenth pack.
- 2.4 full art Trainers and secret rares per 36 packs, double the full
  art Pokémon-GX figure.

Data: the author's own sample and the @burpies data. No Derium video
exists for this set. The two samples disagree on the ratio of full art
Trainers to secret rares.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Dragon-type theme. Higher hit rate than the main sets.
- Gold cards and rainbow rares share one sheet with the full art
  Trainers (Elite Fourum model).
- The two Prism Star cards had different observed counts. The author
  does not treat the difference as real.

## Open topics

- Pack order for this set: not confirmed by any source.
- The Prism Star slot: Unknown. In the main sets, Prism Stars come in
  the reverse slot, but no source confirms this for Dragon Majesty.
- Code card in the pack: Unknown.
- Pack size from Bulbapedia: Unknown. Only ThePriceDex gives 11.
- The data sample is smaller than for the main sets.
- The author did not check if the reverse Basic Energy cards are rarer
  than other reverses.
- **Rarity list order:** Rainbow Rare and Secret Rare both use 1 in 46.9 from ThePriceDex. The list places Rainbow Rare first, from the era's rarity system order.
