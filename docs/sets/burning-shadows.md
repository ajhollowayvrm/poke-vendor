# Burning Shadows (2017)

Set data for Burning Shadows. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: August 4, 2017.
- Abbreviation: BUS. English set number: 73.
- Card count: 147 main set + 22 secret rares = 169.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex and the era template).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known. No Prism Star cards in this set.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra,
Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 9 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 23.3 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 71.0 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 102.6 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 8.9 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.6 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 4.0 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.1 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.3 full art Trainers and secret rares. Each
of the 22 secret rares prints 3 times on the shared sheet.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Lycanroc and Alolan Ninetales (non-holo rares with theme deck
  exclusive holo versions) appear short printed. Confidence: empirical
  study.
- The study author rebuilt the full common and uncommon sheets for this
  set from videos (Elite Fourum post 143).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure and the ThePriceDex
odds above. The card list holds no Basic Energy card, so the Basic
Energy slot does not draw from the card list. TCGdex records Rare Holo
GX and Ultra Rare (full art) under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow
Rare and Secret Rare under one rarity, Secret Rare. The card numbers
tell the four outcomes apart: Rare Holo GX is the first appearance of
each GX card, Ultra Rare is the full art repeat, Rainbow Rare is the
second repeat, and Secret Rare is the trailing Trainer and Energy
cards. The Rare rarity splits cleanly: 26 cards carry only a Normal
variant, and 14 carry a Holo variant.
