# POP Series 1–9 (2004–2009)

One combined file for the nine Pokémon Organized Play (POP) promotional
sets. POP Series 6 to 9 fall in the Diamond & Pearl and Platinum era (see
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md)). POP
Series 1 to 5 are older. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

## Release

POP sets were prizes at Pokémon League and Organized Play events. Bulbapedia:
"Each series would be exclusive to POP for six months". Later, POP packs
came in blisters, tins, and value packs. All nine sets have 17 cards and no
secret cards. Each set has its own set symbol image (Bulbapedia). This file
does not describe the symbols.

## Pack structure

| Set | Cards per pack | Slot breakdown | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| POP Series 1–5 | 2 | Unknown. Bulbapedia: "2-card booster packs, with the odds of finding a rare card extremely low". | Bulbapedia (count). Breakdown Unknown. |
| POP Series 6 | 2 | Bulbapedia: "Due to a production error, every POP 6 pack contains one rare, and one common or uncommon card." | Bulbapedia |
| POP Series 7 | 2 | Unknown | Bulbapedia (count) |
| POP Series 8 | 3 | 2 set cards plus "an Energy card". Bulbapedia: "the first POP set to include 3 cards per pack". | Bulbapedia |
| POP Series 9 | 3 | Bulbapedia: "two Pokémon and one basic Energy card". | Bulbapedia |

- No reverse holo slot is documented for any POP Series pack.
- The Energy card in POP Series 8 and 9 packs is not one of the 17 set
  cards.

## Pack order

- Unknown for all nine sets. No source describes the card order.
- NinePocket: "Promos and special sets (McDonald's collections, POP series,
  promo packs) do not use the standard booster layout either, so treat any
  'method' for them with suspicion." Confidence: community guide.
- A POP pack has 2 or 3 cards. So every card is one of the last three
  cards. The game default is true for every POP pack.
- For POP Series 8 and 9, the position of the Energy card is Unknown.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- Holo versions of many POP cards exist only in other products, not in POP
  packs. Examples: Cosmos Holo versions in blisters and value packs.
  Cracked Ice Reverse Holo versions of some POP Series 8 and 9 cards.
  Source: Bulbapedia "Additional cards" lists.
- POP Series 7 has only reprints (Bulbapedia).
- POP Series 7 Wormadam Sandy Cloak was banned from the 2008 North American
  Professor Cup (Bulbapedia).

## Rarity list

The stop rule menu on the rip screen shows this list (see
[../18-ripping.md](../18-ripping.md#the-stop-rule)). This file covers
nine separate sets with no confirmed per-pack odds, so the list gives
only the shared rarity order. Rare Holo ex appears only in POP Series
1–4. Rare Holo ☆ appears only in POP Series 5. A production error makes
every POP Series 6 pack hold a Rare card; every other set has no known
Rare rate.
