## Pack structure

9 cards per pack: "one was a reverse foil card, one a rare, two were
uncommons, and five were commons" (PSA).

The holo is an extra card (Model B):

- PSA, from collector Zack Browning: "if there is a regular holographic
  card in the pack, there will also be another rare card. In these
  instances, the regular holographic card takes the place of one of the
  common cards".
- The Elite Fourum rarity guide gives the same average pack: "4 2/3
  commons, 2 uncommons, 1 rare, 1/3 holos and 1 reverse".

So a holo pack holds 4 commons, 2 uncommons, 1 rare, 1 holo, and 1
reverse holo. Confidence: community estimate (two sources agree).

The reverse holo slot can hold any card except the H cards and the Crystal
cards (Bulbapedia, PSA).

## Pack order

**Confidence: community estimate, low.** Two trick guides agree.

| Source | Cards to move (N) | Rare position (of 9) | In the last three? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PokéPatch | 2 | 7 | Yes |
| Card Collector | 2 | 7 | Yes |

- PokéPatch says that the reverse holo is second to last after the trick.
  In the natural order, that is card 6. Card 6 is not in the last three.
- The position of the extra holo is Unknown.
- The rare positions come from our reading of the trick guides. See
  [eras/e-card.md](eras/e-card.md#pack-order).
- No source shows a real opened pack in order.

**Default for the rip screen:** card 6 is the reverse holo. Card 7 is the
rare. In a holo pack, put the holo in the last three cards until a source
says otherwise. Flag both as low confidence.

## Rarities and hit odds

| Figure | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Non-holo rare per pack | 1 per pack (100%) | Community estimate (PSA, Elite Fourum) |
| Holo per pack | About 1 in 3 | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Holos in 72 packs (2 boxes) | 19 (about 26%) | Empirical study, very small (Loose Packs) |
| Crystal card per pack | About 1 in 36 | Community estimate (Flipside Gaming) |
| Crystal cards per box | 1 to 3 | Community estimate (PSA) |
| Crystal cards per box | 0 in one box; "2 crystals or so per box" | Community anecdote (Elite Fourum forum posts) |
| One named Crystal card per pack | About 1 in 105 | Derived. Assumes Crystal cards share the holo sheet at holo rarity. The Elite Fourum guide says this assumption is "rather arbitrary". |
| Reverse holo that is a rare | About 29% of packs | Community estimate (Elite Fourum: "29% reverse rares, 38% reverse uncommons and 33% reverse commons") |

The derived Crystal figure agrees with Flipside: (1/3) × (3/35) is about
1 in 35 packs.

A pack can hold three hits: the rare, a holo, and a rare-rarity reverse
holo.

Wizards of the Coast published no odds.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **H-numbered holos:** the first set to number holos apart from the main
  set, H1 to H32 (Flipside Gaming, Bulbapedia). PSA calls this the first
  such set.
- **Crystal Pokémon:** Aquapolis introduced them. Their "Crystal Type"
  Poké-Body changes the Pokémon's type to the type of the attached Energy
  (Bulbapedia). The 3 Crystal cards are holo secret rares with no reverse
  holo version.
- **Dot Code variants:** Golduck, Drowzee, Mr. Mime, and Porygon each have
  "a" and "b" versions with different Dot Code strips (Bulbapedia).
- **Box toppers:** Entei, Espeon, Scizor, and Suicune. They are cards
  05–08 of the 12-card e-Card box topper subset (PSA, Bulbapedia).
- **Wrappers:** Arcanine, Scizor, Entei, and Tyranitar (Bulbapedia).

## Open topics

- **Extra holo position.** Unknown where the holo sits in a holo pack.
- **Reverse holo position.** Card 6 puts the reverse holo outside the
  last three cards. A rare-rarity reverse holo is a hit.
- **Crystal odds.** Flipside gives 1 in 36 packs. PSA gives 1 to 3 per box.
  One forum poster got 0 in a box. No large study found.
- **Crystal sheet.** The Elite Fourum guide assumes the Crystal cards
  share the holo sheet. The guide calls this assumption arbitrary.
- **Reverse holo card count.** The Elite Fourum guide gives 151 reverses
  (337 cards in total). Flipside gives "340 once you add in reverse holos".
- **Holo odds.** "1 in 3" is not official. Loose Packs found 19 in 72
  packs, and it says holos split cleanly by weight in only 1 of 2 boxes.
- **Case size and Abyss theme deck contents.** Not found or not checked.
