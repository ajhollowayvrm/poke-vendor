# Silver Tempest (2022)

Set data for Silver Tempest, the last main set of the Sword & Shield era. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the approach. See
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md) for the era template and the
evidence behind the pack order.

## Release

- English release: November 11, 2022.
- Set code: SWSH12. Abbreviation: SIT. TCGplayer set code: SWSH12.
- Cards: 245 total. 195 in the main numbering, plus 20 secret rares, plus 30
  Trainer Gallery cards.

## Pack structure

- Bulbapedia: "Each Silver Tempest booster pack contains ten cards from the
  expansion, a Pokémon TCG Live code card, and either a basic Energy card or a
  VSTAR marker card."
- Slots (era template): 5 commons (derived), 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
  slot, 1 rare slot.
- TCGplayer: "Roughly 12% Silver Tempest booster packs contain a Trainer
  Gallery card in the Reverse-Holo slot." A Trainer Gallery card "doesn't
  affect your chances of finding an Ultra Rare or Secret Rare in the Rare slot
  of the same pack."
- Radiant slot: Unknown for this set.

## Pack order

**Confidence: community guide.** NinePocket lists Silver Tempest and its
Trainer Gallery under the Sun & Moon to Sword & Shield method.

| Position | Reveal starts at the commons end |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot (can be a Trainer Gallery card) |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy or VSTAR marker |
| 9–11 | Uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare is **not** one of the last three cards in either direction.
- A Trainer Gallery hit sits next to the rare slot, near the middle.
- With the community pack trick, the rare is last.
- Which end faces the wrapper front: Searched 2026-09-12: no source found (see the era file, which also resolves the code card's physical position).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: the era template gives the slots. A Trainer Gallery card
replaces the reverse holo card, with a third, rarer group for the
gold-and-black VMAX pair (TG29–TG30). This set's Radiant slot is
Unknown; the map puts it in the reverse holo slot, matching Crown
Zenith, a later set with a confirmed Radiant slot.
