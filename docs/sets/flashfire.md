# Flashfire (2014)

Set-specific data for XY—Flashfire. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for what a set file covers. See [eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack
template of this era.

## Release

- English release: May 7, 2014 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: FLF. Bulbapedia also numbers it XY2.
- Card count: 106 cards plus 3 secret rares (109 total).
- Japanese counterpart: Wild Blaze, 90 cards (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons. ThePriceDex
  gives "3 cards per pack" uncommon and "5 cards per pack" common.

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
EX, Rare Ultra, Rare Secret.

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Per box (36) | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 24 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.1 | 7.1 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 11.3 | 3.2 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 25.7 | 1.4 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 114.3 | 0.3 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | about 12 | Derived from the rows above |

All per-pack figures come from ThePriceDex. Flipside Gaming gives a
secret rare rate of 1:72 for the whole era (**community estimate**).
The two figures conflict.
