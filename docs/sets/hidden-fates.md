# Hidden Fates (2019)

Set data for Hidden Fates, a special expansion with the Shiny Vault
subset and no booster boxes. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: August 23, 2019.
- Abbreviation: HIF. No English set number ("special expansion").
- Card count: 68 main set + 1 secret rare + 94 Shiny Vault cards
  (SV1–SV94) = 163.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

"Each booster pack contained ten cards, plus one Energy card and a code
card" (Bulbapedia). ThePriceDex gives 11 cards per pack.

| Slot | Count | Contents |
|------|-------|----------|
| Common and uncommon | Unknown split | Commons and uncommons |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse holo (including reverse Basic Energy) or a Shiny Vault card |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, holo rare, Pokémon-GX, full art, or rainbow rare |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | 1 | Pokémon TCG Online code card |

- The Elite Fourum post says commons, uncommons, rares, holos, and
  reverses work "just as in the main expansions". It does not give the
  common and uncommon counts.
- Shiny Vault cards come in the reverse slot. The post says so directly
  for the Shiny Vault full art Trainers, and its model puts all Shiny
  Vault groups in the reverse slot.
- Confidence: well documented for 10 + Energy + code card; empirical
  study for the slot of the Shiny Vault cards.

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw template order: no

## Rarities and hit odds

### Rare slot

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.7 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 6.7 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 25.6 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare (Moltres & Zapdos & Articuno-GX) | 1 in 90.9 | Empirical study |

### Reverse slot (Shiny Vault)

| Rarity (ThePriceDex label) | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|----------------------------|---------------|------------|
| Shiny Rare ("baby shiny") | 1 in 4.7 | Empirical study |
| Shiny Rare GX | 1 in 10.6 | Empirical study |
| SV Secret Rare | 1 in 63 | Empirical study |
| SV Ultra Rare | 1 in 84 | Empirical study |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set. The four Shiny Vault figures add to about 1 in 3 packs. This
matches the Elite Fourum model.

Elite Fourum model: about 1 in 3 packs (12 per 36) has a premium card
in the reverse slot. About 2 in 5 packs (14.4 per 36) has a premium card
in the rare slot. Data: the author's samples, Derium (1,480 packs), and
the @burpies thread.

Second source, quoted in the Elite Fourum post (a Reddit post by
cardzard.com, 2,350 packs): baby shinies 1 in 4, Shiny Pokémon-GX 1 in
9, Shiny Vault full art Trainers 1 in 73, full-gold cards 1 in 57.
Confidence: empirical study; the original Reddit post was not read.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- **Shiny Vault**: 94 cards numbered SV1–SV94. Most are Sun & Moon
  Series reprints of Shiny Pokémon with an embossed holofoil
  (Bulbapedia).
- The study models 3 Shiny Vault sheets: baby shinies (11x11); Shiny
  Pokémon-GX; and Shiny Vault full art Trainers with full-gold Tapu
  Pokémon-GX.
- The main set has one full art Pokémon-GX and one rainbow rare, both
  Moltres & Zapdos & Articuno-GX, the first TAG TEAM trio.
- The Japanese equivalent, GX Ultra Shiny, has a different pack (10
  cards, a guaranteed Pokémon-GX). Do not use it for the English set.

## Open topics

- Pack order for this set: not confirmed by any source.
- **Pack size conflict.** Bulbapedia gives 10 cards plus an Energy and a
  code card. ThePriceDex gives 11 cards. The common and uncommon split
  is Unknown.
- **Label conflict.** ThePriceDex "SV Secret Rare" (1 in 63) and "SV
  Ultra Rare" (1 in 84) do not match the cardzard.com groups (full-gold
  1 in 57, full art Trainers 1 in 73) one to one.
- A Cardrake guide claims 3–4 Shiny Vault cards per pack on average. No
  other source supports this. It conflicts with the 1 in 3 figure, so
  this file does not use it.
- The @burpies data does not separate main set and Shiny Vault full art
  Trainers.
- Release date of the Ultra-Premium Collection: Unknown.
