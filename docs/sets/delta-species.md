# EX Delta Species (2005)

Set-specific data for EX Delta Species, the eleventh set of the EX
Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system are in
[eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and every
exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

Engine (Bulbapedia).

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **δ Delta Species.** A Pokémon printed with a type other than its
  usual type, marked with the δ symbol and a double-helix border on the
  artwork. Rare Holo δ cards use a refractor holofoil (Bulbapedia). Not
  a separate print rarity: a δ card can be Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
  ex, or Rare Holo ☆
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#rarity-system)).
- **Holon Engine.** Holon cards function as both a Pokémon card and an
  Energy card at the same time, for example Holon's Electrode and
  Holon's Magneton (Bulbapedia). Holon Energy FF, GL, and WP are
  Special Energy cards with unique effects. Holon Trainer cards include
  Holon Research Tower and Holon Lass. Holon cards sit in the normal
  Common, Uncommon, and Rare rarities, not a separate rarity
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#rarity-system)).
- **Rare Holo ☆.** Groudon ☆, Kyogre ☆, Metagross ☆
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#sets-that-break-the-template)).
- **Secret Rare.** Azumarill, at 114/113
  ([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#sets-that-break-the-template)).
- **God packs:** no source found. Confidence: unknown.
