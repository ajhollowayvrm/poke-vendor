# Arceus (2009)

Set data for Platinum—Arceus. This set uses the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: November 4, 2009. Japanese release: July 8, 2009, as
  Advent of Arceus.
- Set number: PL4. Abbreviation: AR.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 99. Arceus cards: 9 (AR1 to AR9). Shiny cards: 3 (SH10 to
  SH12). No secret cards numbered above 99.
- Last set of the Platinum Series. Last set with Pokémon LV.X in a set
  (Bulbapedia).

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 rare slot. Source:
  Bulbapedia (era template). Loose Packs: "36 Booster Packs per box".
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, or a Rare Holo LV.X.
  Confidence: community reference.
- **Exception:** the AR cards come in the reverse holo slot. PokeBeach Card
  Ratios: "Comes as a Reverse Holo". Confidence: community estimate.
- **Exception:** the SH cards come in the reverse holo slot. Confidence:
  community estimate (PokeBeach Card Ratios).

## Pack order

The era template applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period. The
  two sources conflict.
- LV.X cards sit in the rare slot position.
- **Exception:** AR and SH cards are hits in the reverse holo slot. Their
  position is Unknown. About 1 in 4 packs has an AR card, so this
  exception is frequent.
- The common and uncommon order: Unknown. No code card. No separate Energy
  slot. Which end the cards face: Unknown.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Arceus cards (AR1–AR9):** Arceus in nine types, numbered outside the
  main set.
- Three versions of Arceus LV.X (94/99, 95/99, 96/99).
- Pokémon SP continue from earlier Platinum sets.
