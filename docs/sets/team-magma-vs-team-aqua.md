# EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua (2004)

Set-specific data for EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua, the fourth set of
the EX Series. The shared pack template, pack order, and rarity system
are in [eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md). This file records the set data and
every exception. See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general
approach.

This set introduces dual-type Pokémon and splits its cards between two
warring factions, Team Magma and Team Aqua, each with its own card
border design.

## Pack structure

Follows the era's pack template
([eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#pack-contents)): 9 cards per pack.
**Confidence: community estimate.** A box-count source (Elite Fourum)
gives this set 5 commons and 2 uncommons per pack, worked out below.

| Slot | Count | Can hold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 5 | Common |
| Uncommon | 2 | Uncommon |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, or Secret Rare |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse Holo of a Common, Uncommon, or Rare |

Box: 36 packs (Bulbapedia merchandise page). The box-count source gives
180 commons and 72 uncommons per box, which divide evenly to 5 and 2
per pack.

## Rarities and hit odds

Source: Elite Fourum's "Guide to early EX Series pull rates," as a
36-pack (one box) breakdown. **Confidence: community estimate** for
every row. This is one of the six sets that
[eras/ex.md](eras/ex.md#open-topics) flags as unresolved; this
research pass extracted the box counts directly from the thread.

| Rarity | Count per 36-pack box | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 180 | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Uncommon | 72 | Every pack | Community estimate |
| Rare | 24 | 1 in 1.5 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 8.7 | 1 in 4.1 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo ex | 3 | 1 in 12 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 0.3 | 1 in 120 | Community estimate |

The Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo ex, and Secret Rare counts (24 + 8.7 + 3
+ 0.3 = 36) add up to exactly one card per pack across the 36-pack
box, matching a single rare slot. The fractional Rare Holo and Secret
Rare counts (8.7, 0.3) are not whole numbers; this file uses them as
given. See Open topics.

**Default for the simulation:** use the box-count column above.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Team split.** Cards belong to Team Magma (curvy border) or Team
  Aqua (bubble-pattern border). Non-team cards keep the standard
  border (Bulbapedia).
- **Dual-type Pokémon.** Bulbapedia: this set "introduced dual-type
  Pokémon, in which Pokémon have a secondary type."
- **Last dot-code set.** Bulbapedia: only non-team cards retain the
  e-Reader dot-code strip. This is "the final expansion to include the
  dot code strip."
- **Two Theme Decks,** Team Magma and Team Aqua (Bulbapedia).
- **Prerelease promo.** Card 24/95, Team Aqua's Cacnea (Grass/Darkness),
  carries a "Prerelease" stamp (Bulbapedia).
- **God packs:** no source found. Confidence: unknown.
