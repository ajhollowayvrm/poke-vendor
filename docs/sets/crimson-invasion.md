# Crimson Invasion (2017)

Set data for Crimson Invasion. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: November 3, 2017.
- Abbreviation: CIN. English set number: 74.
- Card count: 111 main set + 13 secret rares = 124.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex and the era template).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known. No Prism Star cards in this set.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra,
Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 22.2 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 75 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 120 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 7.7 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.6 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 3.0 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.2 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.2 full art Trainers and secret rares. Each of
the 13 secret rares prints 5 times on the shared sheet. That gives 0.78
secret rares per box.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Ultra Beast Pokémon-GX, with a red GX design (Bulbapedia).
- Fewer "regular" Pokémon-GX per box (3.0) than the earlier sets (4.0).
- The study guesses that Hydreigon and Kommo-o (theme deck exclusive
  holos) print 4 times on the rare sheet, and other rares 6 or 7 times.
  Confidence: community estimate (the author calls it a guess).
