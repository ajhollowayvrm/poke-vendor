# Crimson Invasion (2017)

Set data for Crimson Invasion. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: November 3, 2017.
- Abbreviation: CIN. English set number: 74.
- Card count: 111 main set + 13 secret rares = 124.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex and the era template).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known. No Prism Star cards in this set.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra,
Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 12 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 22.2 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 75 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 120 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 7.7 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.6 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 3.0 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.2 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.2 full art Trainers and secret rares. Each of
the 13 secret rares prints 5 times on the shared sheet. That gives 0.78
secret rares per box.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Ultra Beast Pokémon-GX, with a red GX design (Bulbapedia).
- Fewer "regular" Pokémon-GX per box (3.0) than the earlier sets (4.0).
- The study guesses that Hydreigon and Kommo-o (theme deck exclusive
  holos) print 4 times on the rare sheet, and other rares 6 or 7 times.
  Confidence: community estimate (the author calls it a guess).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: this map follows the pack structure and the ThePriceDex
odds above. The card list holds no Basic Energy card, so the Basic
Energy slot does not draw from it. TCGdex records Rare Holo GX and
Ultra Rare (full art) under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare
and Secret Rare under one rarity, Secret Rare. The card numbers tell
the four outcomes apart: Rare Holo GX is the first appearance of each
GX card, Ultra Rare is the full art repeat, Rainbow Rare is the second
repeat, and Secret Rare is the trailing Trainer and Energy cards. The
Rare outcome draws from the Rare rarity's Normal variant, all 33 cards;
13 of those also carry a Holo variant, used by the Rare Holo outcome.

## Open topics

- **Slot map:** TCGdex records Rare Holo GX and Ultra Rare (full art)
  under one rarity, Ultra Rare, and Rainbow Rare and Secret Rare under
  one rarity, Secret Rare. The slot map splits each pair by card
  number, from the repeating pattern in the card names. No source
  confirms the split.
- **Slot map:** the Ultra Rare Normal variant (19 cards) and the Secret
  Rare Normal variant (13 cards) stay unused. TCGplayer added these
  Normal prints to cards that pull only as Holo in a pack.
- **Slot map:** the Rare outcome draws from the Rare rarity's Normal
  variant, all 33 cards. 13 of those cards also carry a Holo variant,
  used by the Rare Holo outcome. The two outcomes share cards.
- Pack order: no source shows a real opened Crimson Invasion pack.
  Searched 2026-09-12 at the era level: no card-by-card video or
  photo source was found. See eras/sun-moon.md#orientation for the
  resolved raw-order vs pack-trick-order question.
- Bulbapedia says the Japanese sets Awakened Heroes and
  Ultradimensional Beasts guaranteed an SR or better per box. No source
  gives a guarantee for English Crimson Invasion boxes.
- The pack mix in the Three Pack Blister: Unknown.
- **Boxes per case: 6.** See the era file
  [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md#box-and-case-structure).
