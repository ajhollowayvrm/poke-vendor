## Pack structure

- 10 cards per pack. This follows the era template.
- 36 packs per booster box. Confidence: community box reports ("9 holos
  (out of 36 packs!!!)").
- Slots: 5 common, 3 uncommon, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare (community
  description).
- A Prime replaces the reverse holo (Bulbapedia, PokéBeach).
- The set list has no basic energy cards. It has Darkness Energy 79 and Metal
  Energy 80, which are special energy cards.
- One reported box opening counted these reverse holos: 7 rares, 1 holo rare
  card, and 2 Trainer cards. The reverse holo slot can hold any rarity.

## Pack order

This set follows the era pack order. Confidence: community claim.

| Card (front facing the player) | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common or uncommon, random order |
| 6 | Reverse holo, or a Prime |
| 7 | Rare slot: rare, holo rare, LEGEND half, or Alph Lithograph |
| 8–10 | Common or uncommon, random order |

- **The rare slot is card 7. It is not one of the last three cards.**
- A Prime or a reverse holo rare is card 6. It is not one of the last three
  cards.
- cardcollector.co.uk lists "HS Undaunted" by name as "3 cards to the back".
- NinePocket says this era has no reliable pack order. See the era file.

### Pack order in a box

One forum user logged a box opening video pack by pack. The user saw a
repeated pattern, "rare,rare,holo,rare,rare,legend,rare,rare,holo etc.", in
UD, UL, and HS boxes. Confidence: community observation, one user, no data
published.

### Box reports

These are self-reported counts from one sealed box each, posted in the
PokéBeach "Undaunted Pack Rates" thread in August 2010. Confidence:
empirical, small sample, not verified.

| Report | Primes | LEGEND halves | Holo rares | Alph Lithograph |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (YouTube opening, reported by a user) | 6 | 4 | 9 | No |
| 2 | 5 | 2 | 9 | Yes |
| 3 | 5 | 3 | 9 | No |
| 4 | 5 | 2 | 10 | No |
| 5 | 5 | 3 | 9 | No |
| 6 | 5 | 3 | 8 | Yes |
| 7 | 5 | 2 | 10 | Yes |
| 8 | 5 | 3 | 9 | No |
| 9 | 5 | 3 | 9 | Not stated |
| 10 | 5 | 2 | 9 | Yes |
| 11 | 5 | 3 | 9 | Not stated |
| 12 | 4 | 2 | Not stated | Yes |
| 13 | 5 | 2 | 10 | Not stated |

Summary of the 13 reports:

- Primes: 5 in 11 reports, 6 in 1, and 4 in 1. That is about 1 in 7 packs.
- LEGEND halves: 2 to 4 per box. That is about 1 in 12 to 1 in 18 packs.
- Holo rares: 8 to 10 per box. That is about 1 in 4 packs.
- Alph Lithograph: in 5 reports. 5 reports say no Alph. 3 reports do not
  say.

One user proposed a fixed box pattern: "either 5 primes, 9 holos, 3 legends
or 5 primes, 9 holos, 2 legends, alph lithograph". Report 1 and report 12 do
not match that pattern. Confidence: community hypothesis.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon LEGEND.** Kyogre & Groudon LEGEND and Rayquaza & Deoxys LEGEND.
- **Pokémon Prime.** Six Prime cards take the reverse holo slot.
- **Theme deck variants.** Bellossom 1, Espeon 2, and Umbreon 10 exist as
  non-holo cards. They come only from the Daybreak and Nightfall theme decks,
  not from booster packs (PokéBeach forum, 2011).

- Unknown: boxes per case.

- Conflict: Prime estimates are 1 in 6 or 1 in 7. Box reports support 5 per
  box.
- Conflict: Alph Lithograph estimates are 1 in 3 boxes. Box reports show it
  in 5 of 13 boxes, and 3 reports do not say. The sample is too small to
  decide.
- Report 1 and an earlier post can describe the same video. The thread does
  not make this clear.
- Some posts are not in the table because they give no counts ("All Primes,
  3 Legends, Alph"). One post lists 10 Primes and 3 Alph Lithographs for "a
  box", which is likely more than one box.
- Unknown: whether the box collation is fixed. The "5 primes, 9 holos"
  pattern is a hypothesis.
- Unknown: the pack split between Undaunted and Triumphant in each Prime
