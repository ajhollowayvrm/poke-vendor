## Pack structure

- 10 cards per pack. This follows the era template.
- 36 packs per booster box. Confidence: community box reports ("9 holos
  (out of 36 packs!!!)").
- Slots: 5 common, 3 uncommon, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare (community
  description).
- A Prime replaces the reverse holo (Bulbapedia, PokéBeach).
- The set list has no basic energy cards. It has Darkness Energy 79 and Metal
  Energy 80, which are special energy cards.
- One reported box opening counted these reverse holos: 7 rares, 1 holo rare
  card, and 2 Trainer cards. The reverse holo slot can hold any rarity.

## Pack order

This set follows the era pack order. Confidence: community claim.

| Card (front facing the player) | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Common or uncommon, random order |
| 6 | Reverse holo, or a Prime |
| 7 | Rare slot: rare, holo rare, LEGEND half, or Alph Lithograph |
| 8–10 | Common or uncommon, random order |

- **The rare slot is card 7. It is not one of the last three cards.**
- A Prime or a reverse holo rare is card 6. It is not one of the last three
  cards.
- cardcollector.co.uk lists "HS Undaunted" by name as "3 cards to the back".
- NinePocket says this era has no reliable pack order. See the era file.

### Pack order in a box

One forum user logged a box opening video pack by pack. The user saw a
repeated pattern, "rare,rare,holo,rare,rare,legend,rare,rare,holo etc.", in
UD, UL, and HS boxes. Confidence: community observation, one user, no data
published.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon LEGEND.** Kyogre & Groudon LEGEND and Rayquaza & Deoxys LEGEND.
- **Pokémon Prime.** Six Prime cards take the reverse holo slot.
- **Theme deck variants.** Bellossom 1, Espeon 2, and Umbreon 10 exist as
  non-holo cards. They come only from the Daybreak and Nightfall theme decks,
  not from booster packs (PokéBeach forum, 2011).

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

The rare slot holds the non-holo Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo LEGEND, and
Rare Secret outcomes; the reverse holo slot holds Rare Prime instead of
a plain reverse holo. This set has no basic Energy card; Darkness
Energy 79 and Metal Energy 80 are special energy cards, part of the
Uncommon pool.

Confidence: the slots follow the era template and the pack structure
above. The Rare Holo LEGEND odds are a range, not a single figure, so
the row uses `—`.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Rare Prime | Rare Prime | Rare PRIME | Holo | All | 1 in 7 |
| Reverse holo | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | All | Rest |

- The pack order is a community claim. Sources conflict. See the era file.

  existing box reports.

  box.
- Conflict: Alph Lithograph estimates are 1 in 3 boxes. Box reports show it
  in 5 of 13 boxes, and 3 reports do not say. The sample is too small to
  decide.
- Report 1 and an earlier post can describe the same video. The thread does
  not make this clear.
- Some posts are not in the table because they give no counts ("All Primes,
  3 Legends, Alph"). One post lists 10 Primes and 3 Alph Lithographs for "a
  box", which is likely more than one box.
- Unknown: whether the box collation is fixed. The "5 primes, 9 holos"
  pattern is a hypothesis.
- Unknown: the pack split between Undaunted and Triumphant in each Prime
  Challenge Box variant.
- **Slot map: Rare Holo LEGEND odds.** The source gives a range (1 in 9
  to 1 in 12), not a single figure. The row uses `—`.
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Holo uses the era estimate of 1 in 3, about 12 per box. Box reports for this set show 8 to 10 holo rares per box.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Prime uses 1 in 7 from PokeBeach Card Ratios' final post. An earlier version of the same post gives 1 in 6.
- **Rarity list order:** Rare Secret uses 1 in 108 from PokeBeach Card Ratios and Flipside Gaming. Box reports show it in 5 of 13 boxes, about 1 in 94 packs.
