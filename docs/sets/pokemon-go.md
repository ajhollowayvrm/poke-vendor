# Pokémon GO (2022)

Set data for Pokémon GO, a special expansion. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the approach. See
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md) for the era template and the
evidence behind the pack order.

## Release

- English release: July 1, 2022.
- Set code: PGO. Abbreviation: PGO. TCGplayer set code: PGO.
- Cards: 88 total. 78 in the main numbering, plus 10 secret rares.

## Pack structure

- Bulbapedia: "Each booster pack contained 10 cards." Asian packs had 6 cards.
- DigitalTQ: 10 cards, "1 energy card or VSTAR Marker and a code card you can
  use online."
- DigitalTQ: "Each pack has a guaranteed pull of at least one Rare Holo or
  above card." The Bulbapedia set list supports this: it has no plain Rare
  cards.
- Slots: Unknown. The era template gives 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse
  holo, 1 rare or higher.

## Pack order

**Confidence: community guide, low.** NinePocket lists Pokémon GO under the
Sun & Moon to Sword & Shield method. No other source was found.

| Position | Reveal starts at the commons end |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot (Rare Holo or higher) |
| 8 | Basic Energy or VSTAR marker |
| 9–11 | Uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare is **not** one of the last three cards in either direction.
- Which end faces the wrapper front: Unknown.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Radiant Venusaur, Radiant Charizard, and Radiant Blastoise.
- Hidden Ditto: Bulbapedia lists reverse holo Spinarak, Numel, and Bidoof
  with a "Hidden Ditto Stamp peel away sticker". DigitalTQ: packs "have a
  chance to contain the exclusive Ditto card".
- No English booster box. Asian markets had booster boxes.
