# Pokémon GO (2022)

Set data for Pokémon GO, a special expansion. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the approach. See
[eras/sword-shield.md](eras/sword-shield.md) for the era template and the
evidence behind the pack order.

## Release

- English release: July 1, 2022.
- Set code: PGO. Abbreviation: PGO. TCGplayer set code: PGO.
- Cards: 88 total. 78 in the main numbering, plus 10 secret rares.

## Pack structure

- Bulbapedia: "Each booster pack contained 10 cards." Asian packs had 6 cards.
- DigitalTQ: 10 cards, "1 energy card or VSTAR Marker and a code card you can
  use online."
- DigitalTQ: "Each pack has a guaranteed pull of at least one Rare Holo or
  above card." The Bulbapedia set list supports this: it has no plain Rare
  cards.
- Slots: Unknown. The era template gives 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse
  holo, 1 rare or higher.

## Pack order

**Confidence: community guide, low.** NinePocket lists Pokémon GO under the
Sun & Moon to Sword & Shield method. No other source was found.

| Position | Reveal starts at the commons end |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot (Rare Holo or higher) |
| 8 | Basic Energy or VSTAR marker |
| 9–11 | Uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- The rare is **not** one of the last three cards in either direction.
- Which end faces the wrapper front: Searched 2026-09-12: no source found (see the era file, which also resolves the code card's physical position).

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Radiant Venusaur, Radiant Charizard, and Radiant Blastoise.
- Hidden Ditto: Bulbapedia lists reverse holo Spinarak, Numel, and Bidoof
  with a "Hidden Ditto Stamp peel away sticker". DigitalTQ: packs "have a
  chance to contain the exclusive Ditto card".
- No English booster box. Asian markets had booster boxes.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

Confidence: the era template gives the slots. The set list has no plain
Rare cards, so the rare slot holds Rare Holo or higher. The Radiant
slot is Unknown; the map puts it in the reverse holo slot, matching
Astral Radiance and Crown Zenith. The DigitalTQ figures sum past 100%
even with Radiant moved out, so the map uses Rest for Rare Holo.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 5 | Common | Common | Common | Normal | All | 100% |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommon | Uncommon | Uncommon | Normal | All | 100% |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse holo | Reverse holo | Common, Uncommon, Holo Rare | Reverse holo | All | Rest |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Rare Radiant | Rare Radiant | Radiant Rare | Holo | All | 5.39% |
| Rare slot | 1 | Rare Holo | Rare Holo | Holo Rare | Holo | All | Rest |
