# Phantom Forces (2014)

Set-specific data for XY—Phantom Forces. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era.

## Release

- English release: November 5, 2014 (Bulbapedia, Limitless TCG).
- Code: PHF.
- Card count: 119 cards plus 3 secret rares (120/119, 121/119,
  122/119). Total 122.

## Pack structure

This set follows the XY-era template. Confidence: **community wiki and
community estimate**.

- 10 cards per pack, plus 1 code card.
- 1 rare slot, 1 reverse holo, 3 uncommons, 5 commons (ThePriceDex).

**Unverified exception.** CardGuide Wiki says: every booster contains
1 reverse foil, "though in 1:6 packs the reverse foil is replaced by a
Holo Energy card". This text was seen only as a search snippet. The
page returned HTTP 402. No other source confirms it. The ThePriceDex
reverse holo figures for this set add up to about 1.0 per pack, which
leaves no room for a holo Energy in 1 of 6 packs. Confidence:
**unverified community wiki**. Do not use it until a second source
confirms it.

## Pack order

This set follows the XY-era order. No source describes this set
separately. Confidence: **community guide, era-wide**.

1. Positions 1–5: five cards (inferred: commons).
2. Position 6: reverse holo.
3. Position 7: rare slot (the hit).
4. Positions 8–10: three cards (inferred: uncommons).
5. Position 11: code card.

The hit is **not** one of the last three cards. It is card 7 of 10.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo
EX, Rare Ultra, Rare Secret.

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Per box (36) | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | 24 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 5.1 | 7 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX | 1 in 10.9 | 3.3 | Community estimate |
| Ultra Rare (full art) | 1 in 25.8 | 1.4 | Community estimate |
| Secret Rare | 1 in 114.3 | 0.3 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better (derived) | about 1 in 3.0 | about 12 | Derived from the rows above |

All per-pack figures come from ThePriceDex. ThePriceDex notes that
"individual cards are assumed to have equal pull rates".

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 10.1 packs", Reverse Rare "1 in 6.4 packs", Reverse
Uncommon "1 in 3.3 packs", Reverse Common "1 in 2.3 packs".

Flipside Gaming gives a secret rare rate of 1:72 for the whole era
(**community estimate**).
