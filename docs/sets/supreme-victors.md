# Supreme Victors (2009)

Set data for Platinum—Supreme Victors. This set uses the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release: August 19, 2009. Japanese release: March 6, 2009, as
  Beat of the Frontier.
- Set number: PL3. Abbreviation: SV.
- Set symbol: Bulbapedia shows a set symbol image. This file does not
  describe it.
- Card count: 147. Secret cards: 3 (148/147 to 150/147). Shiny cards: 3
  (SH7 to SH9).

## Pack structure

- 10 cards: 5 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Reverse Holofoil, 1 rare slot. Source:
  Bulbapedia (era template). DA Card World: "36 packs per box, 10 cards per
  pack".
- The rare slot can hold a Rare, a Rare Holo, a Rare Holo LV.X, or a secret
  card. Confidence: community estimate (PokeBeach Card Ratios).
- **Exception:** the SH cards come in the reverse holo slot. Confidence:
  community estimate (PokeBeach Card Ratios).

## Pack order

The era template applies. Summary:

- The rare slot card is card 7 of 10. Three cards follow it. It is **not**
  one of the last three cards. Source: Card Collector ("3" cards to the
  back). Confidence: community guide, no method. The position is derived.
- NinePocket says no reliable order exists for packs from this period. The
  two sources conflict.
- Secret cards and LV.X cards sit in the rare slot position.
- **Exception:** an SH card is a hit in the reverse holo slot. Its position
  is Unknown.
- The common and uncommon order: Unknown. No code card. No separate Energy
  slot. Which end the cards face: Unknown.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- **Pokémon SP:** Pokémon C (Cynthia) and Pokémon FB (Frontier Brains)
  debut.
- **LV.X groups per box (claim):** a PokeBeach poster says a box has one of
  two groups. Group 1: Rayquaza C LV.X, Charizard G LV.X, Staraptor FB
  LV.X, Garchomp C LV.X. Group 2: Blaziken FB LV.X, Electivire FB LV.X,
  Absol G LV.X. Another poster reports a box that does not follow this
  pattern. Confidence: disputed anecdote.

## Slot map

How the game builds one pack from the card list. Each row is one
outcome of one slot. The game picks one outcome for each card in the
slot, then picks one card at random from the cards that match the row.

- **Slot** and **Count** come from the pack structure above.
- **Rarity list entry** links the outcome to the stop rule.
- **TCGdex rarity** and **Variant** match the card list exactly.
- **Cards** limits the matching cards: `All`, `Nos. a–b`, `Not nos. a–b`,
  `Part: <card list table>`, or `Category: <category>`.
- **Odds in slot** is the chance of the outcome for one card in the
  slot. `Rest` is the remainder. `—` means no source gives the odds.
- A variant that no row uses does not come from booster packs.

**The 3 secret cards (148–150) and the Shiny (SH7–SH9) cards share the
"Rare" TCGdex rarity with the non-secret Rares.** The secret cards use
the "Holo" variant; SH7–SH9 use "Reverse holo".

Confidence: this map follows the era template in
[eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md](eras/diamond-pearl-platinum.md), with
the set's own exception that Shiny (SH) cards come in the reverse holo
slot. The Rare Holo LV.X rate uses 1 in 9, the higher figure in the
file's "1 in 9 to 1 in 12" range (see this file's Open topics).

## Open topics

- Pack order: the two community guides conflict. No source shows real
  opened packs. The SH card position is Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: no
  source found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- The LV.X group claim is disputed. A box-opening study must confirm or
  reject it. Searched 2026-09-12: the PokeBeach thread repeats the same
  two groups, with no new confirmation or rejection.
- The names of the secret cards 148–150 and SH8–SH9: not recorded here.
- The non-holo rare rate. Boxes per case. Searched 2026-09-12: no source
  found (era-wide search, see the era file).
- **Rarity list odds:** no source gives the odds per pack for Rare. The era's rarity system decides the place of each one in the rarity list.
- **Rarity list order:** Reverse holo ranks above Rare. It fills a guaranteed slot every pack; no source gives the non-holo Rare rate.
- **Slot map:** the Rare Holo LV.X odds use 1 in 9, the higher end of
  the file's "1 in 9 to 1 in 12" range. The lower end (1 in 12) would
  give fewer LV.X pulls per box.
