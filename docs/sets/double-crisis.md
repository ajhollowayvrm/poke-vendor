# Double Crisis (2015)

Set-specific data for Double Crisis, a special set. See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for what a set file covers. See
[eras/xy.md](eras/xy.md) for the pack template of this era. This set
**breaks the template**.

## Release

- English release: March 25, 2015 (Bulbapedia, Pokémon.com, Limitless
  TCG). Japanese release: January 30, 2015.
- Code: DCR.
- Card count: 34 cards. No secret rares.

## Pack structure

- **7 cards per pack.** Pokémon.com: "4 Pokémon TCG: Double Crisis
  7-card booster packs". Confidence: **official**.
- **2 foil cards per pack.** Pokémon.com: "28 cards in all, including
  8 foil cards". That is 8 foils in 4 packs. Confidence: **official**
  for the blister total. The per-pack split is derived.
- **Slot breakdown: 3 commons, 2 uncommons, 1 holo rare or EX, 1
  reverse holo.** ThePriceDex gives "3 cards per pack" common and "2
  cards per pack" uncommon. Its holo and EX figures add up to about 1
  per pack, and its reverse figures add up to about 1 per pack.
  Confidence: **community estimate**, consistent with the official
  foil count.
- There is no non-holo rare. Bulbapedia lists only Common, Uncommon,
  Rare Holo, and Rare Holo EX.
- A code card is in the blister (Pokémon.com). Whether each pack also
  has a code card is Unknown.

## Pack order

**Unknown.** No source describes the card order in a Double Crisis
pack. The XY-era method (move 3 cards) is for 10-card packs. Do not
apply it here without a source.

Until a source is found, the rip screen has no real order for this
set. The game default (hit in the last three cards) is a placeholder
only.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities (Bulbapedia): Common, Uncommon, Rare Holo, Rare Holo EX.

| Rarity (rare slot) | Per pack | Confidence |
|--------------------|----------|------------|
| Rare Holo | 1 in 1.1 | Community estimate |
| Rare Holo EX ("Ultra Rare" on ThePriceDex) | 1 in 9 | Community estimate |
| Holo rare or better | Every pack | Official (derived from 8 foils in 4 packs) |

Reverse holo slot (ThePriceDex, **community estimate**): Reverse Rare
Holo "1 in 5.3 packs", Reverse Uncommon "1 in 2.7 packs", Reverse
Common "1 in 2.3 packs".

Under the game's hit rule (rare or higher), every pack has at least
one hit.

## Special subsets and mechanics

- A special set about Team Aqua and Team Magma (Pokémon.com: "34 cards
  featuring the Pokémon and people of Team Aqua and Team Magma").
- Bulbapedia: the cards show the Team logo on the attack box and a
  grunt headshot.
- Bulbapedia: "Double Crisis booster packs could not be purchased
  individually from booster boxes."
