# Shining Legends (2017)

Set data for Shining Legends, a special expansion with no booster boxes.
The era template is in [eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See
[../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md) for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: October 6, 2017.
- Abbreviation: SLG. No English set number ("special expansion").
- Card count: 73 main set + 5 secret rares = 78.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 cards per pack, "with 2 guaranteed foil cards" (Bulbapedia).

| Slot | Count | Contents |
|------|-------|----------|
| Common | 5 | Commons |
| Uncommon | 3 | Uncommons |
| Reverse holo slot | 1 | Reverse holo commons, uncommons, holo rares, or Basic Energy |
| Rare slot | 1 | Holo rare or higher. The set has no non-holo rares. |
| Basic Energy | 1 | Basic Energy |
| Code card | Unknown | Bulbapedia does not say |

- Sources: Bulbapedia (11 cards, 2 foils); ThePriceDex (5 commons,
  3 uncommons, 1 Energy); Elite Fourum post 144 (no non-holo rares; all
  hits in the rare slot; reverse Basic Energy on the reverse sheet).
- Confidence: well documented for the 11 cards. Code card: Unknown.

## Pack order

**Not confirmed for this set.** The pack-trick guides do not name
Shining Legends. If the era template applies, the order is:

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card (if present) |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw template order: no,
  the rare slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide for the era; not confirmed for this set.
- Exception: the rare slot always holds a holo rare or higher.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra, Rare
Shining, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare Holo | 1 in 1.4 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 9 | Empirical study |
| Rare Shining | 1 in 11.3 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 24.2 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 49.5 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 197.8 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 5.4 | Estimate |
| Reverse Energy (reverse slot) | 1 in 14.3 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum model (per 36 packs, for comparison only): 4.0 "regular"
Pokémon-GX, 1.0 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.4 full art Trainers and secret
rares, 3.2 Shining Pokémon. Pokémon Breeder (the only full art Trainer)
prints 35 times on its sheet. Each of the 5 secret rares prints 13
times. Data: the author's own small sample (an outlier), the @burpies
data, and two Derium videos of about 501 packs each.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- Shining Pokémon: a special card type with different coloration
  (Bulbapedia). They come in the rare slot.
- "MewTube" (the Test Tube Mewtwo secret rare) appeared more often in the
  sample. The author thinks the sample is too small to call this real.

## Open topics

- Pack order for this set: not confirmed by any source.
- Code card in the pack: Unknown. Searched 2026-09-12: Bulbapedia states
  only "11 cards, with 2 guaranteed foil cards" and does not mention a
  code card. No source found.
- Shining Pokémon count: the Elite Fourum post says 6 Shining Pokémon.
  Bulbapedia lists 5 secret rares. These are different card groups, but
  the exact Shining Pokémon list was not checked.
- The three data samples do not agree well. The Shining Pokémon ratio
  comes only from these samples.
- Derium's first video had 29 reverse Energy cards, and the model
  expects about 37. The author treats this as noise.
