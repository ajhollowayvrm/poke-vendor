## Pack structure

- **10 game cards per pack.** Confidence: official rule, via Bulbapedia.
- **Slots:** 5 commons, 1 reverse holo, 1 rare slot, 3 uncommons.
  Confidence: community template for the era. No source covers this set
  by name.
- **Code card: depends on the print run.** Bulbapedia says "The first
  print run of Black & White did not include the QR code cards, and was
  made available in later print runs." A first-print pack has 10 cards
  and no code card.
- **No basic Energy card.** Energy cards in packs started in Sun & Moon.

- **Where the hit sits:** card 7 of 10 in the listed order, or card 4 of
  10 in reverse order. The hit is **not** one of the last three cards.

- **Confidence:** Unknown for this set. The order comes from a 2013

## Special mechanics

- Black & White introduced Abilities and Full Art cards.
- All Pokémon in the set are Generation V, except the secret Pikachu.
- The Emerging Powers page says its new reverse holo pattern replaced
  "the plain Mirror Holofoil pattern". So Black & White reverse holos
  use the plain Mirror pattern.
