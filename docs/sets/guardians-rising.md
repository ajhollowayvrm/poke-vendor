# Guardians Rising (2017)

Set data for Guardians Rising. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: May 5, 2017.
- Abbreviation: GRI. English set number: 72.
- Card count: 145 main set + 24 secret rares = 169.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex gives 5 commons, 3 uncommons,
  and 1 Energy; the era template gives the other slots).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known. No Prism Star cards in this set.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX, Rare Ultra,
Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6.4 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 9 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 23.4 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 66.7 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 111.1 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 7.9 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.7 | Estimate |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set. Searched 2026-09-12: ThePriceDex now gives reverse slot figures
for this set (see the two rows above).

Elite Fourum model: all 24 secret rares print 3 times on the 10x10 full
art Trainer and secret rare sheet. That is 72 of 100 slots, and 0.86
secret rares per 36 packs. Each secret rare is 1 in 1,000 packs. A video
of real print sheets shows part of this sheet and supports the model.
The Derium data comes from three large opening videos.

## Special subsets or mechanics

- 24 secret rares, the most of the early Sun & Moon sets.
- Lunala and Solgaleo (non-holo rares with theme deck exclusive holo
  versions) appear short printed. The study estimates 3 copies each on
  the sheet. Confidence: empirical study.
