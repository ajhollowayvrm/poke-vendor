# Unbroken Bonds (2019)

Set data for Unbroken Bonds. The era template is in
[eras/sun-moon.md](eras/sun-moon.md). See [../13-sets.md](../13-sets.md)
for the general approach.

## Release

- English release date: May 3, 2019.
- Abbreviation: UNB. English set number: 80.
- Card count: 214 main set + 20 secret rares = 234.
- Source: Bulbapedia.

## Pack structure

11 game cards plus 1 code card: 5 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 reverse holo
slot, 1 rare slot, 1 Basic Energy, 1 code card.

- This set has no Prism Star cards (Elite Fourum post 153). The reverse
  slot holds only reverse holos.
- Confidence: well documented (ThePriceDex, era template).

## Pack order

This set follows the era template. No source gives a set-specific
order.

| Position | Card |
|----------|------|
| 1–5 | 5 commons |
| 6 | Reverse holo slot |
| 7 | Rare slot |
| 8 | Basic Energy |
| 9–11 | 3 uncommons |
| 12 | Code card |

- **Is the hit one of the last three cards?** Raw order: no, the rare
  slot is card 7. Pack-trick order: yes, the rare slot is last.
- Confidence: community guide (NinePocket, PokéPatch), for the whole
  era group.
- Exceptions: none known.

## Rarities and hit odds

Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo, Rare Holo GX (includes TAG
TEAM Pokémon-GX), Rare Ultra, Rare Rainbow, Rare Secret.

| Rarity | Odds per pack | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Rare (non-holo) | 1 in 1.5 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo | 1 in 6 | Empirical study |
| Rare Holo GX | 1 in 10 | Empirical study |
| Ultra Rare (full art Pokémon-GX + full art Trainer) | 1 in 24.2 | Empirical study |
| Rainbow Rare | 1 in 56.5 | Empirical study |
| Secret Rare (gold) | 1 in 131.9 | Empirical study |
| Reverse Rare Holo (reverse slot) | 1 in 8 | Estimate |
| Reverse Rare (reverse slot) | 1 in 4.5 | Estimate |
| TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX (any version) | Unknown | Unknown |

Odds source: ThePriceDex, which cites the Elite Fourum post for this
set.

Elite Fourum box ratios (per 36 packs): 3.6 "regular" Pokémon-GX,
1.1 full art Pokémon-GX, 1.3 full art Trainers and secret rares. On the
shared sheet, the 6 full art Trainers print 5 times, 10 secret rares
print 4 times, and 10 print 3 times. Data includes Derium (1,080 packs)
and TheGameCapital (2,592 packs).

## Special subsets or mechanics

- TAG TEAM Pokémon-GX continue from Team Up.
- Mewtwo and Zeraora (theme deck exclusive holos) are short printed.
- Butterfree, Meowstic, Persian, Purugly, Raichu, and Wigglytuff are the
  6 rares at the "R4" weight. Confidence: empirical study.
