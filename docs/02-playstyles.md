# Playstyles

Four archetypes, fully combinable. You are never told "you are a Sourcer
now" — every day you pick from any of these, and most successful play mixes
all four. Each has its own skill, risk, and reward profile.

## The Sourcer (garage sales / estate sales)

- **Skill**: spotting value in unsorted lots, haggling with sellers who do
  not know card values.
- **Risk**: time cost, dead ends, driving distance, competing sourcers at the
  same sale. Can bust — see
  [01-premise-and-loop.md](01-premise-and-loop.md#variance-and-busts).
- **Reward**: cheapest possible cost basis, occasional huge hits (a vintage
  holo in a shoebox).

## The Flipper (meets and shows)

- **Skill**: reading the room, fast math on trade value, knowing which cards
  move quickly versus sit.
- **Risk**: cash-flow crunch (money tied up in slow inventory), other
  flippers undercutting your prices.
- **Reward**: fast, repeatable small margins. Builds capital steadily rather
  than in spikes.

## The Creator (social media)

- **Skill**: content timing, personality, knowing what pulls or takes go
  viral.
- **Risk**: burnout, algorithm luck, hype you cannot back up with real stock,
  follower backlash if you scam or flake.
- **Reward**: an audience becomes your customer base and your
  price-discovery engine. Sponsorships and live-stream income before you have
  a shop at all.
- Full detail: [06-social-media.md](06-social-media.md).

## The Grinder (packs and singles arbitrage)

- **Skill**: set knowledge, pull-rate math, tracking price trends.
- **Risk**: pure gambling variance on packs; arbitrage margins thin out as
  you scale.
- **Reward**: the most "game-like" path, appeals to players who want odds
  and probability play.
- Full detail on getting the product itself:
  [12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md).

## Cross-pollination

The archetypes are meant to feed each other, not stay siloed:

- A garage-sale find becomes a social post, which builds followers, which
  lets you sell a flip faster, which funds a bigger meet buy.
- A player can "make it" using only one archetype (e.g., a hermit who never
  posts and never leaves the house, hoarding collection value), or by
  combining all four. Combining all four is the more efficient route, but
  never the only one.
