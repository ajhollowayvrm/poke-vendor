# Reputation and Followers: What They Unlock

Both currencies unlock "access to more things," but they gate different
kinds of access because they represent different audiences: reputation is
your standing with the in-person trading world, followers are your standing
with an anonymous online crowd.

- **Reputation unlocks exclusivity and quality.**
- **Followers unlock speed and scale.**

## Reputation tiers

Five tiers, each strictly gating one set of unlocks. A player enters at
Unknown and only moves up through fair dealing — no cash purchase, no
follower count, moves this track.

| Tier | Name | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Unknown | Public show tables and open meets only. Starting point. |
| 1 | Known | Invite-only trading circles start including you. |
| 2 | Trusted | First look at new vendor stock before it hits the public table. Better table location at limited-badge shows. |
| 3 | Respected | Distributor/wholesale access at true wholesale pricing (see [12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md)). Invitations into case splits. Other traders start proactively bringing you pulls they know you want. |
| 4 | Elite | Priority case allocation on the rarest splits. Private consignment offers — someone's whole vintage collection, offered directly before it ever reaches a public sale. |

This resolves the open question in
[12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md#open-topics): wholesale
and case-split access share the same reputation track as meet access, and
sit at tier 3 (Respected), not a separate threshold.

Standing with a local game shop is not part of this track. Each game
shop keeps its own standing with the player. Big retail stores and the
Pokemon Center have no standing (see
[12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md#the-local-game-shop)).

## Follower tiers

Five tiers, read directly off the raw follower count the social media
system already tracks (see [06-social-media.md](06-social-media.md)). No
new mechanic — the count already grows through reach and shrinks through
decay; these tiers just mark thresholds on it.

| Tier | Followers | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | Baseline reach only. Nothing gated yet. |
| 1 | 1,000 | First sponsorship offers, small/niche brands. Crowdsourced tips start appearing — followers DM you about garage sales or local finds. |
| 2 | 10,000 | Sellers and other collectors start noticing you: better trade deals, free product, and first offers on exclusive items. This does not change Pokemon Center drop odds, which are the same for every player (see [12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md#pokemon-center-drops)). |
| 3 | 100,000 | Larger sponsorship deals. Reach measurably speeds up any sale. |
| 4 | 1,000,000 | Your own hype becomes a market force — posting about a card can move its price. |

Because followers decay (see
[06-social-media.md](06-social-media.md#growth-and-monetization)), a
player can drop out of a tier, not just rise through it — an inactive or
scandal-hit account can lose sponsorship access it already had.

## How each track moves

- **Reputation** rises through fair dealing: completed trades, accurate
  grading calls, delivering as promised. It falls through backlash that
  spills over from the social media system (see
  [06-social-media.md](06-social-media.md#consequences)), or through
  scams. The exact reputation-point value of each action is not yet set
  — a balancing task, not a research task, same as the archetype-formula
  coefficients in
  [11-card-archetypes-and-scaling.md](11-card-archetypes-and-scaling.md#open-topics).
- **Followers** rise and fall entirely through the existing social media
  reach/decay formulas — nothing new to design here.

## Status

Tiering is now designed: five tiers per track, with unlocks assigned to
each. What is still open: the exact reputation-point cost/reward of each
action, and the exact reach numbers behind follower growth/decay — both
balancing passes, not design gaps.
