# Reputation and Followers: What They Unlock

Both currencies unlock "access to more things," but they gate different
kinds of access because they represent different audiences: reputation is
your standing with the in-person trading world, followers are your standing
with an anonymous online crowd.

- **Reputation unlocks exclusivity and quality.**
- **Followers unlock speed and scale.**

## Reputation unlocks (in-person, relationship-based)

- Invitations to meets that are not publicly listed — invite-only trading
  circles.
- First look at new vendor stock before it hits the public table.
- Access to limited-badge shows, or a better table location.
- Other traders proactively bringing you pulls they know you want.
- Distributor/wholesale access and invitations into case splits — see
  [12-acquiring-product.md](12-acquiring-product.md) — gated the same
  way as meets, since both are about being trusted, not about reach.
- At the top end: private consignment offers — someone's whole vintage
  collection, offered directly before it ever reaches a public sale.

## Follower unlocks (online, scale-based)

- More reach per post — any listing or content sells faster.
- Early access to bigger drops, as brands or resellers start noticing you.
- Sponsorship and collab offers past follower thresholds.
- Crowdsourced tips — followers DM you about garage sales or local finds.
- At the top end: your own hype becomes a market force — you can move a
  card's price just by posting about it.
