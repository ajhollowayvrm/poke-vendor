# Upgrades

**Status: built.** The Upgrades screen (hub, Today card) lists every
upgrade that the player buys once. Upgrades that belong to one place
are on that place's screen, and the "On other screens" box on the
Upgrades screen links to them. Upgrades are cash only.

## What an upgrade is

A one-time purchase that permanently improves what the player can do.
It is not a consumable, a subscription, or a fee for each use. Stock,
entry fees, rent, wages, and insurance premiums are recurring costs,
not upgrades.

- An upgrade can need another upgrade first. The cargo van needs the
  better car, the camera kit needs the studio lights, and the big
  contact book needs the contact book. The hand truck needs the folding
  cart, the overhead camera needs the camera kit, and the reprint radar
  needs the price tracker app. The row on the Upgrades screen says so.
- An upgrade with a number states the number in its text, for example
  "Shipping costs 30% less on every sale that ships".

## Systems with no upgrades

Ripping, haggling, messages, debt, taxes, and the job have no upgrades
on purpose. They are systems of skill, story, or obligation. The
seller's permit is a license (see [26-taxes.md](26-taxes.md)).
