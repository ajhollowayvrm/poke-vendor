# Currencies

Four independent progress tracks. Each is a legitimate, standalone route to
"making it" (see [01-premise-and-loop.md](01-premise-and-loop.md)). They are
never directly convertible into each other — they connect only through what
they unlock. You cannot spend followers to buy reputation, for example.

## Cash

Pure spending power. Buys inventory, packs, grading slots, and eventually a
storefront lease.

## Followers / clout

Social reach, built through the unified social media system (see
[06-social-media.md](06-social-media.md)). Lets you move product fast, get
sponsor deals, and get early tips on hyped drops. Can be built with zero
cash — a good post is free.

## Reputation

Trust among sellers and buyers at meets and shows. Unlocks better deals,
first dibs on rare consignments, invites to private trades. Built through
fair dealing, not money. See
[04-reputation-and-followers-unlocks.md](04-reputation-and-followers-unlocks.md).

## Collection value

The raw worth of cards you personally hold, separate from cash. A player can
sit on a vault of rare cards and never sell them, and that is still "making
it."

## Why separate, why this matters

Each currency has its own visible number and its own way to grow, so very
different playstyles all look and feel legitimate:

- **Hermit grader**: no followers, no reputation, just quietly sourcing and
  hoarding until collection value is huge.
- **Clout chaser**: barely any cash, huge followers, income through hype and
  sponsorships.
- **Old-school trader**: no social media at all, pure reputation and
  meet-to-meet trading.
- **Balanced hustler**: uses all four. The fastest route, but never the only
  one.

Keeping the currencies separate, connected only through unlocks, avoids the
game collapsing into a single spreadsheet number to maximize.
