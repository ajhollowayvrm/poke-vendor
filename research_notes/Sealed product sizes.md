# Sealed product sizes

Research date: 2026-11-21. English product only. All sizes are outer sizes in inches (L × W × H). Volume is in cubic inches (in³).

## Summary

- A modern booster box is **smaller** than an ETB. A box is about 72 in³. An ETB is about 171 in³. The ETB is 2.4 times the volume of the box.
- The booster box size is almost the same in every era. A WOTC box (131 × 121 × 74 mm) and a modern box (about 5.25 × 4.75 × 2.9 in) have almost the same volume. One acrylic case fits every English box from June 2003 to now.
- The ETB size did not change from XY to Mega Evolution. Retailers list XY, SM, SWSH, SV, and ME ETBs at about 7.4–7.5 × 6.5–6.8 × 3.4–3.5 in. I found no source for a different XY or SM ETB size. See "Open questions".
- The Pokémon Center ETB has the same outer size as a regular ETB. The Crown Zenith Pokémon Center ETB Plus is a little longer (case interior 8.74 × 6.65 × 3.50 in).
- The big era change is in tins. A 2010 tin is about 80 in³. A tin from XY onward is about 120 in³.
- Many product kinds did not exist in older eras. The ETB starts with Plasma Storm (2013, BW). The booster bundle, the Build & Battle box, the mini tin, and the stacking tin are modern only.

## Proposed carry-space scale

### The problem with a straight volume unit

A straight unit (space = volume ÷ unit) cannot put both a modern box and a modern ETB at 3 to 5. The real ETB is 2.4 times the box.

- Unit 28 in³: box 3, ETB 6. The tote (8) holds one ETB only.
- Unit 35 in³: box 2, ETB 5. A bundle, a blister, and a Build & Battle box all become 1, the same as one pack.

### Recommended rule: square root of volume

**space = max(1, round(√(volume ÷ 8 in³)))**

- A pack is 1.
- A box is 3. An ETB is 5. Both are in the 3 to 5 band, and the box is smaller than the ETB.
- Small products get a real step: a blister, a bundle, and a Build & Battle box are 2.
- Big products do not explode: an Ultra-Premium Collection is 7. A Super-Premium Collection is 9, so it needs the folding cart.
- For a "[Set of N]" listing, take the space of one unit, then multiply by N. Do not take the root of the total.
- Keep the current sealed-case rule (`casePacksPerSpace`) as it is. The root rule is for single products.

### Gameplay check

- Tote (8): one ETB and one box (5 + 3). Or two boxes and a bundle (3 + 3 + 2). Or eight loose packs.
- Folding cart (30): six ETBs. Or ten boxes. Or four Ultra-Premium Collections.
- A box gets much easier to carry (7 to 3). Pack density in the tote goes up. A tote with two boxes holds 72 packs, against 36 today. If this is too strong, use a divisor of 6 in³ in place of 8: then a box is 3, an ETB is 5, a tin is 4, and an Ultra-Premium Collection is 9 (√(72/6) = 3.46 → 3, √(171/6) = 5.3 → 5).
- Collections get much bigger (2 to 5). This matches the real product. An ex Box is a flat window box that is as big as an ETB.

### Era rule

For the game, one size per kind is enough for every era except the standard tin. Use these era changes only:

- Standard tin: 3 before XY (2014), 4 from XY on.
- Booster pack: WOTC packs are 115 mm long, not 120 mm. The space is 1 in both cases. No rule is necessary.
- WOTC e-card boxes (Expedition, Aquapolis, Skyridge) are shorter than other WOTC boxes. A collector reports this. The space is still 3.

## Open questions

1. AJ said that XY and SM ETBs had a different size. I found no source for this. Retailers list XY base, XY Evolutions, Celestial Storm, and Crimson Invasion ETBs at 7.5 × 6.5 × 3.25–3.5 in. This is the same as SV and ME. The only difference I found is in the box style (the lid). One retailer lists the XY Phantom Forces ETB at 8 × 5 × 3 in. This number is probably a data error.
2. I found no outer size for a BW ETB (Plasma Storm, Plasma Blast, Boundaries Crossed). The BW value is an estimate.
3. Retailer sizes for the booster bundle and the Build & Battle box (about 4.3 × 3 × 2 in) are shorter than a 4.7 in pack. The real box can be about 4.7 in long. The space stays 2 in both cases.
4. Blister depth is uncertain (0.6 to 1.0 in). The space stays 2 in both cases.
