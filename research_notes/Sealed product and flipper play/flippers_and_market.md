# Pokemon TCG Sealed-Product Flipping and Scalping: Culture and Market Dynamics (2020 to November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-20. Scope: English/US market. Prices are USD. Many price pages are tracker, seller, or affiliate sites. These sites often disagree. Each figure has its date where the source gives one.

## 1. Flipper workflow, sales channels, margins, and fees

### Takeaway
A flipper buys at MSRP through restock alerts, invite systems, and store drops, then lists on eBay or TCGplayer. Platform fees take about 10% to 15% of the sale, so a small price rise can become almost no profit after fees and shipping.

### Cited Findings
- Paid Discord "cook groups" sell restock alerts for Pokemon Center, Target, Best Buy, and "200+ stores". These pages are vendor marketing. — [Cook Groups](https://cook-groups.com/)
- Reseller memberships and courses exist as products. Examples: a $69.99 per month group and a $20 course on Gumroad. — [Gumroad: kickflips](https://caseywoodard.gumroad.com/l/kickflips); [Gumroad: reselling guide](https://eloquence073.gumroad.com/l/bjgbl)
- Bots buy new expansions online at release. In September 2025, a scalper group bought over 42,000 Phantasmal Flames items from Pokemon Center. Reports say a site error let bots buy before the listings went live. Pokemon Center cancelled the orders. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/card-games/let-the-criers-cry-42-000-pokemon-tcg-phantasmal-flames-products-have-been-swiped-by-a-scalper-group-thanks-to-a-massive-error-on-the-pokemon-center); [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/the-pokemon-company-just-saved-fans-from-a-massive-42000-item-scalping-tcg-nightmare/); [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/scalpers-phantasmal-flames-preorders)
- Best Buy sold the Prismatic Evolutions ETB at $49.99 only through an invite system with a 24-hour purchase window. Best Buy warned that not every person who asks gets an invite. — [TechYorker](https://techyorker.com/best-buy-is-selling-pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolutions-etbs-at-msrp-but-youll-need-an-invite/)
- One flipper on a Discord server said that they bought over 2,000 units from Pokemon Center, Walmart, Amazon, Target, and Best Buy drops (September 2026, 30th Celebration). — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Some flippers weigh sealed packs in stores to find packs with heavy foil or rare cards. GameStop said in November 2025 that it will permanently ban US customers who weigh packs. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/gamestop-bans-pokémon-scalpers-using-scales-to-detect-rare-packs)
- eBay fees for trading cards: sources disagree. One source gives 13.25%. Another source gives 12.9% + $0.30. A third source gives "about 13-15%" plus shipping. — [PokeTop10 fees guide](https://poketop10.com/guides/marketplace-fees-explained); [Underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-pokemon-cards); [PokemonPriceTracker flipping guide](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-flipping-guide-buy-low-sell-high-in-2026)
- TCGplayer fees: one source gives 10.75% + 2.5% + $0.30 after February 2026. Another source gives 10.25% + $0.30. A third source gives about 9% to 12%. The fee applies to the order total, including shipping and tax. — [PokeTop10 fees guide](https://poketop10.com/guides/marketplace-fees-explained); [Underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-pokemon-cards)
- One guide shows a 30% price rise that becomes about 4% profit after fees and shipping. — [Underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-pokemon-cards)
- Common practice: sell singles on TCGplayer, and sell sealed or graded product on eBay. One guide tells flippers to list 10% to 15% above MSRP right after a restock buy. Another guide says wait 60 to 90 days after release for sold prices to become stable. — [Underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-pokemon-cards); [DealFlip](https://dealflip.ai/resources/pokemon-card-flipping-2026); [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/how-to-resell-pokemon)
- One flipper said that they used the full limit of three credit cards to buy stock (September 2026). — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)

### Inferences
- Game model: a flipper NPC has four stages. These are "get alert", "buy at MSRP (limit per person)", "hold", and "list with a fee of about 13%". A player flipper must pay a fee on each sale, so a small gain over MSRP is not profit.
- Credit-card funding means that some flippers must sell fast when prices fall. This gives forced selling, which pushes prices down more.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable data on the typical monthly income of a flipper.
- I found no reliable source on Facebook Marketplace, Mercari, or Whatnot fees for Pokemon sealed product.
- I found no reliable data on bot software prices or bot success rates.

## 2. Price cycles for hyped products (MSRP, peak, later)

### Takeaway
Hyped modern products often sell for 2x to 5x MSRP at release, then fall 25% to 50% when a reprint arrives. Many of them climb again after the reprint stock sells through. Old out-of-print sets climb slowly over years. In 2026, the 30th Celebration first wave crashed fast because of a large print run.

### Cited Findings
**Prismatic Evolutions (released January 2025)**
- ETB MSRP is $49.99. Some sources list $54.99. — [PokemonPriceTracker 2026 guide](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/prismatic-evolutions-price-guide-2026-market-update); [player.one](https://www.player.one/pokemon-tcg-extremely-rare-etb-now-back-stock-available-below-market-price-163487)
- The ETB sold for more than $250 at launch. — [PokemonPriceTracker 2026 guide](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/prismatic-evolutions-price-guide-2026-market-update)
- Packs with a retail price that sources give as $27 (per bundle) resold for over $70 (January 2025). — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/the-pokemon-company-addresses-prismatic-evolutions-and-tcg-shortages-as-scalpers-feast-we-are-actively-working-to-print-more/)
- ETB price during the 2025 restock: $135.79 (1 April 2025), $118.06 (1 May 2025), $95.90 (9 June 2025). This is a 29% fall. The price then rose to $212.83 (23 March 2026) and fell to $140.40 (25 September 2026), 34% below the peak. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)

- A 2026 tracker lists other Prismatic products: Super Premium Collection (MSRP $119.99) at $475 to $575; Surprise Box (MSRP $29.99) at $85 to $110; Booster Bundle at $110 to $135; loose packs at $22 to $28. — [PokemonPriceTracker 2026 guide](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/prismatic-evolutions-price-guide-2026-market-update)

- The booster box fell 10.7% from its 2026 peak by 25 September 2026. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)

**Mega Evolution era and 30th Celebration (2025-2026)**

- The Mega Gengar ex single fell about $225 to about $1,077 in one TCGplayer update (August 2026). — [TCGplayer seller blog](https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/price-trends-pokémon-cards-dropping-in-price-08-18-2026)
- Chaos Rising and Pitch Black sealed product prices fell on the secondary market from late July 2026. They still sold above MSRP, but the margin for holders "seems to be steadily deteriorating". — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/print/article/63244)
- 30th Celebration first wave (September 2026): singles rose for a short time, then SIR cards fell about 40% to 50%. Some Pikachu illustration cards fell over 80%. **Older reprint precedents**
- Hidden Fates ETB (third print in December 2020) traded at $137 to $186 from 2022 to late 2024. In 2026 it is $564.69. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- Celebrations ETB (Walmart restocks in 2021) was about $50 in November-December 2021 and $54 to $81 from April 2022 to October 2024. In 2026 it is $359.35. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)

**Production volume**
- TPC produced over 85 billion cards from March 2025 to March 2026. This is about 10 billion more than the year before. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)

### Inferences
- A good game price curve for a hyped product: release spike (2x to 5x MSRP), reprint dip (minus 25% to 50%, often to 1.5x to 2x MSRP), a flat period while stock stays in stores, then a slow climb after the product goes out of print.
- Old sets (Hidden Fates, Celebrations) stayed flat for about 3 years before they climbed. A player who holds must wait a long time. This is the cost of a long hold.
- Presale prices can be far too high. The Destined Rivals ETB fell from $349.99 to $80.99 in about 3 months.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable MSRP-to-peak series for Crown Zenith and Surging Sparks at release.
- I found no reliable sealed prices for the Mega Evolution base set or Ascended Heroes.
- The 30th Celebration article gives singles drops, not ETB price numbers.

## 3. Flipper losses and "bag holding"

### Takeaway
Reprint waves and large print runs cause flipper losses. The clearest 2026 case is the 30th Celebration first wave. Flippers who bought in volume with credit could not sell at a profit or return the stock.

### Cited Findings
- 30th Celebration (September 2026): one flipper bought over 2,000 units. Another flipper used the full limit of three credit cards and could not sell at a profit or return the stock to local stores. A third flipper talked about legal action because TPC does not publish print numbers or pull rates. ICv2 notes that some posts may be satire. — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Flippers called themselves "secondary distributors" and said that they supported the brand by distributing product. Fans mocked them as "parasites". — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Retailers expected a large print run from the preorder numbers. ICv2 cites a two-year reprint cycle. — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Prismatic Evolutions scalpers started to lose money after TPC promised reprints (early 2025). — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolution-scalpers-are-starting-to-hurt-thanks-to-the-promise-of-reprints-despite-the-current-lack-of-supply)
- Destined Rivals ETB buyers at the $349.99 presale price saw a low of $80.99 by June 2025. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- A Prismatic Evolutions ETB was reported at $43.85 on Amazon, below its MSRP (date not clear). — [Archyde](https://www.archyde.com/pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolutions-elite-trainer-box-drops-to-43-85-on-amazon/)
- Many recent SIRs that spiked at release now sell at 40% to 60% of their peak. The tracker says quick flips are unlikely to return as a strategy. — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- Booster boxes that sold for $200+ are now "closer to MSRP" (March 2026). — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- After the holidays, prices historically fall another 15% to 25% when gift recipients sell unwanted cards. — [PokemonPriceTracker bubble article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-bubble-2026-market-crash-or-correction)

### Inferences
- A flipper loss event in the game can have three causes: a reprint announcement, a large first wave, and the post-holiday sell-off.
- A flipper who buys at MSRP rarely loses below MSRP. The large loss comes to a buyer who paid a secondary price (for example, a presale price).
- A flipper with stock that cannot be returned must hold or sell at a loss. Store return rules make this worse (see section 8).

### Gaps
- I found no total dollar loss figures for any flipper or for flippers as a group.

## 4. Sealed-as-investment ("PokeInvesting") culture and long-hold returns

### Takeaway
Many adult buyers hold sealed boxes as an investment. Out-of-print sets show large long-term gains (Evolving Skies at more than 15x MSRP). Modern sets with large print runs gave weaker and slower returns in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Pokemon cards rose 3,821% since 2004. The S&P 500 rose 483% in the same period. This is a tracker-site claim. — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- Card Ladder data shows average Pokemon card values rose about 46% per year. — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/pokmon-cards-are-beating-crypto-and-stocks-and-investors-are-paying-attention/)
- Some buyers are "pure investors" who treat sealed booster boxes like stocks. — [PokeInsider](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/19/the-pokemon-card-boom-why-everyones-suddenly-collecting-again/)
- PokeInvesting tells buyers to compare the expected value of the singles inside with the sealed price. — [PokeInvesting](https://pokeinvesting.com/sealed/evolving-skies-booster-box)
- Evolving Skies booster box: MSRP about $143.64, 2026 price $1,800 to $2,700. — [The Card Shop Finder](https://thecardshopfinder.com/shop/pokemon/sealed/evolving-skies/)
- Hidden Fates and Celebrations ETBs stayed near flat for about 3 years after their reprints, then climbed to $564.69 and $359.35. TCGinvest says that none of the past reprint cases stayed down. TCGinvest also warns that part of the 2025 rise came from a general sealed market rise. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- During the 2020-2024 boom, grading backlogs reached 18+ months, and modern cards sold 300% to 500% above MSRP. — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- The first large correction started in October-November 2025 and continued into 2026. — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- Vintage WOTC cards rose 30% to 50% in 2026, and a 1st Edition Charizard PSA 10 neared $1 million (November 2026 teaser). — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)

### Inferences
- Game model: out-of-print sealed product gains value at a slow rate each year, but only after the print run ends. A reprint resets the clock.
- An investor NPC holds for years and sells into spikes. A flipper NPC sells within weeks.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable source on storage practice (climate, acrylic cases, insurance) or storage cost.
- I found no reliable r/PokeInvesting survey data. Reddit pages were not fetched.

## 5. How collectors, parents, and players view flippers; regulation

### Takeaway
Most collectors and parents dislike flippers. Fans celebrated flipper losses in 2026. The US has no law against card scalping. Japan has political and platform action.

### Cited Findings
- Fans mocked the 30th Celebration flippers as "parasites" and "scalpers". Many fans only wanted to open the set at near-retail prices. — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- A Nintendo Life opinion piece says that Pokemon TCG "is no longer a hobby", because people without distributor access cannot get cards at retail. — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/features/opinion-by-definition-pokemon-tcg-is-no-longer-a-hobby)
- In May 2021, Target stopped in-store sales of Pokemon and sports cards "out of an abundance of caution". A man pulled a gun in a Wisconsin Target parking lot after four men attacked him to steal cards. Target first set a limit of one pack per day, then restocked only on Fridays. Target restored sales from about June 1, 2021. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/target-is-no-longer-selling-pokemon-cards-amid-scalper-crisis-1572507/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/target-stops-selling-pokemon-cards-stores-violent-event/); [Game Rant reversal](https://gamerant.com/target-pokemon-cards-in-store-sell/)
- In June 2026, some Target stores set a limit of one Pokemon TCG item per person. — [My Nintendo News](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/06/08/some-target-stores-start-to-limit-pokemon-merch-and-tcg-sales-to-stop-scalpers/)
- Target employees cut open sealed products in some stores to stop resellers. Target then told stores to stop because of legal risk. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/target-orders-stores-to-stop-cutting-open-pokemon-cards-over-legal-risk-3397840/)
- ICv2 links to reports of armed robberies tied to Pokemon product in Chicago and parking-lot fights over product. — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Scalpers emptied the McDonald's Pokemon card promotion and the World Championships merchandise store. — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Scalpers-invade-Pokemon-World-Championships-merch-store-after-ruining-McDonald-s-card-promotion.1088082.0.html)
- A card show banned scalpers. — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/collectibles/news/pokemon-card-show-strikes-back-at-scalpers-with-long-awaited-ban/)
- US: I found no enacted law. A change.org petition asks for a "Trading Card Fair Access and Anti-Scalping Act of 2026" that would penalize buying new sealed product to resell it. I found no proof that Congress received a bill. Other petitions ask for purchase limits, bot bans, resale waiting periods, and a scalping ban in British Columbia. — [change.org draft act](https://www.change.org/p/enact-the-trading-card-fair-access-and-anti-scalping-act-of-2026); [change.org BC](https://www.change.org/p/make-pokemon-scalping-illegal-in-bc); [change.org bots](https://www.change.org/p/outlaw-automated-checkout-bots-for-e-commerce-websites)
- Japan (2026): about 20 ruling-party lawmakers formed a group on the trading card market, which they value at US$2.1 billion. The group wants rules on fake cards and bulk purchases for resale. The government started a special team on scalping and fakes. — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/east-asia/article/3362620/japans-ruling-party-mps-form-group-protect-us21-billion-trading-card-market); [SoraNews24](https://soranews24.com/2026/07/22/japanese-government-sets-up-new-group-to-deal-with-trading-card-boom/amp/); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/japan-launches-taskforce-to-crack-down-on-trading-card-scalpers-and-fakes-3390001/)
- Journalism commentary in August 2026 says that scalping now affects hobbies, not only tickets. — [Business Journalism](https://businessjournalism.org/2026/08/scalping/)

### Inferences
- Game model: a player flipper can lose reputation with collectors, parents, and stores. Store events can include purchase limits, a "Friday only" restock, and a sales stop after violence.

### Gaps
- I found no survey data on parent or kid opinions of flippers. Kid-buyer backlash is clear in commentary, but not in numbers.

## 6. Adult demand, Chinese/Asian demand, and big-money buyers

### Takeaway
The adult collector boom started in 2020 with lockdowns, stimulus money, and influencers. It continued into 2025. Sources on Chinese demand and on funds that buy sealed product are weak.

### Cited Findings
- The 2020 boom came from pandemic lockdowns, stimulus checks, and influencers. Logan Paul's $5.25 million purchase of a PSA 10 Base Set Charizard drew global attention. — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- Spending on non-sports trading cards rose 350% from 2020 to 2025 (Circana data). — [Culture.org](https://culture.org/gaming/pokemon-cards-speculative-boom-investment/)
- The Pokemon card market had $1.8 billion in sales in 2024 (blog claim). — [PokeInsider](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/19/the-pokemon-card-boom-why-everyones-suddenly-collecting-again/)
- Millennials drive much of the investment demand. — [Konvi](https://konvi.app/magazine/article/how-millennials-are-driving-the-pokemon-card-investment-boom/)
- Livestream sellers still create strong demand for sealed product (2026). — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-melt-down-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans)
- Japanese PSA 10 cards carry a 20% to 40% premium over English cards. Raw English cards cost 2x to 6x more than Japanese cards (August 2026 teaser). — [PokemonPriceTracker crash article](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/pokemon-card-market-crash-2026-what-really-happened)
- Thieves tried to take Pokemon product through a Chinese airport. — [Bleeding Cool](https://bleedingcool.com/games/chinese-airport-stops-ambitious-pokemon-tcg-bandits)

### Inferences
- Game model: an "influencer" event can create a demand spike. A "livestream breaker" NPC can be a large buyer of sealed product.

### Gaps
- I found no WSJ, Bloomberg, or CNBC article in my searches. I could not confirm fund or institutional buying of sealed Pokemon product.
- I found no reliable source that shows Chinese buyers move US sealed prices.

## 7. TPCi actions against scalping and their effect

### Takeaway
TPCi's main tool is reprints. Reprints cut prices 25% to 50% for a time, but prices often climb again. Pokemon Center uses queues, purchase limits, invites, and order cancellations. These steps also hit normal fans and do not stop bots.

### Cited Findings
- January 2025: TPC said that it was "actively working to print more" Prismatic Evolutions product, and that more products would come in the next months. TPC did not say how the reprints would be divided. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/the-pokemon-company-addresses-prismatic-evolutions-and-tcg-shortages-as-scalpers-feast-we-are-actively-working-to-print-more/); [Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/news/2025/01/15/the-pokemon-company-promises-theyre-printing-more-prismatic-evolutions/); [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/reprint-promised-prismatic-evolutions)
- TPC said that it was printing at "maximum capacity". — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-scarlet-violet-sv-prismatic-evolutions-maximum-capacity-statement/)
- Measured effect: the Prismatic Evolutions ETB fell 29% during the 2025 restock window ($135.79 to $95.90), then rose to $212.83 by March 2026. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- TPC printed 10.2 billion cards in the 12 months before March 2025, but shelves still emptied in minutes at release. By late 2025, more production and anti-bot steps pushed some sealed products back toward retail price. — [Culture.org](https://culture.org/gaming/pokemon-cards-speculative-boom-investment/)
- Pokemon Center added purchase limits and a virtual queue in 2025. Most new expansions still fell to scalpers. Critics say that the queue cannot tell bots from fans. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-center-tcg-scalpers-journey-together-1851770342); [Proof](https://www.proof.com/blog/why-you-cant-get-pokemon-center-drops)
- April 2025: Pokemon Center announced an invite-based Early Access program. Fans expected bots to make many email accounts to get invites. — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-has-a-new-plan-to-crack-down-on-scalpers-but-fans-are-skeptical/)
- Pokemon Center cancels reseller orders in batches. Normal fans also lost orders. In 2026, the store cancelled many 30th Celebration preorders. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-tcg-fans-lose-their-orders-in-pokemon-center-crackdown-on-scalpers-bots-3406602/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/pokemon-center-cancels-countless-reseller-orders-but-tcg-fans-seem-to-have-been-burned-as-well/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-orders-cancelled-bots-scalpers-center-company-2000732116)
- Nintendo's president said in July 2025 that TPC "will continue to take measures to respond to this issue". — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-scalpers-mega-evolution-tiktok-2000638182)
- October 13, 2026 (Japan only): TPC and LINE Yahoo banned listings of cartons, empty boxes, and empty packs on Yahoo! Auctions and Yahoo! Flea Market. Large lots of the same new product are banned shortly after release, starting with 30th Celebration. Mercari Japan put temporary limits on sealed 30th Celebration product in September 2026. No similar steps exist outside Japan, for example on eBay. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-cracks-down-on-tcg-scalpers-with-new-resale-bans-3418473/)
- TPC restricted some high-price 30th Anniversary boxes to Japanese residents. TPC said that it will use facial recognition in Japanese TCG shops. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-orders-cancelled-bots-scalpers-center-company-2000732116); [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/the-pokemon-company-will-use-facial-recognition-in-pokemon-tcg-shops-to-deter-scalpers-1753283/)
- 2025-2026 production: over 85 billion cards, about 10 billion more than the year before. — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- The 2026 market fall came partly because many top chase cards were still in print. — [TCGplayer seller blog](https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/price-trends-pokémon-cards-dropping-in-price-08-18-2026)

### Inferences
- The most effective TPCi action in the data is a large print run (30th Celebration, 2026). It hurt flippers more than queues, limits, or cancellations.
- Game model: a "TPCi reprint" event cuts the price of one product by 25% to 50% over 1 to 3 months. A "large first wave" event stops the release spike.

### Gaps
- I found no TPCi data on how many orders it cancelled, except the 42,000-item Phantasmal Flames case.
- I found no US measure that matches the Japan marketplace rules.

## 8. Fraud linked to flipping

### Takeaway
The main sealed-product frauds are resealed boxes with swapped contents, pack weighing, and fake cards. I found no confirmed case of retail return fraud with weighed packs. Store rules block most returns of opened cards.

### Cited Findings
- Scammers remove the cards from a box, put in items of similar weight, and shrink-wrap the box again. One buyer paid about US$3.5 million in 2022 for a "sealed" case of 1st Edition Base Set boxes that held re-shrinked boxes. Logan Paul's opened boxes held 1991 G.I. Joe packs. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/logan-paul-fake-pokemon-cards-gi-joe/); [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/?p=53629)
- An X-ray scan shows if a box has the correct number of packs or if somebody tampered with it. — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/?p=53629)
- A Pokemon card shop stopped online sales after thieves resealed mail orders. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-card-shop-stops-online-sales-after-thieves-reseal-mail-orders-3406208/)
- GameStop bans customers who weigh packs (November 2025). — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/gamestop-bans-pokémon-scalpers-using-scales-to-detect-rare-packs)
- PSA found more than $200 million of fake and altered trading cards in 2025, with Pokemon at the front. Six of the top 10 most-faked characters in 2025 were Pokemon. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/fake-pok-mon-cards-flooding-120252009.html); [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/collectibles/news/shocking-new-pokemon-fraud-report-reveals-just-how-big-the-problem-is-how-do-you-spot-them/)
- Buyers said that Walmart sold fake or repacked Pokemon product. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/fake-pokemon-tcg-cards-sold-walmart-claims/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/walmart-fake-pokemon-tcg-products-deep-pocket-monster-accusation/)
- Target lists Pokemon booster packs as items that "cannot be returned". — [Target](https://www.target.com/p/pokemon-scarlet-violet-151-booster-pack/-/A-1001304528)
- Amazon sellers report return scams where buyers send back a different item. — [Amazon Seller Central forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/4e4b0ccc-f51f-4f3f-9f9a-f4c13f78d63c)
- Card-related crime and scams grew in 2026. — [AZFamily](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/03/23/wild-card-when-cardboard-gold-meets-real-world-crime/); [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-gb/blog/hotforsecurity/pokemon-card-scams-2026); [KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51477273/pokmon-cards-are-igniting-an-international-crime-spree)

### Inferences
- Game model: a "resealed box" risk applies when the player buys sealed product from a stranger. The higher the price, the higher the risk. An X-ray or a trusted source can remove the risk.
- A weighed-pack "searched" product gives lower value than its expected value. A store can ban a pack weigher.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed case of retail return fraud with opened Pokemon ETBs at Walmart or Target.
- I found no data on fake (counterfeit) sealed ETBs in the US market.
