# US Retail Allocations, Restocks, and Purchase Limits for Pokemon TCG Sealed Product (2023 to November 2026)

Notes date: 2026-11-20. Scope: the US market. Source quality: many restock facts come from tracker blogs (Ripster, DoubleHolo, PokeInsider). These blogs often copy each other and sometimes conflict. This file marks blog-only claims as "blog claim" and social-media claims as "anecdotal".

## 1. How each chain receives product, who stocks it, and when restocks happen

### Takeaway
At Walmart and Target, store staff do not stock the card aisle. A third-party merchandising vendor owns the stock and stocks it on regional routes, about one or two visits each week. Store restock days are stable for each store but are not the same across the chain. The vendor varies its times to stop crowds. Online drops follow clearer patterns: Walmart.com on Wednesday at about 9 PM ET, and Target.com overnight at about 3 to 6 AM ET, often on Friday.

### Cited Findings
- **Walmart vendor:** A third-party merchandising vendor, MJ Holdings, stocks Walmart's trading-card section. Its reps drive between stores with sealed boxes (blog claim) — [Ripster: Walmart restock guide](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- A Walmart employee said that Walmart does not distribute Pokemon cards. "A special vendor" comes in and stocks the shelves. Other reports say independent merchandisers enter stores for the distributors (anecdotal, reported in coverage) — [Search summary of CBS / Vice / Game Rant coverage](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/target-pokemon-cards)
- **Target vendor:** Collectors most often name MJ Holding for Target too — [Ripster: Target restock guide](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards). One other guide names Excell Marketing as Target's vendor, with Friday mornings as the usual day. Only one source makes this claim — [DoubleHolo: Walmart & Target restock guide](https://doubleholo.com/articles/when-walmart-target-restock-pokemon-cards). The two sources conflict.
- **Vendor-owned inventory (confirmed by an internal notice):** An internal Target "Trading Cards Update" (posted on X on 2026-08-12, valid 2026-08-07 to 2026-08-23) says trading card inventory is "vendor owned, not Target owned". It tells staff not to break seals or remove wrapping "under any circumstances", to "reduce legal risk" — [Dexerto, 2026-08-12](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/target-orders-stores-to-stop-cutting-open-pokemon-cards-over-legal-risk-3397840/)
- At Target, store staff may know the truck day but not whether cards are on it, because the vendor handles card stock (blog claim) — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart in-store timing (blog claims, conflicting):**
  - Common days are Tuesday to Thursday. Thursday is the day people cite most. Time is about 7 to 10 AM. The vendor visits about one or two times each week — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
  - The MJ Holdings contract window reportedly runs from Thursday midnight to the end of Saturday — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
  - The vendor reportedly told its reps to change the stocking day and time to make restocks less predictable. This followed crowds that formed before setup. These crowds possibly broke the vendor's contract terms with the store — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart visit size:** A typical vendor visit stocks about 300 packs. The stock often sells out within one hour (blog claim, no primary source) — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart shelf location:** Cards can be near checkout, in toys or electronics, at customer service, or in a locked case — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Target in-store timing:** Target has no national restock day. Each store has a stable day, but stores differ. Trackers name weekday mornings (Tuesday to Friday) shortly after opening. Monday and weekends are rarely named. Signs of a restock are empty pegs with fresh price tags or an open vendor cart in the aisle. Some stores keep cards in a locked case or at guest services — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart.com online timing:** The most consistent drop is Wednesday at about 9 PM ET / 6 PM PT. Restockd logged 74 Walmart Pokemon detections in 90 days (to 2026-11-02). Wednesday was the busiest day, with a peak from 9 PM to midnight ET. One tracker logged a main drop at about 9 PM ET on eight Wednesdays in a row (2026-07-01 to 2026-08-19). Hot sets sell out in less than 60 seconds — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Target.com online timing:** Swooper logged 408 restocks in 116 days (to 2026-11-02). 46% were on Friday. 87% were between 10 PM and 6 AM ET. The peak hour was 4 AM ET. Restockd puts the peak window at 3 to 6 AM ET, and major launches at about 12 AM PT / 3 AM ET — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Hobby shops versus big-box (Prismatic Evolutions, January 2025):** PokeBeach reported that Target, Walmart, and Best Buy expected their "typical allotment", while local hobby stores got severe cuts — [PokeBeach, 2025-01](https://www.pokebeach.com/2025/01/prismatic-evolutions-allocations-severely-cut-to-local-stores-demand-higher-than-supply)
- **GameStop:** GameStop stopped all Pokemon TCG preorders, online and in store, in February 2025 — [Nintendo Life, 2025-02](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2025/02/gamestop-discontinues-pokemon-trading-card-game-pre-orders)
- **Best Buy release events:** Before the store opens, employees give tickets to customers from the front of the line, one ticket stack for each product. Best Buy says it cannot guarantee a ticket for everyone in line. A Colorado Springs Best Buy had customers camping from about 9 PM the night before (30th anniversary product, 2026) — [Search summary citing Best Buy event terms and Brobible](https://brobible.com/culture/article/pokemons-30th-anniversary-crowd-control-security-precautions/)
- **Walmart Collectibles Draw (from August 2026):** This is an online lottery. Entry is free, needs a Walmart account, and allows one entry per drawing. Drawings mostly open at about 5 PM ET. October 2026 drawing dates were 7, 8, 14, 21, 26, and 28. Walmart charges winners automatically. Walmart can remove entries for suspected fraud or abuse. The 2026-10-07 drawing had the 30th Celebration ETB and the Prismatic Evolutions ETB at $69 each. The 2026-10-14 drawing limited the Prismatic ETB to 3 — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Target raffle (rumor):** Code references to a Target raffle appeared in late September 2026. A monitor flagged the 30th Celebration ETB as a raffle product on 2026-10-12. As of mid-October 2026, Target had not announced a drawing — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)

### Inferences
- A good game model: each big-box store has one hidden "vendor day" each week, fixed per store, with a random time in the morning. A vendor visit adds a batch of about 300 packs-equivalent. Crowds that camp the vendor can make the vendor shift the day (a feedback loop).
- The vendor owns the stock, so store staff have limited power over it. This explains the conflict between staff anti-scalper actions and corporate policy.
- Online drops are more predictable than in-store drops, but they sell out in seconds. A game can model online drops as a weekly timed event with a speed or luck check.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable source for Costco, Sam's Club, Barnes & Noble, Walgreens, CVS, or Dollar General receiving or stocking methods (vendor or store staff). I found no restock days for these chains.
- I found no Pokemon Center receiving data (it is TPCi's own direct store, so no vendor is involved).
- I found no primary source (vendor or retailer document) for the MJ Holdings contract window or the 300-pack visit size. These come from blogs only.
- The Target vendor name (MJ Holding versus Excell) is unresolved.

## 2. Purchase limits, Target pulls, and 2025-2026 policies by chain

### Takeaway
Limits are now standard. Walmart uses 5 trading-card items per visit (from 2025-11-05). Target uses 2 per SKU per guest at enterprise level, often 1 on hot items. GameStop uses 1 or 2 per account or customer. Best Buy uses 1 to 5. Pokemon Center uses 1 on premium items. Local managers often set lower limits, and enforcement varies by store. Target stopped in-store card sales in May 2021 after violence. I found no confirmed 2025-2026 chain-wide Target pull.

### Cited Findings
- **Target 2021 pull:** Target stopped in-store sales of Pokemon and sports cards in May 2021, after safety incidents, including a parking-lot gun incident in Wisconsin. Online sales continued — [NBC News, 2021](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/target-halts-sale-trading-cards-including-pok-mon-over-safety-n1267442); [KOMO News, 2021](https://komonews.com/news/nation-world/target-suspends-in-store-sales-of-pokemon-cards-after-parking-lot-brawl)
- Before the 2021 pull, Target limited customers to one pack per day — [Inquirer, 2021](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/?p=1432107)
- In 2021, Walmart said it had not stopped selling any trading cards — [Search summary of 2021 coverage](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/target-and-walmart-stores-will-stop-selling-pokemon-cards-for-safety-reasons)
- **Target 2025 claim (unverified):** PokeInsider (2025-11-05) says Target suspended in-store card sales and resumed with 2 items per customer per day. It says managers can reduce this to 1 per day. The article cites no sources, and I found no news report of a 2025 Target suspension. It is possibly a mix-up with 2021 — [PokeInsider, 2025-11-05](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/05/major-retailers-tighten-purchase-limits-on-pokemon-tcg-products-to-combat-scalping/)
- **Target 2026:**
  - Target limits Pokemon trading cards to 2 units per SKU, and prints the limit at the register (reported at the Lisbon, CT store, August 2026) — [Notebookcheck, 2026-09](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Pokemon-30th-Anniversary-restock-marred-by-Target-altercation-that-injures-4-year-old.1406433.0.html)
  - Target sets purchase limits for high-demand cards at enterprise level, and they apply to every store — [Dexerto, 2026-08-12](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/target-orders-stores-to-stop-cutting-open-pokemon-cards-over-legal-risk-3397840/)
  - For the 30th Anniversary release (September 2026): 2 per SKU per customer, 1 Pokemon TCG purchase per credit or debit card, and a "personal use only" rule. Target can cancel orders that break the resale rule — [The Bulletin Time](https://www.thebulletintime.com/news/target-pokemon-30th-anniversary-tcg-release-limits)
  - The Pokemon x Target collaboration (launched 2026-06-06) is "one unit per item, per guest", in stores and online — [ScreenRant, 2026-06](https://screenrant.com/target-pokemon-card-limits-policy-changes/)
  - Target.com: almost all tracked restocks have a per-order limit, usually 2. The Delta Reign ETB ($59.99) had a limit of 1 (checked 2026-10-16). Polygon reported 2 units per transaction and up to 2 transactions per product — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
  - In-store: no official chain-wide pack limit. Some stores limit packs to 1 or 2 per person (blog claim) — [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart:** An internal memo (reported as from the OneWalmart employee portal, not officially confirmed) sets "a maximum of five packs and/or boxes of Trading Cards per visit", from 2025-11-05. It covers departments 7 and 82 (Pokemon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, and sports cards). The stated reasons are to reduce conflicts and crowding, and resale and stock shortages. A viral TikTok of a buyer who took a full display at a Las Vegas Walmart came just before the memo — [Daily Dot, 2025-11](https://www.dailydot.com/news/walmart-trading-cards-purchase-limit-pokemon/); [Kotaku, 2025-11](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-trading-card-tcg-scalper-walmart-purchase-limit-2000641073); [Wargamer, 2025-11](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/walmart-sets-buy-limits-viral-tiktok)
  - Coverage says that it is not clear what counts as a "box", or whether repeat visits get around the rule — [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/news/walmart-trading-cards-purchase-limit-pokemon/)
  - The limit applies in store and online. Single products and drawings can set lower limits — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **GameStop:**
  - The earlier policy limited all Pokemon TCG products to 2 per customer, except single booster packs. For Destined Rivals (released 2025-05-30), new products were 1 per customer per SKU. Single sleeved boosters stayed at 5 per customer — [esports.gg, 2025-05](https://esports.gg/news/pokemon/gamestop-restricts-pokemon-tcg-destined-rivals-purchases-ahead-of-may-30-release); [Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/gamestop-pokemon-card-buy-limits-destined-rivals-release)
  - The GameStop Pokemon Day 2026 Collection page shows a limit of 1 per account — [GameStop product page](https://www.gamestop.com/toys-games/trading-cards/products/pokemon-trading-card-game-pokemon-day-2026-collection/436435.html)
  - Official policy is 2 per account. Some managers set 1 per person on hot releases (blog claim) — [PokeInsider, 2025-11-05](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/05/major-retailers-tighten-purchase-limits-on-pokemon-tcg-products-to-combat-scalping/)
  - Engadget reported that GameStop marked up some Pokemon cards by more than 300 percent — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2212688/gamestop)
- **Best Buy:** Limits range from 1 to 5 per customer, by product type and channel. Premium collections get stricter limits than single boosters and tins (blog claim) — [PokeInsider](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/05/major-retailers-tighten-purchase-limits-on-pokemon-tcg-products-to-combat-scalping/). A 30th-anniversary product was 1 per order. Event terms list 1 per person — [Search summary citing Best Buy event terms / Brobible](https://brobible.com/culture/article/pokemons-30th-anniversary-crowd-control-security-precautions/)
- **Barnes & Noble:** No central policy. Local managers set limits. Many stores use 1 item per customer (blog claim) — [PokeInsider](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/05/major-retailers-tighten-purchase-limits-on-pokemon-tcg-products-to-combat-scalping/)
- **Pokemon Center:** Limits vary by product. Premium items, for example the Mega Charizard X ex Ultra Premium Collection, are usually 1 per customer. Sleeved boosters can allow up to 6. The store enforces limits per order, account, credit card, person, and household (blog claim) — [PokeInsider](https://pokeinsider.com/2025/11/05/major-retailers-tighten-purchase-limits-on-pokemon-tcg-products-to-combat-scalping/)
- **Costco:** A newsletter reported the $121.99 online Prismatic Evolutions bundle as 1 per membership per day (single, less established source). Shoppers reported that some Costco stores used no limit on the 151 bundle, and stock sold out within one hour — [Search summary: pkmnnewsletter / Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/costco-pokemon-cards-purchase-limits/)
- **Sam's Club:** A May 2026 customer review says the limit on the Prismatic ETB + Poke Ball bundle "should have been one box" (anecdotal) — [Sam's Club reviews](https://www.samsclub.com/reviews/product/19170800669)

### Inferences
- A clear game design ladder: (a) no limit, (b) a per-visit limit (Walmart 5), (c) a per-SKU limit (Target 2), (d) a 1-per-account or 1-per-card limit on hot items, (e) a lottery or ticket system (Walmart Draw, Best Buy tickets). Stores move up the ladder after viral incidents.
- Per-visit limits are weak because a flipper can make repeat visits. Per-payment-card and per-account limits are stronger.
- Enforcement varies by store, so a game can give each store a "strictness" stat.

### Gaps
- I found no limit data for Walgreens, CVS, or Dollar General.
- I found no official Target or Walmart public policy page. The Walmart limit is a memo that outlets reported; Walmart did not confirm it publicly in the sources I found.

## 3. Allocation sizes for big releases

### Takeaway
Hard per-store numbers are rare. The best data: in January 2025, hobby stores got about 10-15% of their Prismatic Evolutions orders, while big-box chains got their typical allotment. Industry estimates for 2026 sets are about 30-50% of requested volume. The only per-visit figure is a blog claim of about 300 packs per Walmart vendor visit.

### Cited Findings
- **Prismatic Evolutions (January 2025):** PokeBeach polling found that an average US store got less than 10% to 15% of what it asked distributors for. Example: a store that asks for 100 ETBs gets approval for 15. Target, Walmart, and Best Buy expected their typical allotment — [PokeBeach, 2025-01](https://www.pokebeach.com/2025/01/prismatic-evolutions-allocations-severely-cut-to-local-stores-demand-higher-than-supply)
- A store owner blamed new stores: "stores who do not normally order Pokemon are requesting to buy this set from distributors" — [PokeBeach, 2025-01](https://www.pokebeach.com/2025/01/prismatic-evolutions-allocations-severely-cut-to-local-stores-demand-higher-than-supply)
- **2026 fill rates (unofficial):** Industry observers (via Sportskeeda, cited by Samurai Sword Tokyo) estimate 30-50% of requested volume for recent sets, up from about 10-15% for Prismatic Evolutions. These are not TPCi figures — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/why-japanese-pokemon-boxes-sell-out-scalpers-allocation)
- **Distributor rationing:** On hot launches, distributors get a fixed allotment from TPCi and divide it among their accounts. Established, high-volume accounts with a steady purchase history get priority. Mega Evolution is named as one such launch (vendor guide, not official) — [Digital Media Vending](https://www.digitalmediavending.com/pokemon-card-wholesale-sourcing-distributor-guide)
- **Case sizes:** A Mega Evolution Enhanced booster display case holds 6 booster boxes. A 3-pack blister case holds 12 (wholesale listings) — [Black Eagle Wholesale](https://blackeaglewholesale.com/product/pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-enhanced-booster-display-box-case-6-boxes-wholesale/); [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/397309819967)
- **Walmart vendor visit:** about 300 packs per visit, sold out often within one hour (blog claim) — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Late-2026 schedule context:** Mega Evolution: Delta Reign released 2026-11-06, with 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections (Day and Night, $179.99 MSRP), the Ditto Premium Collection, and the Mew or Mewtwo Figure Collection. The 30th Celebration Binder Collection releases 2026-12-04. In mid-October 2026, the only Delta Reign ETBs on Walmart.com were marketplace listings at $129.99 to $139.99 (ETB MSRP $59.99 at Target) — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards); [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)

### Inferences
- For a game, model the hobby-store (player) allocation as a fill rate on each order: about 10-15% on a "hype" set, about 30-50% on a normal hot set, and higher on a quiet set. Give a bonus to stores with a long order history.
- Big-box chains get a steadier allotment than hobby stores on hype sets, so big-box retail is the flipper's main source.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable per-store case or pallet counts for Target, Walmart, Costco, or Best Buy for any set: Surging Sparks, Journey Together, Destined Rivals, Black Bolt / White Flare, or the Mega Evolution sets.
- I found no set-specific allocation data for Surging Sparks, Journey Together, Black Bolt / White Flare, or Phantasmal Flames.

## 4. Online drops: queues, bots, cart limits, and cancellations

### Takeaway
Pokemon Center uses a virtual waiting room with no visible position or wait time. Bots still get many queue entries. Pokemon Center cancels bulk orders in waves, and real fans lose orders too. Walmart moved hot items to an online lottery in August 2026. Target uses per-order and per-card limits with resale cancellation rules.

### Cited Findings
- **Pokemon Center queue (from February 2025):** The site added a virtual queue against bots and reseller raids. Visitors wait on a static page that they must keep open without refresh. The page redirects them when their turn comes — [SNKRDUNK, 2025-02-06](https://snkrdunk.com/en/magazine/2025/02/06/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-center-international-website-implements-queue-system)
- The queue does not show how many people are in it or how long the wait is. One account says one person can run hundreds of queue entries. Sources claim bots use proxy IPs (anecdotal and forum claims) — [Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-pokemon-center-queue); [Proof blog](https://www.proof.com/blog/why-you-cant-get-pokemon-center-drops)
- Too many page refreshes in the queue can flag a fan as a bot and remove them from the session (anecdotal) — [Search summary, Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-trading-card-game-cracking-down-bots-tcg-scalpers)
- **30th Celebration cancellations (September 2026):** Pokemon Center cancelled a large number of 30th Celebration preorders overnight around 2026-09-07 (the set released 2026-09-16). Reports say "thousands", but no exact count exists. Users waited hours in the queue. One user was flagged as a bot after queuing on a phone. Some real customers lost single orders. TPCi did not explain the cancellations. Pokemon Center ETBs (with the Pokemon Center promo) were already on eBay at about 4x MSRP — [Kotaku, 2026-09-08](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-orders-cancelled-bots-scalpers-center-company-2000732116); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-tcg-fans-lose-their-orders-in-pokemon-center-crackdown-on-scalpers-bots-3406602/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/pokemon-center-cancels-countless-reseller-orders-but-tcg-fans-seem-to-have-been-burned-as-well/)
- In July 2026, Nintendo President Shuntaro Furukawa told shareholders that The Pokemon Company would continue to take measures against the problem — [Kotaku, 2026-09-08](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-orders-cancelled-bots-scalpers-center-company-2000732116)
- **Early Access program:** Pokemon Center later tied early-access invitations to newsletter sign-up. Fans expect bots to make many email accounts to get around it — [Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-has-a-new-plan-to-crack-down-on-scalpers-but-fans-are-skeptical/)
- **Target online:** Per-order limits (usually 2, sometimes 1), 1 Pokemon purchase per payment card, and resale-rule cancellations (30th Anniversary, 2026) — [The Bulletin Time](https://www.thebulletintime.com/news/target-pokemon-30th-anniversary-tcg-release-limits); [Ripster: Target](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-target-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **Walmart online:** Walmart Collectibles Draw (lottery) from August 2026, one entry per account per drawing, fraud removal. Walmart.com also has third-party marketplace sellers at high markups — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- A Slickdeals thread says GT Collectibles, a Walmart marketplace seller, has a link to MJ Holdings (anecdotal, single forum source) — [Search summary of Slickdeals threads](https://slickdeals.net/f/19043506-pokemon-tcg-destined-rivals-booster-bundle-39-99)

### Inferences
- A game can model online drops as: (a) a queue with random position, (b) a chance of a bot-flag false positive, and (c) a later cancellation wave that hits bulk buyers hardest but also hits some honest buyers.
- Lotteries remove the speed advantage. They are a strong late-stage anti-flipper mechanic.

### Gaps
- I found no official Pokemon Center FAQ on queue order rules (first-come or random) or on cancellation criteria.
- I found no data on how many Target or Walmart online orders were cancelled for resale.

## 5. Store-employee behavior and incidents at restocks

### Takeaway
Viral incidents (fights, arrests, display clear-outs) drive policy changes. Some Target staff cut shrink wrap to stop resale until Target banned it in August 2026. Claims about staff holding stock for friends or selling to flippers are anecdotal. I found no confirmed firing.

### Cited Findings
- **Shrink-wrap cutting:** In June 2026, a Target employee was filmed slicing the shrink wrap on Prismatic Evolutions ETBs. The worker said corporate ordered it, to deal with scalpers. In August 2026, staff at another Target removed ETB wrapping fully. Target's August 2026 notice then banned this. Opened seals lower resale value — [Dexerto, 2026-08-12](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/target-orders-stores-to-stop-cutting-open-pokemon-cards-over-legal-risk-3397840/)
- **Employee purchases (May 2026, anecdotal):** A social-media user asked Target to fire two employees. One employee bought high-volume Pokemon product on shift. The other employee sold it to that person — [Bent Corner](https://bentcorner.com/pokemon-idiot-tries-to-get-two-target-associates-fired/)
- **Walmart display clear-out (November 2025):** A viral TikTok showed a buyer taking a full display at a Las Vegas Walmart. Claims that store management helped the reseller are conjecture — [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/news/walmart-trading-cards-purchase-limit-pokemon/)
- **Lisbon, Connecticut Target (2026-08-28):** During the 30th anniversary rollout, two customers allegedly took all the remaining cards and hid them in a cart in the men's wear aisle, despite the 2-per-SKU limit. At the register, a man allegedly punched another customer. That customer fell into a 4-year-old girl, who hurt her elbow. A fan page says police arrested the man and charged both men with trespassing. Police records are not public. Target made no statement (anecdotal, one video and one eyewitness) — [Notebookcheck, 2026-09](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Pokemon-30th-Anniversary-restock-marred-by-Target-altercation-that-injures-4-year-old.1406433.0.html)
- **Target restock arrest (2026):** Dexerto reports that police arrested an alleged scalper after a Target restock confrontation turned violent (reported, not fully confirmed) — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-scalper-reportedly-arrested-after-assaulting-man-during-trading-card-restock-3410523/)
- **Costco (April 2025):** Instagram video shows a crowd of 30 to 40 people outside a Costco for Prismatic Evolutions. Police on scene chased a car — [Kotaku, 2025-04](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-restock-prismatic-evolution-costco-fight-2000687843)
- **Vending machine fight (March 2025):** Two men fought at a Bay Area card vending machine after a 10 AM restock. ### Inferences
- Game events can include: "vendor-camp crowd", "display clear-out goes viral" (raises chain limits), "fight at restock" (store pulls stock or adds security), and "staff sells out the back" (hidden stock leak).

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed case of a store banning a named reseller, or of an employee fired for holding stock.
- Most incident details rest on viral video, not police records.

## 7. TPCi response: reprints, "maximum capacity", and allocation changes

### Takeaway
TPCi said in January 2025 that it prints at "maximum capacity" and will reprint. It repeated this at least three times in early 2025. Reprints take about 6-9 months to reach shelves. By 2026, TPCi added queues, cancellations, and early access on Pokemon Center. Fill rates to hobby stores improved to an estimated 30-50%. TPCi publishes no print-run numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **January 2025 statement:** At the Prismatic Evolutions launch, TPCi said it is printing more product at maximum capacity, and that reprints will reach retailers as soon as possible. It also promised Pokemon Center restocks. The statement also covered Surging Sparks — [Kotaku, 2025-01](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolutions-surging-sparks-sold-1851738920); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolution-shortage-statement-comment-response/); [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/pokemon-tcg-prismatic-evolutions-shortage-response/)
- Wargamer called it "a pretty boilerplate statement", almost word for word like past reprint statements — [Wargamer, 2025](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/reprint-promised-prismatic-evolutions)
- **March 2025:** TPCi posted the statement a third time. It said it will continue to reprint affected products for participating retailers. PokeBeach estimated that reprints usually take six to nine months — [PokeBeach, 2025-03](https://www.pokebeach.com/2025/03/for-the-third-time-tpci-reiterates-more-pokemon-tcg-product-on-the-way)
- **Reprints into 2026:** Prismatic Evolutions reprints continued through 2026. TPCi does not publish print runs — [TCGinvest](https://tcginvest.io/blog/pokemon-reprints-2026)
- Walmart's Collectibles Draw still offered the Prismatic Evolutions ETB in October 2026 at $69, which shows stock still flowing about 20 months after launch — [Ripster: Walmart](https://www.ripster.gg/blog/when-does-walmart-restock-pokemon-cards)
- **2026 anti-bot measures:** Furukawa's July 2026 shareholder comment, the September 2026 order cancellations, and the Early Access newsletter program — [Kotaku, 2026-09-08](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-orders-cancelled-bots-scalpers-center-company-2000732116); [Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-has-a-new-plan-to-crack-down-on-scalpers-but-fans-are-skeptical/)
- TPCi said it "will continue to explore measures that help create the best possible experience for Pokemon Center customers" — [Search summary of Pokemon Center coverage](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-trading-card-game-cracking-down-bots-tcg-scalpers)
- **Allocation changes:** Unofficial fill-rate estimates rose from about 10-15% (Prismatic Evolutions, 2025) to about 30-50% (recent 2026 sets) — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/why-japanese-pokemon-boxes-sell-out-scalpers-allocation)

### Inferences
- A game can model TPCi as a slow supply response: a hype set sells out, a "reprint announced" event follows, and extra supply arrives 6-9 months later. That supply lowers sealed prices over time.
- The "maximum capacity" statement is a repeated PR line, not a number. A game can treat it as flavor text.

### Gaps
- I found no official TPCi statement on 2025-2026 allocation policy changes for specific retailers.
- I found no TPCi statement that targets big-box retailer limits directly.
